## Integer checks

* Main engine `z75_push2_prime_ap.c` uses blocked Eratosthenes on `1+step*i`; modes 8, 4, 1 mean R₈, Type A, R respectively. For R or R₈, write `n=kabc-a-b`, k=4 or 8. For two fixed coordinates with product t≤bound it is enough to cross `n=-a-b mod kt` or `n=-a mod kt-1`. Type A is `n=4abc-4a-b`: for fixed `(a,b),(a,c),(b,c)` cross respectively `-4a-b mod 4ab`, `-4a mod 4ac-1`, `-b mod 4bc-4` (skip bc=1). Dividing yields a positive third coordinate since n is large. When the progression step is not a unit, solve the index congruence after dividing by the gcd. Late scan of larger word moduli only discards proven positives. Final exact check of `kabc-l₀a-l₁b-l₂c=n` tries all six coordinate orders: for smallest x and middle y, `kx³-(Σl)x≤n` and `kxy²-lᵢx-(lⱼ+lₕ)y≤n`; positive last denominator follows except in a degenerate A chart giving only negative n. `H` is a stored positive solution, `MISS` an exhausted search.
* The following `primes / late / exact / misses` are **per progression**, not additive. Each progression is 1 modulo the indicated step; `last=` denotes the last candidate in the progression, not necessarily a prime. `r8a` (step 840, 1.000–1.2352 trillion): 44,157,596 / 3,925 / 366 / 0. `r8b` (120, 1.000–1.288 trillion): 324,184,423 / 14,133 / 1,320 / 1. `r8c` (840, 1.2352–3.2512 trillion): 369,705,714 / 32,410 / 2,946 / 1. Exact endpoints and prime counts in the last lines. `A,Ab` are preliminary restricted progressions; `Aall,Rall` are the consecutive hard-class scans. `Rall` had 509 late candidates, all resolved by word scan; `Aall` none.
* Independent exhaustive record checks: `factor.txt` is the round-1 factorization algorithm from `z75_obstruct.c` (no AP sieve), factoring every `p+u` through the smaller-u bound and exhausting divisors. `pycheck.txt` is the unmodified round-1 Python checker (also trial-divides each prime to its square root); `third.txt` runs `z58_disproof_eng.c` with EXACT=1, counting both parities of the ordinary slice. The three independently recorded outputs agree wherever their counts overlap.
* Second census engine `z75_push2_prime_modcheck.c` does **not** use the large AP engine's CRT, factor-pair crossing, or primality sieve. For each `f=3 mod 4` through 8191 it evaluates the word predicate directly on each allowed index in one period `f/gcd(f,24)`. Crossed off = guaranteed hit, no heuristic densities. It additionally drops small composites and tests the rest with deterministic 64-bit Miller–Rabin. Every surviving prime has a larger explicit word in its search. `A2,R2` report max late f and survivor counts, not total prime counts.
* `hard.txt` checks all 4,180 distinct exact-fallback primes, including both zeros, for R, A, U₇, and (S) separately. All have four positive witnesses. Independent `z75_push2_prime_report.py` recovers `u=-p mod f` (Type A can use `f+1` instead of 1), `t=(f+1)/u`, `v=(p+u)/f`, and checks `p=tuv-u-v`. Respectively `4|t`, `4|u`, `f=7 mod 8`, or `min(t,u,v)≥2` and `8|(p+t)tuv`. Final hard column is deliberately 0=not tested, **not** a zero assertion. Ordinary maxima are in `report.txt`.

Match each within the same residue to the nearest available smooth shift-composite, and to the nearest available rough prime of each shift status (ties for rough matches tried above first). The square cohort is exhaustive for positive m divisible by 420 in range, matched for *both* smoothness and 12p+1 status. The three-shift cohort samples 128 per trillion-wide bin by fixed hash and matches rough shift-prime controls in which **both** additional shifts are composite. Distinct within each family. `report.txt` gives maximum pair distance, all four other detector counts and discordant pair counts. These matches do not control higher powers of small primes in p−1 (especially the forced 420² in the square-offset group); do not identify a causal effect. Smoothness mattered descriptively in both groups; 12p+1 prime alone did not enhance the smooth group. The stronger observed difficulty is m²+1, even after matching; no inference about an infinite family. Neither new true zero has 12p+1 prime or p−1 61-smooth (`report.txt` gives full factorizations).

## Replay

From the root: `bash scripts/z75_push2_prime_replay.sh`. This builds serially, reproduces all logs in a temporary subdirectory, checks byte equality and independently verifies identities, under `ulimit -v 6000000` and individual timeouts ≤1800. No network or third-party Python packages. The stored records are from the individual runs; no claim of a second execution of the entire replay script. In particular the proposed consecutive bounds above do not depend on a model of either tail.
