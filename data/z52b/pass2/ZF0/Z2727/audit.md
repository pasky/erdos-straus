# Z2727 — special 0-cycle degrees, not just Diff support

**PROVED dictionary relative to Deuring/Gross–Keating; CERTIFIED finite enumeration. ES is OPEN.**

Pass1's covered citation does not do the requested degree comparison. KUDLA_ES.md's header explicitly says “These deep identities are cited, not used”; §1 computes Diff and §2 computes a support majorant, not arithmetic degrees. Its Type I dictionary is correct: a tuple gives the quaternion order generated by u²=−d,v²=−ab,uv+vu=1, hence an index T(d,ab)=[[d,−1/2],[−1/2,ab]]. At n=p, its quaternion algebra is B_{p,∞}; an embedding into a maximal order gives special endomorphisms of a supersingular elliptic curve. This is a map to possible points over the index, NOT a bijection between certificate tuples and stack points or a degree identity.

I fetched and read Kudla's Bourbaki survey, https://www.math.toronto.edu/~skudla/bourbaki.final.vers.pdf, §5, pp.18–21. Equations (5.2)–(5.5) explicitly compare the geometric degree, Gross–Keating length, and Kitaoka/Whittaker derivative; this supplies the missing formulas. For T locally equivalent to diag(unit,p^b unit), b odd, the a=0 branch of (5.4) and the (a₁,a₂,a₃)=(0,0,b) branch of (5.3) both give
 δ_p(T)=(b+1)/2,
 W′_{T,p}(0)/[log(p)γ_p(1−p^(−2))]=(b+1)/2.
Thus the local analytic derivative factor and the geometric deformation length agree. We use this positive-definite, odd-p local identity on the modular curve, not an unverified global compact-Shimura normalisation.

For a concrete arithmetic degree comparison take d=1, p=3 or 7, the unique supersingular elliptic curve in each characteristic, and the explicit maximal order
 O=Z[(−1+i)/2,j], i²=−p,j²=−1,ij=−ji.
It has reduced discriminant p (hence is maximal), and |O×|=12,4 respectively. In the trace-zero lattice write v=(xi+yj+zij)/2, x even, y≡z mod2; nrd(v)=(px²+y²+pz²)/4. The integer enumeration counts pairs u,v with nrd(u)=1,nrd(v)=m and px_u x_v+y_u y_v+pz_u z_v=−2. It is a tiny three-dimensional ellipsoid enumeration, not a dense Cartesian product over large meshes.

With the stack convention deg Z(T)=Σ length/|Aut(E,u,v)| log p, the orbit-stabilizer formula gives
 deg Z(T)/log p = r_O(T) δ_p(T)/|O×|.
For supported indices b=v_p(4m−1) is odd (4 is a p-adic unit); this is the degree computed. The certificate count, by contrast, enumerates factorisations ab=m with e=(4m−1)/p dividing a+b, c=(a+b)/e.

These already disprove equality with either unordered or ordered certificate counts; no global rescaling can repair positive degree at zero certificate count. Extending to every m≤1000 with p|(4m−1): at p=3, 200 of 334 indices have positive cycle degree, 198 of those have no certificate, total degree/log3=591 versus 2 unordered certificates. At p=7, 92 of 143 indices are supported, 89 have no certificate, total degree/log7=230 versus 3 unordered certificates. This is the actual geometric count/deformation-length step missing from the cited memo, not a repeat of its Hilbert-symbol census.

**Assessment:** the special-cycle correspondence exists but is many-valued and loses the factorisation/ray condition. KRY positivity is not the missing certificate forcing. We did not independently numerically differentiate a Siegel series: the analytic derivative is evaluated by the explicit cited formula, while endomorphism pairs and certificate counts are independently enumerated. Nor did we construct a compact quaternionic Shimura curve at arbitrary discriminant; the small cases are its split/modular-curve analogue with the same good odd-prime local identity, explicitly specified.
