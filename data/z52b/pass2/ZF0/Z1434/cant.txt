Title: Combinatorial and Additive Number Theory (CANT 2026)

URL Source: https://www.theoryofnumbers.com/cant/

Markdown Content:
**Description:** This is the twenty-fourth in a series of annual workshops sponsored by the New York Number Theory Seminar at the CUNY Graduate Center on problems in combinatorial and additive number theory and related parts of mathematics.

This is a hybrid conference: Talks may be presented in person in the Science Center on the 4th floor of the CUNY Graduate Center or on Zoom.   
 The lectures from Monday, July 13, to Thursday, July 16, will be in person at the CUNY Graduate Center and broadcast on Zoom.   
 The lectures on Friday, July 17, and Saturday, July 18, will only be on Zoom.   
 There will be a problem session every afternoon at the CUNY Graduate Center.   
 Everyone is invited and encouraged to attend in person both the morning and afternoon sessions.   
 There will be daily morning coffee and afternoon wine and cheese.

All conference lectures, both those presented in person and those delivered remotely, will be broadcast live on Zoom and uploaded to YouTube.   
 To join the conference on Zoom, you should download the free software Zoom 5.0 from www.zoom.us.

The conference program, list of speakers, and abstracts will be posted on this website and on [researchseminars.org](http://www.researchseminars.org/).

Mathematicians who would like to speak at the meeting, either in person or remotely, should submit a title and abstract (both pdf and LaTex file) to: [melvyn.nathanson@lehman.cuny.edu](mailto:melvyn.nathanson@lehman.cuny.edu).
