May 14- 17, 2025

University of Georgia

Athens, Georgia

The Editors of Integers and the Department of Mathematics at the University of Georgia are pleased to announce the Integers Conference 2025. The Integers conferences are international conferences held for the purpose of bringing together mathematicians, students, and others interested in number theory and combinatorics.

Contributed Talks:

We welcome contributed talks in number theory, combinatorics, and combinatorial game theory. All contributed talks will be scheduled for 20-minute time slots. Note: as there is a ceiling on the number of talks we are able to accommodate, we cannot guarantee that all requests to present a talk will be honored. Those submitting abstracts will be notified regarding approval of their talk within one week of the abstract submission. To submit your abstract, click on the "Abstract Submission Form" link below.

**UPDATE**:We have received a large number of requests to speak. For this reason, for those who have not yet made such a request, we ask that they do so by March 3, via an email to [IntegersJournal@gmail.com](mailto:IntegersJournal@gmail.com) , giving the title of the talk.The deadline for submission of abstracts is April 7, 2025.

Financial Support:

Limited financial support for presenters may be available. Send requests for support to IntegersJournal@gmail.com. Student applications for support should include a brief letter of endorsement from the student's advisor. The deadline for requests for financial support is March 3, 2025.

Lodging and Hotel Reservations:

Conference attendees must make their own hotel reservations. A block of rooms has been set aside for conference attendees at the Holiday Inn Express at a reduced rate of $159 per night. To obtain the reduced rate: telephone the Holiday Inn Express (706-546-8122) and say that you are with the "UGA Math Integers Conference." Note that this block of special-rate rooms will be held for the conference only until April 16. The Holiday Inn Express is about 1 mile (a 20-minute walk) to the site of the conference talks on the University of Georgia campus, and is conveniently located in downtown Athens, within walking distance to many restaurants. The Holiday Inn Express offers a complimentary hot breakfast.

There are several other nearby hotels in addition to the Holiday Inn Express, including: Hampton Inn, Hilton Garden Inn, Fairfield Inn, and Springhill Suites. Making reservations far in advance is recommended for any of these.

Conference Proceedings:

Travel:

The closest airport to the University of Georgia is Hartsfield-Jackson International Airport in Atlanta. It is approximately 75 miles from Athens. In addition to numerous car rental companies at the airport, there are also shuttle services between the airport and Athens - for example, [Groome Transportation](https://groometransportation.com/athens/?&sd_client_id=9a219212-9e62-4280-a16d-0da3d2291acf)makes frequent runs to Athens every day. Other options from the airport include Uber and Lyft. Directions to the conference venue on campus, and parking information, will be sent to all registrants.
