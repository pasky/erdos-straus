1. Introduction 

We denote by Zq the ring of residue classes mod q.For q1|q, let πq 1 : Zq → Zq1 be the quotient map. Our purpose is to state the general case of the sum–product theorems obtained in [8,7,4,6] for special moduli q.We have 

Theorem 1. Given δ1, δ 2 > 0, there are ε > 0 and δ3 > 0 such that the following holds. Let q be an arbitrary (suffi-ciently large ) modulus and A ⊂ Zq satisfying 

(i) |A| < q 1−δ1 ,

(ii) If q1|q and q1 > q ε, then |πq 1(A) | > q δ21 .Then 

(iii) |A + A| + | A.A | > q δ3|A|.

Remark 1. In Theorem 1, ε depends on both δ1 and δ2 (unlike in the square-free case where ε only depends on δ1, see [6]). An important aspect of Theorem 1 is the uniformity of the estimates in q.
