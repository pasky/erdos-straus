## THREESQ — C
- Gauss’s 1801 *Disquisitiones* is the source for the theorem, but the explanation is not an accurate statement of his proof: “all determinant-1 positive ternary forms are equivalent” is false as phrased (forms are not all equivalent); the sum-of-three-squares form has class number one in its genus.
- source: https://archive.org/details/disquisitionesar00gaus

## LINNIK-ERG — C
- ELMV’s Annals paper is “Distribution of periodic torus orbits and Duke’s theorem for cubic fields” (2011); it does not establish the claimed generalized Linnik sphere-point statement as phrased. Duke 1988’s theorem removes the fixed-prime restriction in the ternary quadratic-form setting.
- source: https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2011/173-2/p05.pdf ; https://www.math.ias.edu/~akshay/research/elmv2.pdf

## BHS — C

Attribution “Browning-Matthiesen-Skorobogatov 2014” is imprecise; their 2014 paper treats pencils of conics/quadrics with many degenerate fibres, not an unrestricted replacement of H for all linear-fibre cases.

## SHORT — C
- BHP’s 0.525 result is for primes in [x−x^0.525, x], equivalently intervals ending at x, not directly the displayed forward interval [x,x+x^0.525] (the latter follows by reparameterizing with a negligible exponent adjustment).
- source: https://doi.org/10.1112/S0024611501002698

## ARTIN — C
- Heath-Brown proves at least one of any three multiplicatively independent integers is a primitive root for infinitely many primes; equivalently, at most two prime numbers are exceptions to Artin’s conjecture. The last-step account’s “pigeonhole … all of F_p^* for all three” misdescribes the sieve argument.
- source: https://academic.oup.com/qjmath/article/37/1/27/1515517

## SNIRBOUND — C

## KAKEYA-FF — C
- Dvir’s theorem is size at least c_n q^n for fixed dimension, but the last-step description is misleading: the polynomial vanishes on the Kakeya set and its leading homogeneous part vanishes in every direction; this contradicts nonzero polynomial degree, not “vanishes at infinity” alone.
- source: https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.2336

## KEMNITZ — C

## SUN135 — C
