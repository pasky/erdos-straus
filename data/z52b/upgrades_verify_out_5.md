## HLSQ — C
- “Squares fail by the algebraic factorisation” is not a valid obstruction: a square can equal a prime plus a square (for example, `25 = 2 + 23`); the factorization displayed does not imply non-representability.
- The broad Hardy–Littlewood conjecture that every sufficiently large nonsquare is a prime plus a square remains open; the cited 1923 *Acta Mathematica* reference is consistent with the conjecture attribution.
- source: https://www.google.com/search?q=Hardy+Littlewood+every+large+non-square+prime+plus+square ; https://mathoverflow.net/questions/440606/recent-works-on-the-hardy-littlewood-conjecture-on-primes-represented-by-quadrat
