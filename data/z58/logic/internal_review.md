## Proof audit: what survives

- **Props 2.1/2.2:** MS has a bounded matrix once definitions are expanded. A false instance gives a Q refutation by finite case expansion; all bounds and arithmetic term sizes for MS are polynomial in the *value* p, so p^{O(1)} proof length is a valid upper bound. Σ₁-completeness then gives the truth-from-nonrefutability implication; consistency is unnecessary for that implication. Eventual threshold statements are Σ₂ upper-bound formulations, and independence alone supplies no truth value for them. ES has polynomial witness bounds after sorting denominators (e.g. x≤3n/4, y≤2nx, z≤nxy≤2n⁴). The real-parameter qualifications are finding 6, not a refutation of the main MS classification.
- **Thm 3.1/Cor 3.2:** With the guarded factor equivalence repaired, the consequent is a polynomially balanced Σᵇ₁ search relation. Buss witnessing applies despite primality being Πᵇ₁: its negation supplies the factor branch. The conclusion is a nontrivial factor pair, not necessarily a complete prime factorization. It does not solve general factoring. The FP^NP bound, higher-level coarse bounds, and S²₂→T¹₂→PLS route are sound in their stated scope.
- **Lemmas 5.1–5.3:** A hit is coprime to p and has Jacobi symbol (p/d)=−1. An odd prime divisor ℓ of d therefore has (p/ℓ)=−1; reciprocity for p≡1 (4) gives ℓ≥n_p. The conditions at 2,3,5,7 give exactly the six unit-square classes modulo 840. Finally, for k|A, D=kA divides A² and −4D≡−k mod d, proving H_d⊆𝓡(d). No additional condition on the Ω witness family was lost in this inclusion.
- **Prop 5.4:** All three small-coordinate congruence descriptions are sound; the displayed 16X² bound is safe (4X² already suffices). For a *specified* (u,C), the exact residue is −u mod d, not all of H_d; membership in H_d correctly tests existence of some hit at that d. The error is the later quantitative no-go inference, not the congruence reduction.
- **Lemma 6.1:** Positive leading coefficients give positivity beyond the largest nonnegative root of uBC. At that root all three coordinates are nonnegative, and vanishing of any coordinate makes 4uBC−u−B≤0, contradicting n>0 there. Thus the three constant terms are positive integers and evaluation gives a standard solution. This proof is sound.
- **Thm 6.2:** n₀>0 follows from the allowed residues. In a common polynomial variable σ, n₀+840σ^k has only simple complex roots (its derivative vanishes only at 0, which is not a root), so cannot be a polynomial square. Positivity on σ≥0 and the sign lemma exclude solutions. All standard residues of n₀+840t equal those of n₀ because t/m belongs to the model for every positive standard m. Nonstandard positive elements are reducible: refine the variable to fourth powers, split into real-algebraic factors of degree at most two, and rescale a proper factor to constant term ±1 and the complementary factor to ±n₀; the zero-constant case splits off a positive fractional power. Both factors are positive infinite after choosing positive leading coefficients. Standard positive prime factorizations and standard-prime solutions cannot acquire infinite factors/coordinates. In particular n≥3u−1, n≥3B−1 and n≥4C−2 bound all solution coordinates by a standard n. Thus the model's primes are precisely standard primes, and clause (b) works **with its truth hypothesis**. Shepherdson's assertion that the given model satisfies IOpen remains the cited model-theoretic input, not a newly verified primary-source theorem here.
- **Independent decider:** Monotonicity of 4uBC−u−B in each positive coordinate gives n≥4m³−2m for m=min(u,B,C). Its two divisor searches cover C=m and, by symmetry, u=m or B=m. Residue −1 modulo 4m already forces positive reconstructed coordinates; the missing explicit lower test in the C branch is not a bug.
