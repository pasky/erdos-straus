# Hostile internal review: z58 FFIELD

Scope: all three commits in `ba0f16ca..7152a913`, including the memo, all added scripts, all summaries/logs, and every stored zero row. Read the FFIELD brief, `z53_depth.md`, and the specified z51 dependency proofs. The memo is not edited by this review.

**Verdict: changes required; no FATAL finding.** The even-degree model, its T1 obstruction, the large-q counterexamples, and the even-degree decider survive review. Several mathematical glosses, claimed method exclusions, data interfaces, and numerical descriptions do not. ES remains open; I found no claim that these computations prove or disprove ES.

## Findings

1. **MAJOR — The norm equivalence after Lemma 1.3 is false (`Z58_FFIELD.md:74–75`).** In the constant quadratic extension, an odd-degree prime is inert, so its exponent in a polynomial norm must be **even**, not zero. Even-degree primes split and have unrestricted exponents. Thus “all factors have even degree” implies “is a norm,” but the converse fails. Over F₃ take `f=T⁴−1`: `f+1=T⁴=N(T²)`, yet its factor T has odd degree and `(t,u,v)=(T+1,1,T³)` is a witness. The actual iff in Lemma 1.3 is correct; the following equivalence and the title “u=1 line = sums of two squares” are not. **Fix:** state the correct norm criterion, retain only the one-way implication, and distinguish the unit-line obstruction from the larger norm set. The all-even-factor density analogy can remain, with this distinction.

2. **MAJOR — Corollary 3.2's valid quantifier conclusion is inflated into an unsupported method-wide impossibility (`:155–158`, opening verdict, §5).** z51 does prove that no constant N₀ works for every q, and indeed that any valid choice N₀(q) tends to infinity. It does **not** exclude every argument with estimates improving as q grows, or every use of Lang–Weil/Deligne/Chebotarev in a hybrid argument where n grows with q. Such an argument need not assert the false positivity statement at fixed n. Nor does “this fibre is zero-dimensional” exclude constructing a different auxiliary family. **Fix:** retain the PROVED no-uniform-threshold theorem; replace “large-q direction is dead” by “fixed-n, large-q universal positivity is false,” and label the broader verdict an Assessment about the approaches actually tested. This is especially important in proposed FRONTIER text.

3. **MAJOR — The RH level is sufficient, not proved sharp; for this particular square-subgroup event the stated summation wastes substantial information (`§3.3`, `§5(d)`, bottom line).** Prop 3.3's progression-by-progression argument works, but does not establish “exactly” the level where avoiders exist or an information barrier at n/2. Let H be the unit squares modulo M. Its indicator on units is `2^(−ω) Σ_{χ²=1} χ`. Applying RH to these characters gives, with the usual prime-power correction,

   `# {degree-n primes in H} = π_q(n; coprime to M)/2^ω + O((deg M+1) q^(n/2)/n)`.

   The relative error is therefore `O((deg M+1) 2^ω q^(−n/2))`, rather than the displayed `O(deg M · Φ(M) q^(−n/2))`. For fixed q and `deg M=O(n)`, `ω(M)=O_q(n/log n)`, so this still supplies avoiders well beyond `deg M<n/2`. **Fix:** call Prop 3.3 a conservative RH-explicit range, remove the sharpness/exact-level rhetoric, and either record the stronger subgroup estimate or explicitly leave it unexamined. The scoped B1 conclusion survives; the purported exact B2 barrier does not follow from it.

4. **MAJOR — F2's final summary contradicts the data, and the conclusion about necessary use of both sub-lines is not warranted (`:255–260`, `:288`).** The final table says `s≡3 (4)` does not retain zeros above the last full-family zero. It does: at n=14 it has **3**, whereas the full family's last observed zero degree is 12. It merely has none at n=16. Furthermore finite exceptions do not rule out eventual positivity for a single residue class with a larger threshold. “Any FF argument must use the full union” / “Arguments must not work on one residue class” are not proved. “Fixed-t-degree lines fail polynomially often, B1” also has no asymptotic proof here; a fixed t-degree does not fix the witness divisor degree, so the finite-divisor-menu B1 theorem is not that statement. **Fix:** give the exact observed ranges, remove the necessity/asymptotic assertions, and say explicitly that F2 is only a proxy and does not certify the integer C-parity screen. Replace “reproduces all four falsifiers” / “confirmed faithful” with this qualified Assessment. “Half-mass” is asymptotic, not exact: at n=16 the masses are 40 and 32, not 36 and 36.

5. **MAJOR — The integer least-u output is by decade, not cumulative, and §4.4 misreports it (`scripts/z58_ffield_zleastu.py:15–18`, memo `:243–246`).** `best[len(str(p))]` groups disjoint intervals. The final value 110 is for `10⁶≤p<3·10⁶`, not for all `p<10⁷` or all tested p. The earlier prime 427249 already has least u=131. **Fix:** either label each interval correctly, or take cumulative maxima: for cutoffs 10³, 10⁴, 10⁵, 10⁶, **3·10⁶**, the reported data give **4,19,78,131,131**. The finite-field sentence also has five degrees but only four maxima: it must read **3,3,3,4,4** for n=10,12,14,16,18. CAP failures are not intrinsically certified zeros; here all twelve skipped primes match the supplied known-zero list, so cite that cross-check rather than infer zero from CAP=3000.

7. **MAJOR — q=11 coefficient serialization is ambiguous, and both diagnostic readers decode the wrong polynomial (`enum.c:145`, `factor.py:10`, `irrzeros.py:29`).** Coefficients are concatenated as decimal strings, but readers consume one character per coefficient. 8. **MAJOR — The decider's public iff claim omits its essential even-degree precondition (`decide.gp:1–7`; duplicated in `target.gp`).** The algorithm is complete for even n, not for arbitrary P as its comment says. For the irreducible `P=T³+2T+1` over F₃, `ffdec(P,3)` returns 0, despite the trivial word `(P+2,1,1)`. Thus an odd-degree targeted run can print false “certified ZERO” results. **Fix:** enforce/document monic even degree n≥2, or handle odd n by returning the trivial witness before the restricted search. The even-degree tables are unaffected. Also document that `Mod(.,q)` and the C arithmetic implement prime fields, not arbitrary prime-power F_q.

9. **MINOR — Local-density blindness is overstated beyond its coprimality scope (`:135–143`).** Prop 3.1 proves residue-blindness at ℓ∤f. A product using primes dividing a square g² does distinguish it from an unrelated irreducible P: those factors are `1−Q⁻¹+Q⁻²` rather than `1+Q⁻²`. The meaningful statement is that a prescribed collection of primes avoiding both inputs cannot distinguish them, and a positive local-density product cannot reproduce the global T1 zero. The text itself gives the differing density at ℓ|f immediately afterwards. **Fix:** insert this scope instead of “no product ... at finite places can separate.” Likewise “where the composite zeros come from” is a heuristic explanation of enrichment, not an explanation of any individual zero; use Assessment consistently.

10. **MINOR — Prop 3.3's displayed Dirichlet formula misses M=1 (`:176–178`).** For constant monic M the stated error `O(deg M · q^(n/2)/n)` is identically zero, falsely asserting `π_q(n)=q^n/n`. **Fix:** use `(deg M+1)` or handle M=1 separately, and explicitly quantify `0<δ<1/2`. For nonconstant M the proof's index and error summation are correct; this is not a failure of the lower bound.

11. **MINOR — Lemma 3.4's “only decomposable/sparse” gloss needs a nontrivial-composition hypothesis (`:186–190`).** k=1 is expressly allowed and includes φ=T, reaching every P. **Fix:** retain the lemma for positive odd k, but restrict the sparsity comment to k≥3 and `deg f≥2`; use the elementary upper bound `q^(n/k+k)` for the number of monic pairs at a fixed decomposition degree, rather than an unspecified `q^{O(...)}` as proof of sparsity. Injectivity of substitution itself is valid.

12. **MINOR — Generic-cover Frobenius language needs an unramified qualification (`:147–153`).** The fixed-point formula using the generic monodromy permutation representation applies off the branch locus. An arbitrary P in a ramified fibre does not acquire that unramified conjugacy class merely because the map is finite flat and generically étale. **Fix:** restrict this sentence to the finite-étale open set, as z51 §3.2 does; or explicitly discuss Frobenius on the actual geometric fibre, not the generic sheets. The large-q lower bound already counts an unramified subset, so its proof is unaffected.

13. **MINOR — Computation provenance and several numerical labels need tightening.** The census/target GP files require globals that the advertised Python runner never sets, and the memo does not supply the wrapper commands used to produce the logs. Record `n,part,nparts` for census and `q,n,m,K3,c0,nsamp,amax` for target, including the first-four-cubics convention. q=7,n=10 enumerates **7¹⁰ target polynomials and 30·7¹⁰ words**, not “7¹⁰ words.” The 8m24.872s log times n=16 and n=18 together; it is not a standalone n=18 timing. The 12-minute q=7 timing is not present in the committed logs. F4's sextic percentages should be 1.43% and 1.25%, not the rounded interval 1.3–1.4%. **Fix:** give reproducible wrapper commands, correctly label totals/timings, and scope the “laboratory to 10¹⁴ in minutes” claim to the tiny targeted families, not all primes up to that norm.

14. **MINOR — Labels and novelty/literature scope should remain explicit in the conclusions.** The bottom line turns the labelled least-u heuristic into “has ... a least witness cofactor of logarithmic degree” / “least u is polylog holds in both rings.” Finite maxima are evidence, not an asymptotic law. R mod 3 is only approximately balanced in the larger runs, not literally equidistributed (q=3,n=4 gives 3,3,12). **Fix:** keep EVIDENCE/CONJECTURE labels at each summary use. T1 and the large-q boundary result are correctly credited to z51; the unit-line factorization observation also already appears in z51 §3.6 and should be identified as a specialization, not a new structural discovery. The brief explicitly asks for a verified literature check; this lane candidly did not do one. Mark that deliverable uncompleted, or supply source-verified references; do not infer absence of a published analogue from an old internal search. Proposed FRONTIER statements must say **even** degree when invoking prime zeros of the restricted family: at odd degree the trivial word prevents them.

15. **MINOR — The enumerator lacks domain/resource assertions and an exact saturation control.** Valid recorded runs fit the budget, but the executable accepts nonprime p, unknown modes as `star`, unbounded n despite 64-element arrays, and unchecked power overflow. `ty` allocation and file opens are unchecked. ## Proof audit: what survives the hostile checks

- **Dictionary:** `deg P even ↔ p≡1 (4)` correctly matches the value of `(−1/P)`. `deg t odd ↔ 4|t` is a **chosen sufficient-colour analogy**, not a ring-theoretic equivalence or unique dictionary. The reciprocity exponent proves its advertised T1 property. In particular deg d need not always be odd in the polynomial model, whereas integer d is always 3 mod 4. The memo's Assessment label is appropriate; “exact/faithful” must not silently upgrade this or the F2 proxy. At the level of ordered shapes the star union is not literally contained in z51's a≥1 union: an a=0 star witness must first be swapped. At even n the other cofactor has positive degree, so containment of the *existence events* is valid.
- **Lemma 1.1:** s≥1 makes the product degree strictly dominate both subtracted terms. Thus n=s+a+b; even n and odd s force opposite cofactor parities, exclude a=b=0, and give the odd-degree trivial word exactly as claimed.
- **Lemma 1.2:** `d=tu−1` is monic, `deg u<deg d≤n`, and `f≡−u mod d`; the monic least residue is therefore u. Conversely the specified divisibilities give monic t,u,v and degree n for f+r. `gcd(r,d)=1`; reciprocity gives exponent `deg d + deg r·deg d + deg r`. Opposite degree parities make it odd. For a square target every prime dividing d instead gives symbol +1, including multiplicities in the Jacobi product. All three parts are valid.
- **Lemma 1.3:** for v=1, `f+1=u(t−1)`; an odd-degree divisor has degree strictly below even n, and conversely choosing an odd-degree prime divisor supplies t=d+1 and monic u. The integer unit-line criterion is also correct for integer n≡1 (4). Only the subsequent norm equivalence fails (finding 1).
- **Prop 3.1:** Q²−(Q−1) regular pairs contribute one z each. For nonzero f₀ one exceptional pair contributes Q further solutions; for zero f₀ none does. The stated counts and odd-integer-prime substitution are correct. The Euler product equals `(1−q⁻³)/(1−q⁻¹)`; its displayed values and observed mean ratios check out, but the limiting-prime-mean statement remains heuristic.
- **z51 Lemma 3.2 / Thm 3.3 / Lemma 3.4 / Thm 3.5 / Cor 3.5′:** the lower-weight deformation has positive degree gaps in all the present shapes; finite-flat degree and generic étaleness apply. At the unramified product boundary the ordered partitions carry the S_n action. Adding the root cover preserves the scaling and the boundary splitting field, including the constant-field argument. An n-cycle fixes no ordered partition having at least two nonempty blocks. For even n the trivial shape has even s and is excluded. Therefore z51's **stronger event “irreducible and no nontrivial word of any shape” is a subset of the present zero event**. Its lower bound applies directly, uniformly over odd q as in z51; no fresh monodromy theorem for the odd-s sub-union is needed. Cor 3.2's threshold-divergence conclusion follows, not merely unboundedness. I find no defect in this dependency application.
- **Prop 3.3:** modulo ℓ^e the reduction to the cyclic group F_Q* has an odd-order principal-unit kernel. Squaring is bijective on that kernel, so each local unit-square subgroup has index 2; CRT gives exactly 2^ω even for nonsquarefree M. A square class modulo M remains square modulo every d|M, contradicting Lemma 1.2 for a witness. Summing the per-class RH error produces exactly the factor Φ(M)/2^ω written in the proof; relative to the main term it is `O(deg M·q^(−δn))` in the stated range. The asserted bound follows uniformly over those M, with the constant-modulus correction in finding 10. `deg M_m=Σ_{d≤m} d π_q(d)⌊m/d⌋` has order q^m for fixed q, validating the logarithmic-degree interpretation, not the sharpness rhetoric.
- **Lemma 3.4:** nonconstant monic substitution preserves the identity, monicity and odd t-degree, and is injective on R, hence on ordered words. The R inequality and its contrapositive are valid.
- **Lemma 4.1 / census restriction:** P(c)=−1 gives the displayed exact word. At q=3 irreducibility of degree≥2 excludes P(c)=0, so a zero must take value +1 at all three c; CRT is exactly `P≡1 mod T³−T`. Conversely this congruence is only a necessary filter, not a zero certificate. Every candidate has a unique monic h of degree n−3. Translation preserves monicity, irreducibility, degree parity, the word identity, and this congruence class. Choosing a lexicographically minimal translate is therefore safe.
- **Decider on its intended domain:** opposite cofactor parities at even n give WLOG a<b; then n=s+a+b≥2a+2. With d=tu−1, `a<deg d=n−b<n−a` and `deg d−a` is odd. Conversely each tested monic divisor with these properties reconstructs monic t and v and a word. Factorization plus all exponent vectors enumerates every monic divisor. The strict window does not lose even-degree solutions.
- **Translation stabilizers:** the code correctly keeps fixed orbits; it does not blindly divide by three. The archived counts imply 6 fixed irreducible orbits at n=12 and 36 at n=18: `(3·reps−irr)/2`. All fifteen full-family prime zeros at n=6,8,10,12 have orbit size three, giving the stated five orbits. The n=18 zero result is not invalidated by its nonfree translation action.
