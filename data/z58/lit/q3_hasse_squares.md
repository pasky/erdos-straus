## Notes / bottom line

1. The closest verified **square-parameter Hasse-failure** example is Dao, Theorem 5.2: a carefully restricted family indexed by k=ℓ² has local points and BM obstruction. It is failure at square parameters, not success at primes with squares as the only exceptions.
2. The closest verified **prime-specific pointwise existence** material surfaced is classical quadratic-form representation (e.g. sum of two squares for p≡1 mod4), but this is a standard norm-splitting theorem and has no “fails for infinitely many composites / obstructed exactly at squares” analogue. The potentially relevant modern Lam paper was not verified at theorem level.
3. No verified theorem located in this pass has the requested shape: “existence for every sufficiently large prime, fails for infinitely many composites, and is obstructed at squares.” No result supplies an analogue of the ES numerator-8 subfamily failure.
