## Function-field ES (2015–2026)

Searched arXiv API for combinations of Erdős–Straus, Egyptian fractions, `F_q[t]`, polynomial rings, and function fields, plus repository `FUNCTIONFIELD_ES*.md` notes and arXiv-ID inventory. **No function-field analogue of the 3-term 4/n, 5/n, or Schinzel k/n problem was verified in 2015–2026.** This is a bounded search result, not proof of nonexistence. The function-field items in repo literature are background (e.g. Mahler 1949; Kristensen 2006; McMullen 2010) and are outside this date interval / not function-field ES papers. Any purported hit not opened and confirmed is **NOT VERIFIED**.
