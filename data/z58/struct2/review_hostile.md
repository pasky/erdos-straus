1. **MAJOR — §5.2, §6, FRONTIER proposal and bottom line: the no-go exceeds its proof.** The scoped assertion about an argument applying *verbatim* to arbitrary congruence sub-regions is valid. Counterexamples below 400,000 also cannot exclude an eventual qualitative theorem above 10¹⁷, even one valid for both numerators.
