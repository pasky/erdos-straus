**§1: `IE₁⁻` proves Π.**

- The new modulus `2d` divides `h` when the cofactor `e` is even, and `2d ≥ j+1`. At `j=h`, `d≥h` and `d|h` force `d=h`.
- The code `m=(a+h)²+a ≥ h` makes `i=h` reachable, since `i` runs up to `2m`. Decoding, the invalid-code escapes and all bounds are E₁ with polynomial bounds.
- The step needs no Bézout and no `D₂` guard, as claimed.
- The injectivity equivalence holds: in the converse, `1+2(u+v) = 1+4u+2k ≡ 0 (mod b)`, and `u ≢ v` because `0<k<h`.

**§2: `T_F` fails Π.**

- I checked one subtle point the note does not discuss. `M∩B` contains `2^L` but not `2`, which is consistent only because halving the exponent `L` never reaches 0 in standardly many steps. The elements `2^{εL}` for standard `ε>0` lie outside `C`. So the fibre is genuinely characteristic zero.
