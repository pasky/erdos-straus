## Logic and finite procedures

- **Π, `DL`, vertical fields** (`IE1_DECIDER` §§1–3,6–9,14). For each ordinary degree, all integral coefficients and `a,b`, `h>0`, the bounded root is required unless there is a divisor `d>1` of `h` coprime to `b`. One packed, parameter-free induction per degree uses earlier decoder/division/gcd results. Π takes `b=2,G=X²`. At `H=2^L` in `T_F` there is no escape and a lift of `P` would square-root `1+8p` in the vertical field. `C` is its largest **convex** Π-subring. The general obstruction needs a field map killing a pure dyadic modulus and `p` transcendental over characteristic-zero `k`; it excludes a regular, finite-relative-algebraic-degree or solvable algebraic part over `k(p)` (the `S₅` instance is for the degree scheme, not the quadratic alone). It is not about every residue field of every modulus. The henselian conclusion is about pairs `(R/hR,I)`, `h>1`, `I⊆Jac(R/hR)`, and lifting a supplied solution.
- **New schedules.** `T_Π` keeps full supplied-unit PW, factorial strings over non-initial PA subrings, full Farey/Thue/proper binary reduction and the other z82 positives, mixed `DL` even for nonscalar `h`, and `¬ES(P)`. Henselization supplies the local valuation branch for the free upper coordinate, **not** the specialization of the new arithmetic edge. Positive denominators descend first; strict odd-square specialization is at the seed. `T_mix` interleaves requests, not an end extension of the completed `T_Π`. At each scalar cut prime `q>C`, `B/qB` is properly elementary in `T_mix/qT_mix` as fields. PA order hosts give Boolean affine least search. Fixed-arity reserve-linear least search is instance-level for a fixed matrix over `C`; not arbitrary matrix parameters or full depth-one closure. The pure-language guard reaches only an `O(L)` range (`Y≥0`). See `BCM_TS_LDIV` §§3,6–11.
- **What fails.** At the horizontal `P` in both towers none of `−1,2,3,6` is a square (regular upper `P`-fibre over the true-arithmetic scalar fraction field and primitive contraction); TS and supplements fail there. This is not the large *scalar*-prime field service. The quintic `h² | (1+u)((1+u)²−P)²` has hits but no least `u≥0` at the smooth squarefree scalar `h`, even though every prime component has a square root of `P`. No root on any large subproduct; §9 chooses the testing ideal **after** the finite history, using cut-bounded marking moduli. Do not confuse this with §1's sparse list or the §2 bounded-degree/five-independent-residue solvable exclusions using different polynomials. Parameterized leastness fails; this is not an `IE₁⁻` or `U` separation for either new tower.
- **One target, not a decision** (`IE1_DECIDER` §§10–15; `BCM_TS_LDIV` §10). `U` is closure of `IOpen` under the theorem-rule, not hereditary closure under arbitrary extra hypotheses. `U ⊆ IE₁⁻ ⊆ IE₁` have the same `∀E₁` consequences. Conservation is **known**, credited to Kaye (1990) by Jeřábek (2020) §5; the original was not obtained by the lane. §10 directly proves the exact formulation used. ES has bounded witnesses, hence the equivalence. Global BCM is dispensable: a proper power cut of true arithmetic inherits every true `∀E₁` and fails BCM at its generator. This does **not** supply ES failure. Other least-search, numerical leastness and least-QNR assertions are not in `∀E₁` *as displayed*; no theorem excludes equivalent axiomatizations or separating consequences. Whether `IE₁` proves TS, the supplements, or “for every prime, at least one of 2, 3, 6 is a square modulo it” is open. Smith's fixed-prime direction is not that disjunction.
- **Herbrand and fragments** (`IE1_DECIDER` §11; `U_TRAP` §§1–6; `ES_JUGULAR` §6). ES-provability is equivalent to some finite axiom suite and finite term-triple menu hitting at every `n≥2` in **every** model of the finite Skolemized suite. Repeated calls must use coherent tuple replies. A standard missing trace for **every** suite/menu is equivalent to an ES-countermodel of `U+Th_∀(ℕ,+,·,<)`, not known equivalent to bare `U` nonprovability for ES. `NT(U)` is open, not established by H. The contained `IOpen`+normalized gcd/compatible CRT+rational Farey+proper binary reduction+`DL` fragment does not prove ES (model `T_Π`). The larger graph-existence package allows integer-part algebraic floors and four squares, **no new expanded-language induction**; all its oracles lie in `U+Lag`, only `Lag` not known here in `U`. This is not a proof that `IE₁` proves Lagrange. The separate linear-query host model lacks `⌊√P⌋`; the first-layer floor hull lacks the needed Bézout row. Neither is a `U` model by closure argument.
- **Theorems C/D** (`ES_JUGULAR` §§7–8). C permits fixed length, finite open cases, defined rational arithmetic and signed quotient/remainder, normalized gcd/Bézout, compatible CRT, level-`Q` rational Farey/convergents, proper positive-definite integral binary reduction *with matrix*, and fixed-degree `DL` with register coefficients, `h>0`, root or escape. For each program, **some** reduced progression inside `1 (840)` has **at every sufficiently large prime** a **valid run with all its outputs missing**. Not all runs, not a missing run, not failure of ES there, not arbitrary `U` witnesses. The floor/four-square obstruction is to specializing the full transcript. D needs only a unital specialization of the ES identity and per-denominator multiples/rows (at least one of each) to an odd square `m²>1` in the integers: the three images cannot all be positive.
