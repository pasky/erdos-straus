# Hostile internal review — z60 LOPEZ

Reviewed `Z60_LOPEZ.md`, all eight `scripts/z60_lopez_*` files, the archived LOPEZ outputs, and the cited prior memos/screens. **Verdict: changes required.** The central equivalences, inversion theorem, and scoped Dickson argument survive. There is a genuine branch-decider bug and a false PROVED cardinality claim; several conclusions also exceed their evidence. Nothing here settles ES, U_II, or U₇.

All executed shell commands used `ulimit -v 4000000; timeout N` with N ≤ 1200; at most two computational jobs ran concurrently. No full 10⁹ joint run or 10¹⁰/10¹² integer census was repeated. The memo and its scripts are unchanged; no commit was made.

## Findings

1. **BLOCKER — A-half decider omits the wrapped target 1; the claimed 17 prime zeros are wrong for the stated branch.** Locations: `scripts/z60_lopez_u7.c:22–24`; memo §2 Reading (lines 101–102), §4.2 table/Reading (249, 261), F2 (54), and `u7half_tgt2_primes_1e8.txt`. The actual A target is `4D_f` **modulo f**. Since `4m_f=f+1≡1 (mod f)`, its canonical representatives are `{1} ∪ {r: 1≤r<f, r|(f+1), 4|r}`, not just the second set. At n=13, f=7, A fires with the word `(8,1,3)` (d=2,c=1, cd even), but `TGT=2` reports ZERO. Five archived zeros are false: 13 (f=7), 29 (15), 293 (7), 389 (15), 18773 (7). Repeating the unmodified 10⁸ A-half run reproduces the erroneous file exactly; checking every omitted residue-1 possibility reduces the true list to **12**:
   `5, 53, 193, 673, 1489, 2521, 20641, 21841, 28921, 66529, 225289, 2654401`.
   **Fix:** accept `r==1 || r%4==0` for TGT=2, fix the branch dictionary, regenerate the A-half archive and replace 17 by 12 throughout. Existing union tests do not test the A half: add a branch-specific word-versus-target check, including n=13. This defect does **not** invalidate the full U₇ census, TGT=3, or the joint engine: their B targets already include 1, and `joint.c` explicitly reduces `4*a` modulo f.

2. **SERIOUS — The per-f union counts and disjointness assertions are false.** Location: §2 Prior art, lines 108–109; summary item 1's literal target-set equality. Both D_f and 4D_f contain 1 for **every** f. For f=3 both sets are `{1}`; for f=7 they are `{1,2}` and `{1,4}`. Positive integer divisors 1 and f+1 represent the same residue. Correct counts are
   `|D_f|=|4D_f|=τ(m_f)`;
   `|D_f ∪ 4D_f|=τ(4m_f)−1` if f≡7 (8), and `2τ(m_f)−1` if f≡3 (8).
   For v=v₂(m_f)≥1 the ratio to |D_f| is `(v+3)/(v+1)−1/τ(m_f)`, not the displayed ratio. Neither pair is ever disjoint. **Fix:** define Div⁺ as a residue image, or restrict its representatives to `1≤r<f`; subtract the duplicated residue in every count. The claimed new per-f result must be corrected, not retained as PROVED. I independently checked the corrected formulas and inversion at all f≡3 (4) through 2000.

## Verified mathematics

- **Lemma L, all implications:** valid for positive letters, including 1. F-a follows from Jacobi reciprocity for composite odd denominators too (with the usual denominator-1 convention); divisibility ensures coprimality. Factoring x into its 2-part gives exactly the stated dichotomy. If x≥xy−1 and a selected letter is divisible by 4, y=1 and x≡0 (4); the canonical residue becomes 1, which proves J but is precisely the boundary the later A-half dictionary forgot. J→X, the factorization `(xy−1)(zy−1)=yn+1`, the cofactor congruence mod T, and both shift constructions are all valid. f=3 and n=1 cause no exception to the equivalences.
- **Prop E:** the residue identities, involutions, and branch swap are correct for actual modular sets D_f and 4D_f. Fixed X, reduced residue classes, preservation of 𝔐 (and 1 mod 24), and PNT-AP imply the asserted *limiting truncated* exchangeability. Finitely many primes dividing L_X are immaterial. No growing-X assertion or pointwise existence follows. `joint.c` implements these actual residue sets correctly.
- **Prop D:** unconditional part valid, including f=3: r=2 is excluded by `(p/3)=+1`. Dickson forms are integer linear forms with positive coefficients; t=0 gives 1 for every form, proving admissibility. Repeated forms may simply be deduplicated. Since `(k+1)L_k|M`, both q_k and Q_k are 1 mod L_k; discard finitely many t to have them exceed k+1. Every divisor of `(k+1)q_k` is e or eq_k. For 4∤k, L_k>k+2; for 4|k, the only possible e is k−1, which cannot divide k+1. This proves the four stated bounds, including the factors of 4. Taking the construction parameter at least `max(4X,4g(X))` disproves the specified uniform link law in both directions. The CONV2/WITNESS/DISPROOF_AVENUES attribution is appropriate. Only the later p-dependent “equivalence” fails.
- **Lemma 7(i)–(v):** the word, shift and T forms are equivalent, including y=1 and x≥f; Jacobi/T1 is correct; f=2n+1 proves the n≡3 (4) case. For n≡1 (4), an even outer letter in every (★) word permits reversal into 8|xy; for an A word, the other outer letter is 3 mod 4, so the U₇ condition is exactly cd even. Thus `U₇=(★) ∪ A_even` as **existence predicates** is correct, notwithstanding the broken A-half script. U⁽⁸⁾ containment is valid; residue 1 is already in its B half. The extra-target valuation range in F2 is correct after using canonical residue images. Moduli 7 and 15 have exactly the stated nonresidue hit sets. “Complete moduli” here does not establish that no larger complete modulus exists.
- **Conditional/conjectural status:** the memo's opening and the main Prop D statement correctly distinguish open ES/U_II/U₇, conditional constructions, and finite computations. The MS implication ladder is correct on its stated Mordell-prime domain. There is no proof of any of these open conjectures in this lane.
