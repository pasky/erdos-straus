## Source verification (theorem numbers and actual scope)

1. **Burrin–Shapira–Yu**, *Translates of rational points along expanding closed horocycles on the modular surface*, Math. Ann. 382 (2022), [primary full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8827300/), **Theorem 1.1**, checked directly:
   |δᵖʳₙ,ₓ,ᵧ(Ψ)−μ(Ψ)| ≪ε S₂,₂(Ψ)[y¹ᐟ²+n⁻¹⁺ε y⁻⁽¹ᐟ²⁺θ⁺ε⁾], θ=7/64, Ψ∈C_c^∞(SL₂(ℤ)\ℍ).
   At y=1/n this is n⁻¹ᐟ²⁺θ⁺²ε times the norm: the memo's exponent is right. It is **not** a discrepancy estimate uniform over arbitrary shrinking indicator targets. The norm cannot be dropped. Formula **(1.5)** uses x+j/n, not (j+x)/n; x=0 in this application, so the error is harmless here. Formula **(1.6)** independently confirms the involution used in Prop 4′.
2. **Strömbergsson**, *On the uniform equidistribution of long closed horocycles*, Duke 123 (2004), [author's primary PDF](https://www2.math.uu.se/~ast10761/papers/dmjhoro.pdf), **Theorem 1**, checked directly: for fixed bounded continuous invariant f, cofinite Γ with width-one cusp, convergence is uniform when β−α>y¹ᐟ²⁻δ, δ>0 fixed. Since ℓ=1/y and hyperbolic segment length=(β−α)/y, this is >ℓ¹ᐟ²⁺δ, exactly the quoted exponent. **Theorem 2** gives its unit-tangent-bundle extension. These are shrinking **source-segment** results, not arbitrary shrinking-target hitting theorems.
3. **Sarnak**, February 2015 [letter, primary text](https://math.berkeley.edu/~zstier/sarnakletter.pdf): definition **(7)**, statements **(i)(a),(b)** (pp. 2–3), discussion pp. 5–6, and Appendix 1 **(32)–(34)** checked. The quoted 4/3≤K≤2 and square-root/big-hole explanation are correct for the specified gate sets. Statement (b) is phrased as “most points”; (34) bounds exceptional target measure. It does not guarantee the prescribed target at every scale, or arbitrary arithmetic target shapes.
4. The closed-horocycle y¹ᐟ² rate is explicitly recorded in Burrin–Shapira–Yu **Proposition 2.3, (2.14)**, citing Sarnak (1981), **Theorem 1**. Their introduction records Zagier's RH equivalence. The memo must say **compactly supported smooth** test functions (and test-function-dependent constants), not literally “all smooth Ψ.” I did not independently re-read Zagier's original proof, Kim–Sarnak Appendix 2, COU, or the cited Nevo slides; none is needed for Prop 3/4′. The proposed Γ\G rate appropriately remains CONDITIONAL. Iwaniec–Kowalski's lemma number was not independently source-verified; the displayed Salié formula is the standard one, including the axis and zero-frequency exceptions already stated.
