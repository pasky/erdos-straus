"Theorem 5.4. With probability 1, the elements of S form a Sidon sequence and an asymptotic basis of order 3. Proof. By Lemma 4.1, S always has the Sidon property. By Lemma 5.3, P(m ∉ S+S+S) ≤ exp(−m^{3c−1−o(1)}) ≪ m^{−2} ... by the Borel-Cantelli lemma."
