## Required fixes, in priority order

1. **MAJOR — keep D's quantifiers and domain everywhere.** Its proved assertion is: there exists a **standard numeral** N₀ for which IOpen (equivalently NOI) proves ES at primes n≥N₀ in the class. The introduction's “IOpen proves ES on the whole class” and the second results table omit restrictions. The refinement step explicitly uses prime n>L ⇒ gcd(n,L)=1; it does not cover arbitrary composites or exceptional standard primes. Do not silently exchange “∃ standard N₀, T⊢…” for “T⊢∃N₀…”.
2. **MAJOR — replace equality/finite-union language by coverage.** Correct statement: K_{r,q} is **contained in** a finite union of ET family cylinders; intersections/refinements are polynomially solvable subclasses, not necessarily ET family members. Literal equality is false: 2 mod9 has polynomials ((n+1)/3,n,n(n+1)/3), but is not a union of ET family classes. Six families contain only odd integers. The only member of the remaining family containing 2 is 2 mod3, not a subset of 2 mod9: ET's Type II parameters at n=2 satisfy ce=a+b and 4abd=2+e, forcing a=b=c=d=1, e=2, f=3.
3. **MAJOR/local — correct Prop 5.2's general-profile recipe.** Take D=16, L=∅ and a square-mimicking unit profile with π₂=9 (and πℓ=1 for odd ℓ). The prescribed prime factors r≡1 mod8 force m≡1 mod8 and m²≡1 mod16, never 9. The odd-prime caveat does not fix this. Either retain only the profile-1 statement, which suffices for the claimed compatibility, or replace the general recipe by a CRT/Dirichlet choice of a prime m≡1 mod4 with (m/ℓ)=1 for ℓ∈L and m²≡π modD, assuming the stated fourth-power conditions at ℓ∈L dividing D. At ℓ≡3 mod4 choose the sign of the root; at 2 choose the sign giving 1 mod4. Do not impose m≡1 mod8.
4. **MINOR — repair landing and E(−1) scope.** Add the integrality hypothesis to Lemma 3.5 (Y=1/2 shows why). U64 Lemma 2.3 is stated with b₁≥1, while P/T use (a),(b) for arbitrary b₁: explicitly extend those two conclusions, whose displayed proof is unchanged. In §4.4 replace “every nonstandard prime h” by “every nonstandard prime h≡1 mod4”; E(−1) has no requirement on the others.
5. **MINOR/proof gap — remove the unsupported strength comparison.** “NR … is weaker than ES★” (§5) is not proved, nor is a comparison base theory specified. The (★) congruence n≡−u mod(4uC−1) does not itself supply a square root of u modulo that modulus. Keep only the proved common arc obstruction unless an implication is supplied.
6. **DEPENDENCY hygiene — U64 B's finite-exception sentence is invalid.** No bound puts its arbitrary threshold below 10¹⁷; “or by direct check” is not a proof that every check succeeds. D uses only B's valid tail form, so survives. Delete that sentence, or repair B without computation: eventually positive denominator polynomials in an ES identity have no positive real zero (at the largest zero their positive reciprocal sum blows up while 4/t stays bounded). Thus they are positive for all t>0; Newton integrality also extends off the tail.
7. **SOURCE hygiene — distinguish original access from quotation.** Update `lit_verify.md` with Jeřábek's explicit stronger Lagrange remark; do not describe Shepherdson's 1964 original, Smith's full construction, or Berarducci–Intrigila's proof as independently read when only later statements/abstracts were checked.

## Original-source checks and limits

- **Jeřábek**, archived `data/z65/logic2/jerabek_2010_abelian_qr.pdf`: Def. 5.2 (p.17) defines Legendre by squareness; Thm 5.7 (pp.19–20) proves QR, both supplements and multiplicativity in IΔ₀+Count₂(Δ₀), uniformly over model primes. The next paragraph explicitly concludes: “Lagrange's four-square theorem is also provable in IΔ₀ + Count₂(Δ₀).” Hence the memo's QR_std/Lag consequences are supported, not just conjectured formalizations. Thm 5.8 supports the Jacobi definability/laws. The underlying Berarducci–Intrigila proof was not independently checked.
- **Shepherdson**: BO Thm 1.1 (p.2) explicitly states the integer-part criterion and suffices for the cited inference. I could not verify Shepherdson's 1964 pp.79–86 directly. The archive entry https://www.rcin.org.pl/impan/dlibra/publication/52329 is a planned/restricted object, explicitly accessible only on Institute terminals; search hits for the 1965 JSL item are Goodstein's review, not the original. **Original-source verification incomplete**, not “verified.” The particular integer-square-root consequence can alternatively be proved directly by open induction on the open formula x²≤c.
