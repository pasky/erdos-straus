# Hostile internal review of Z57_SYNTH

Scope: the 444-line version of `Z57_SYNTH.md` present at review time. I did not edit the memo. ES remains OPEN. The exact reformulation and the new C-even counterexamples survive; several purported implications and the central quantitative/effectivity conclusions do not survive as written.

## Findings

1. **[MAJOR] §1.2, L6 row, lines 84–93; §0, lines 24–26; §2.3, lines 177–204 — all seven displayed violations are outside the stated MS domain.** The ladder explicitly concerns primes in Mordell's six classes. The seven primes have residues modulo 840, in displayed order, `241, 257, 577, 409, 241, 577, 409`; none belongs to `{1,121,169,289,361,529}`. Of the 334 tested primes, 249 are Mordell-hard, and none of those 249 violates the tested inequality. The unrestricted numerical observation is correct, but it does not refute the Mordell-restricted L6 in the ladder, even at moderate sizes. **Fix:** distinguish unrestricted L6 from its Mordell-restricted version everywhere; report zero observed violations on the 249 tested Mordell primes. Remove the inference that these seven examples obstruct the uniform-form route to the stated MS. Moreover, small counterexamples cannot justify “a proof cannot rely on the proved constant with any slack” at a much larger threshold.

2. **[MAJOR] Proposition 1.1, lines 80–82 — the claimed implication ladder is not a consequence of monotonicity.** L6 bounds the full-family `X`, whereas L5 bounds `X_G*`. A lower bound for the former does not bound the latter: corner solutions can supply all the positivity. Also, L5 implies L3 when `M_G*>0`, not L4: positivity in G* need not occur in its complete-level subfamily H3. Neither problematic arrow follows from Theorem V. **Fix:** replace the chain by separate arrows: L6 ⇒ L2, with positive slack and a sufficiently large threshold; L5 ⇒ L3 ⇒ L2; L4 ⇒ L3 ⇒ L2; and the two parity-subfamily positivities ⇒ L2. Do not assert L6 ⇒ L5 or L5 ⇒ L4 without new arguments.

3. **[MAJOR] §1.2, L5 row, line 92 — `M_G*>0` is false at some of the listed G*-zeros.** At `p=37`, exact genus enumeration gives `M_gen=2` and `M_G*=0`. The only positive-weight odd/even pair is `(o,e)=(1,2)`, which lies in the corner because `2^4≤37`. Consequently `R_G*=X_G*=M_G*=0`, and L5 holds vacuously, rather than failing. **Fix:** say L5 fails at every G*-zero *with positive genus mass*. If an exact census of such positivity is claimed, supply it; Theorem V is asymptotic and does not provide it at small primes.

4. **[MAJOR] Proposition A.2, lines 337–342 — false as stated; the proof reverses Legendre symbols without its hypotheses.** `n_p` is defined by `(q/p)=-1`, whereas SMCR concerns `(p/q)`. The proposition omits `p≡1 (mod 4)` and ignores the prime 2. A concrete counterexample is `D=3, g=3, p=7`: `H_3={2}`, every unit avoider modulo 6 is 1 modulo 3 and is a square modulo both 2 and 3, so SMCR(3,3) holds. Yet `n_7=3` and `R(7)=0`. Even at `p≡1 (4)`, a least non-residue 2 is not detected by quadratic residuosity modulo 2; every unit modulo 2 is a square. **Fix:** restrict the proposition to `p≡1 (8)` (which includes all Mordell classes), and invoke quadratic reciprocity explicitly; then `n_p` is odd. Alternatively handle `p≡5 (8)` separately using FAMTRY Prop 2.1, or formulate the 2-adic condition modulo 8. Proposition A.3 itself does not have this defect.

The proof correctly uses `δ>`; the statement's `δ≥` includes the equality case, where the substituted bound is zero. The model's “relative saving” is actually `E/M≤(K/m)L^{k−2}p^{-δ}`, not just `p^{-δ}`. **Fix:** state exactly the sufficient strict budget criterion, with `m>0, K>0`, integer nonnegative R, and the distinction between absolute and relative errors. Remove universal necessity language from the title, summary, verdict, and proposed FRONTIER export.

## Overall verdict

**REJECT.** The exact bijection, Jacobi lemma, HCF specialization, and twelve independent C-even zeros are useful and survive. The memo needs substantive revision before export: its L6 counterexamples miss the MS domain, its ladder and A.2 contain false implications, and the effectivity pincer mixes error normalizations and overstates what its calculations establish. “Narrowed” is defensible for those scoped diagnostics only, not for the claimed elimination of two mechanisms or a universal analytic threshold barrier. **ES remains OPEN.**
