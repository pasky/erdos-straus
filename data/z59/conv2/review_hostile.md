# Hostile review of Z59_CONV2

## Verdict

**Needs substantive corrections before integration.** The main divisor equivalences, square-root bound, coordinate-line bijection, and prime-source obstruction survive review. The K=4 union census is supported by the archived data and bounded recomputation. However, the memo misreports the numerator-8 census domain, claims to rescue its own two exceptions, mislabels witness maxima, gives a wrong small census count, and promotes genus/count heuristics and a gcd observation into conclusions they do not prove. The novelty claims need explicit prior-art qualification. No pointwise progress on ES or (MS) is established.

All existing repository files were left unchanged. Computations used the requested 4,000,000-KB virtual-memory limit, timeouts at most 1800 seconds, and at most two concurrent compute processes. C was compiled with `gcc -O2 -Wall -Wextra` into `/tmp/z59_review_lines`; Python ran through `uv`. Large-range checking was restricted to a 10^7-wide subrange, individual samples/certificates, and the archived records—not a new 10^9–10^10 census.

15. **OK — Lemma 1(v), the square-divisor maximum, and Lemma 1(vi).** Locations: lines 53–56, 69–73. If m=∏q^{e_q}, then r(m)=∏q^{⌊e_q/2⌋}; hence **every c with c²|m divides r(m)**.
