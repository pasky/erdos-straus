## Re-derivation of the main equivalence

1. For E, put X=m₀,Y=m₁,z=m∞. Then (nX,nY,z) is an ES triple. At a prime p the reduced numerator `D=(4XY−X−Y)/gcd(XY,X+Y)` divides p and exceeds 1, so D=p and p∤z. Lemma 1.1 recovers a Type II tuple.
2. Conversely a tuple gives E with `(m₀,m₁,m∞)=(bcd,acd,abd)`. For p≥5, p|abd would make all three ES denominators p-divisible, forcing 4≤3. If p|c, two denominators have p², contradicted by the elementary maximal-valuation/short-interval argument (Z65 Lemma P). Thus p∤abcd.
3. For each prime ℓ≠p, the minimum of vℓ(a),vℓ(b),vℓ(c) cannot occur uniquely: the equation's two sides would have different valuations. Hence the pairwise gcds equal γ. Each m_i divides the lcm of the other two. In `(C_m₀⊕C_m₁⊕C_m∞)/⟨(1,1,1)⟩`, the i-th generator has order gcd(m_i,lcm(m_j,m_k))=m_i. Riemann existence supplies the required prime-to-p A-cover; its pullback has the target genus.
4. An abelian triangle action with the ES ratio cannot have exactly one p-divisible inertia order, because every order divides the lcm of the other two; three are impossible by 4≤3. Thus it is Type II. This proves REAL⇔AB⇔Type II⇔the ES denominator condition.
5. Cyclic sufficiency does not require trusting a remembered Harvey citation: the three ES periods have pairwise lcm N=abcdp/γ. For every odd ℓ|N, choose three local inertia elements summing to zero, with the prescribed valuations; the maximal order occurs at least twice. At ℓ=2 it occurs exactly twice: three equal minimal valuations among a,b,c would make the RHS have that valuation while the LHS has a larger one. Local choices therefore exist also at 2. CRT gives a generating triple in C_N with exactly the required orders. Its cyclic cover proves CYC, completing the equivalence.
6. For composite n, the same construction works under gcd(abcd,n)=1; without it the equivalence with REAL is false. At 105 exhaustive enumeration gives two a≤b arithmetic tuples and **no** coprime tuple, agreeing with the memo's warning.

**F1 re-derived independently:** q=4acd−1 is coprime to ac, and `n≡−a c⁻¹ (mod q)` gives gcd(n,q)=1. For k=ac, q≡−1 mod 4k implies (k/q)=1: the 2-part contributes +1, and odd-part reciprocity cancels the two signs. Consequently `(n/q)=−(ac/q)=−1`. At n=m² this is +1, a contradiction. This is exactly the existing Z57_BFII obstruction, not a new geometric one.
