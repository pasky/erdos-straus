Do not use any prime-window outcomes in the marginal probabilities, or optimize the architecture for their agreement. Use the following *composite-only* reference population. In a height bin take integers n in a hard class, subject to the roughness cutoff below; expressly **exclude all primes**. Treat the central n as ONE formal prime generator, and keep the actual joint prime-factor budgets of the consecutive (n+k)/4 in nonoverlapping adjacent pairs. To avoid empty small-height composite reference populations as the roughness grows, use roughness bound min(123, log(upper endpoint)); additionally enforce gcd(n,k1*k2)=1 per pair. This last rule applies at every height. No tuning, no arbitrary late-index search, no inferred ES zero rate. All within-pair small-prime collisions are preserved; other inter-pair dependence, except for the mod 840 fibre, is discarded. Center primality and factorization of centre are not used in positive predictions (primality used to *remove* primes). No prime outcome is resampled.
