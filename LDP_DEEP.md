# LDP_DEEP — higher-order reciprocity as an escape from "parity sees only quadratic characters", and (LD_p) as geometry of numbers (task O177)

Status: internal research note, SPARK phase (spark relay brainstorm D). **ES is not solved here, nor any infinite case
of it.** Labels as in DISCOVERIES.md. Builds on DIFF_DEEP.md (O176; CLAIMED, under review R176), KUDLA_ES.md (O172 as
repaired by R172), GEOMETRY_ES.md §1 (Bright–Loughran). Memos `BRAINSTORM/bsD_*.md`; scripts `scripts/bsD_*`.

**Question.** (a) Can degree-k cyclic algebras / power-residue reciprocity give a "higher-order Diff-parity" that
forces the order-k part of the ES ray condition f ≡ −1 (mod M)? (b) Is (LD_p) — "some maximal order of B_{p,∞} has a
pair of special endomorphisms with level ≥ 2√(covolume/p)" — a reduction-theory statement with an existence proof?
(c) Are the 71 primes missed by the quadratic slices structured cubically?

**Answer in one paragraph.** (a) No, over ℚ and over every number field, for three independent structural reasons,
each PROVED with exact scope: on the ES surface every Brauer class of any order over any base field corestricts to
Bright–Loughran's quaternion class on ℚ-points (§A.1); every Chebotarev/reciprocity obstruction to a slice lives in an
index-≤ 2 subgroup because the certificate integers are norms from a *quadratic* field (§A.2); and the forcing
geometry behind Diff-parity (definite algebras with non-trivial class sets) exists over ℚ only in degree 2, because
Br ℝ = ℤ/2 (§A.3). The one loophole found — Diff-parity over F = ℚ(ζ_M)⁺ with **F-rational** data, where f ≡ −1 is a
genuinely quadratic ray-class condition — turns the certificate into an ES certificate over O_F, and norms do not
descend the target −1 to ℤ (§A.4). (b) The genus is vacuous: (LD_p) is equivalent to the existence of the integer
Gram matrix (§B.1); a fixed order is a lottery (§B.2). (c) The misses are locally-square primes (quadratic data);
cubic data are unbiased; there is no cubic layer (§A.5).

## A. The higher-order reciprocity escape

### A.1 On the ES surface (bsD_brauer_cores)
BL Prop 2.5–2.6: `Br Ũ_{n,k̄} ≅ ℚ/ℤ(−1)`; over k, `Br Ũ_n/Br k` is represented by `α_ζ = (−u₁/u₃, −u₂/u₃)_ζ`, ζ ∈ μ(k).
So over ℚ(ζ_m) there **are** transcendental classes of order m on the ES surface.

**Theorem A.1 (PROVED relative to BL Thm 1.6).** For any number field K, any β ∈ Br U_{n,K} (n ≥ 2), any place ℓ of ℚ and
u ∈ U_n(ℚ_ℓ): `Σ_{w|ℓ} inv_w β(u) = inv_ℓ β₀ + ε inv_ℓ α(u)` with cores_{K/ℚ} β = β₀ + εα. For β of odd order the
ℓ-total is constant. *Proof:* evaluation commutes with corestriction; inv_ℓ ∘ cores = inv_w; Br U_n = Br ℚ ⊕ ℤ/2·α. ∎

The individual components inv_w α_{ζ₃}(u) (cubic tame symbols) are non-constant on ES solutions (all 50 primes
p ≡ 1 (3) below 600, `scripts/bsD_cubic_symbol.py`): the cubic information exists but satisfies no law.

### A.2 On certificate spaces: the Frobenius closure (bsD_frobenius_closure)
**Theorem A.2 (PROVED, Chebotarev).** For a Type II slice (c, d) at odd n (M = 4cd, N = 1 + 4c²dn) the subgroup of
(ℤ/M)^× generated by the classes of the primes that *can* divide N is all of (ℤ/M)^× unless ℚ(√−dn) ⊂ ℚ(ζ_M), in
which case it is ker ψ (index 2). Type I: ℚ(√−d), target −n. So the only local–global obstruction is quadratic: T1 at
squares, the BL level condition (−d/p) = +1 for Type I, nothing for Type II at primes. Odd-order characters cannot
separate −1 from 1 at all.
*Hypothesis that would have to fail:* certificate integers whose prime factors satisfy a **non-quadratic** splitting
condition (norms from a field containing a cubic Kummer or cyclic extension). ES's N_θ(p) are binary quadratic norm
values; sub-families with cubic norm structure only *add* obstructions (they shrink the Frobenius closure).
Cubic reciprocity makes this concrete: the cubic part of f ≡ −1 (mod q) is "π | q is a cube modulo every prime of f",
a Kummer condition no ES certificate imposes.

### A.3 Degree-k algebras over ℚ: tautology, not forcing (bsD_eichler_degree_k)
**Prop A.3.1 (PROVED).** f ≡ −1 (mod M) ⟺ for every χ mod M the finite Diff-sum Σ_{ℓ∤M} inv_ℓ (χ, f) equals that of
(χ, −1). The ES ray condition *is* a family of degree-k Diff-sum identities — tautologically (Artin reciprocity).
**Prop A.3.2 (PROVED, classical inputs).** Over ℚ, only definite quaternion algebras violate Eichler's condition; every
other central simple algebra (degree ≥ 3, or indefinite quaternion) has one type and one locally free class
(strong approximation), so "some order in the genus has P" is a local (congruence) statement — B1. Definiteness
(inv_∞ = 1/2) is exactly what makes |Diff| odd and the supersingular class set non-trivial. Outer forms (definite
unitary groups) have Landherr's ±1 incoherence; GU(D) with D of degree k has order-k invariants only in opposite pairs
at split primes. **Diff-parity and supersingular geometry are one phenomenon, 2-torsion because Br ℝ = ℤ/2.**
Function-field loophole: over F_q(T), Br(F_∞) = ℚ/ℤ admits definite degree-k algebras (rank-k Drinfeld supersingular
loci) — but ES_q certificates are again quadratic, so the loophole is unused (spark).

### A.4 The loophole: F-rational data over F = ℚ(ζ_M)⁺ (bsD_artin_conjugation)
With K = ℚ(ζ_M), η = η_{K/F} is a quadratic ray-class character of F of conductor 𝔪∞₁⋯∞_r (r = φ(M)/2), and
`Art_{K/F}(𝔞) = N_{F/ℚ}𝔞 mod M ∈ {±1}`.
**Prop A.4.1 (PROVED).** For a divisor f of N that is the norm of an F-ideal 𝔞 (e.g. all prime factors ≡ ±1 mod M),
f ≡ −1 (M) ⟺ η(𝔞) = −1. Parity over F with *F-rational* data therefore sees the **full** ray condition; bsC Prop 1
(rational data) does not apply. If 𝔞 = (β) with β ≡ 1 (mod 𝔪), then η(𝔞) = ∏_{v real} sign_v(β): **f ≡ −1 ⟺ β has an
odd number of negative real conjugates** — an archimedean anchor at the r real places of F.
**Construction attempt (F-Gram matrices).** Put T_F = [[β, 1],[1, β′]] with β, β′ ∈ O_F. The F-quaternion algebra
(−β, −det T_F) is ramified at the real places where T_F is definite; Hilbert reciprocity over F gives
|Diff_F(T_F)| ≡ #(definite real places) (mod 2), so for β ≡ 1 (𝔪) the parity of the finite Diff over F *is* η(β):
a genuine "F-Diff-parity" carrying the full ray condition (here β ≡ 1 (𝔪) generates the ideal of norm f, and
det T_F is assumed totally positive; Assessment-level construction, not checked numerically).
**Prop A.4.2 (where it breaks; PROVED).** An F-Gram certificate ββ′ − 4c²dp = 1, β ≡ β′ ≡ −1 (mod 4cd O_F) is an ES
Type II certificate **over O_F**, not over ℤ. Taking norms gives N(β)N(β′) ≡ 1 and N(β) ≡ (−1)^r ≡ +1 (mod M) for r
even: the norm does not descend the target −1. The rational certificates that *are* F-norms (split-supported f) are a
sub-lottery of the rational one, and the F-structure computes η(𝔞) = f mod M — the tautology again.
*EVIDENCE* (`scripts/bsD_split_cert.py 1000000 30`, 75 s): all 9732 primes p ≡ 1 (24) < 10⁶ have a Type II certificate
with cd ≤ 30; all but 5 (3361, 53089, 76441, 549553, 967129) have one with f or f′ split-supported (58% of all
certificates; mostly f prime ≡ −1); 1345 have none with both sides split-supported. Odd squares < 4·10⁴: 0
certificates (T1 control). *Assessment:* the loophole is real as a dictionary (the ray condition is quadratic over
F), but the existence step is an O_F-certificate that does not descend, or a ℤ-certificate whose F-parity is
tautological. Closed.

### A.5 Cubic tests and the 71 missed primes (bsD_missed_primes, bsD_frobenius_closure)
* Kummer data of p ("2 / 3 a cubic residue mod p", "2 a quartic residue mod p"), stratified by the full residue p mod 4cd,
  24 slice/type tests: all |z| ≤ 2.2 (`scripts/bsD_cubic_test.py 300000`). The large "p a cube mod 7" effect on Type I
  (z ≈ 40) is the congruence p ≡ −1 (mod 7) (trivial divisor), i.e. B1.
* The 71 misses (p ≡ 1 (24) < 10⁶) are locally square: (p/5) = (p/7) = 1 in 68% (control 24.5%), mean least QNR 12.0
  (control 7.5); cubic data unbiased. For p ≡ 1 (24) all nine Type I quadratic slices are BM-blocked
  ((−d/p) = +1 for d | 6), so only nine Type II slices remain. Layers by exponent of (ℤ/4cd)^×: quartic cd | 60 (54
  slices) misses 2; sextic cd | 126 (54 slices) misses 12; exponent 12 misses 0. Ordering = modulus size, not
  reciprocity.

### A.6 Consolidated statement (PROVED parts with exact scope)
Over any number field, a mechanism whose information about the ES divisor f comes from (i) Brauer classes on the ES
surface evaluated at ℚ-points, (ii) Frobenius closures of the certificate's splitting conditions, (iii) Diff-parity or
Weil indices with ℚ-rational data, or (iv) class sets of definite forms of inner forms of GL_k, sees f only through
quadratic characters; for (ℤ/M)^× of exponent > 2 (M ∤ 24) the ray condition is not such a function (bsC Cor 2).
Unviolated hypotheses — the only doors left: F-rational data *with* a descent mechanism for −1 (A.4.2 shows norms fail
for r even; r = φ(M)/2 is odd only for M ∈ {3, 4, 6, q^k, 2q^k : q ≡ 3 (4)}, so for ES moduli M = 4cd > 4 it is always even); non-archimedean anchors (a target equal to a finite
Frobenius, not −1); function fields (A.3).

## B. (LD_p) as geometry of numbers (bsD_genus_vacuity_LDp)

**B.0 Gram form in the basis (x, w = x + y) (PROVED, from ET Type II 4abcd = cp + a + b, a + b = ce).**
Gram(x, w) = [[Ma − 1, Ma], [Ma, Mce]], det = Mcp, ≡ diag(−1, 0) (mod M). So a Type II certificate is a binary
sublattice of O⁰ whose reduction mod M is "one involution plus one radical vector", of determinant exactly Mcp.
**B.1 Genus vacuity (PROVED from DIFF_DEEP Thm 1.1).** Every certificate-shaped T has Diff(T) = {p}, hence is a Gram
matrix in some O⁰; (LD_p) ⟺ existence of the integer T. The Siegel mass Σ_O r(O⁰,T)/|O^×| is positive for every such
T: no reduction-theory or mass-formula statement over the genus adds information.
**B.2 A fixed order (Assessment).** T ↪ O⁰ needs a dual vector of the right norm *and* the right orthogonal complement;
the latter is a joint-equidistribution (Aka–Einsiedler–Wieser-type) event with O(1) expected hits per T: a lottery at
every determinant, consistent with O176 §2.5 (canonical curve ≈ 50%). [A first draft claimed Duke–Schulze-Pillot makes
every T represented at large determinant; false — DSP represents numbers, not binary forms.]
**B.3 Minkowski/Siegel lemma** gives level M at det ≍ M²f^{3/2}p, not ≤ pM²/4 (DIFF_DEEP Prop 2.4): B3.
*Assessment:* direction (b) collapses to the H-A core; the geometry contributes nothing beyond the arithmetic of T.

## C. Verdict
* PROVED: A.1, A.2, A.3.1–A.3.2 (classical inputs), A.4.1–A.4.2, B.0, B.1.
* EVIDENCE: no cubic/quartic Kummer signal (A.5); the 71 misses are locally square; cd ≤ 30 Type II certificates exist
  for all p ≡ 1 (24) < 10⁶ (a fixed finite slice set — fails i.o. under H by redteam-automorphic-2 Thm 2.2, so this is
  a record, not a route).
* Assessment: **the "higher-order Diff-parity" escape is closed over number fields**: the archimedean place has 2-torsion
  Brauer group, and every ES certificate integer is a quadratic norm. The ray condition becomes quadratic only over
  ℚ(ζ_M)⁺, where its certificates live over O_F and do not descend. No route to ES.
