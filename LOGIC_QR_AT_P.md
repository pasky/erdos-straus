**Result.** In the joint PID construction with full TS, NR, internal Dirichlet and four squares (`LOGIC_JOINT_NR_TS.md`, `LOGIC_FOUR_SQUARES.md`), one can have `(R/PR)× / (R/PR)×² ≅ ℤ/2`, with every nonzero rational constant square and `X−1` nonsquare. Here `P=X²+Y²` is the same ES-failing prime and is a square unit at every ordinary place. This gives both directions of quadratic reciprocity **at `P` against every standard odd prime**, not reciprocity against every internal prime.

**Valuation invariant.** At a node with algebra `A`, maintain a discrete valuation `v` on `κ=Frac(A/PA)` with uniformizer `π=X−1`, trivial on `ℚ`, residue characteristic zero. Initially `κ=ℚ(i)(X)` since `Y/X` squares to `−1`; use order of vanishing at `X=1`. Every *previous* type of step extends `κ` purely transcendently: polynomial and Dirichlet steps are lines; a coprime row has a surviving pivot; a cone `DH=V²+s` has the `D≠0` chart even when `P|s`. A TS circle is never taken on a rational associate of `P`, since `P` already splits over the Gaussian constants. At any other target its generic reduction over `κ` is `uw=s̄≠0` since `i∈κ`. The four-square fiber similarly has chart `uw=s̄−zt`, rational even at `s̄=0`. In each case take a Gauss extension on the free parameters of that chart. The valuation is on the **residue field at `P`**, not an adelic valuation or an ordering.

**New step.** Fairly schedule every `a∈A` on each history. At service time, if `ā≠0`, `v(ā)` is even, and `ā` is not yet square, adjoin `B=A[V,H]/(V²−a−PH)`. It is free of rank two over `A[H]` by monicity and embeds on inverting `P` into `A[1/P][V]`. Modulo `P` it embeds into `κ(√ā)[H]`, with that ring's fraction field, so `P` stays prime. Modulo any other old prime `r`, freeness over `(A/r)[H]` makes localization at `P` injective, giving a domain by the polynomial chart. Nagata after inverting `P` gives factoriality. Over `A[i]` the old factors `X±iY` of `P` each have **fraction field of the quotient** isomorphic to `κ` over `A/PA`: over its generic point the finite étale constant extension is `κ×κ`. These are the two simple prime factors (both nonunits; `P` is rational-prime). Thus `ā` is nonsquare on both components and each factor remains prime by the same monic argument. Nagata again gives the Gaussian UFD. Units remain constant: in the localized Gaussian chart a unit is an old Gaussian scalar times powers of the two old factors, whose valuations must both vanish for a global unit. The rational case is the same with `P`.

In the normal form `f(H)+Vg(H)` substitute `H=(V²−a)/P`. The nonzero blocks have even and odd leading `V`-degrees, respectively. Thus `B∩Frac(A)=A`. Order `V` above the old field: only old constants in `V` can be bounded. A positive reciprocal triple descends by taking the residue at `V=∞`: the constant coordinates already give the whole sum, so positivity forbids any omitted term. At every ordinary mark `P` is a unit; choose a generic integral `V`, and `H` is integral wherever `a` is. Tail `H` need **not** be integral at an exceptional place where `a` is not. Over the joint finite tree, including the auxiliary square-root cover of the base, the new edge on `P≠0` is a polynomial line. Hence the existing finite-prefix avoidance and parsimony proof, PID and floor descent, and polynomial obstruction to ES are unchanged. Do not adjoin a square root of `P` in the ordered tower.

**No ramification.** Write `ā=π^{2m}u` with `u` a valuation unit. Adjoining `√ā` is adjoining `√u`. Over the valuation DVR the algebra `Z²=u` is finite étale (`2Z` is a unit). Localize at any prime over its maximal ideal: this is an unramified DVR with the same uniformizer, whether or not the reduction splits. Then extend by Gauss on `H`. These choices can be fixed per node before the adelic marks. In particular no later step halves `v(π)=1`.

**Limit.** In `κ∞=⋃κ` every even-valued nonzero element is square: represent it by `b/c` from a node, and schedule a lift of `bc` in `A`. Its value has the same parity and `(bc)/c²=b/c`. A square already obtained persists. Conversely no odd value is square, and division by `π` changes odd to even. The Bézout unions give `A∞/PA∞=κ∞`; also `A∞=R⊗ℚ` and `R/PR` is a field of characteristic zero, so localizing it at standard integers does nothing. Thus `R/PR=κ∞`. The class of `X−1∈R` is an actual nonresidue (indeed `0<X−1<P` in the initial order).

For ordinary odd `ℓ`, `P` is a nonzero square mod `ℓ`, while `ℓ` and `ℓ*= (−1)^((ℓ−1)/2)ℓ` are squares mod `P` by value zero. Since `P≡1 mod 8`, the sign and dyadic supplements agree too. **Boundary:** the previous reversed nonresidue tag need not persist. For a cone on `P`, the Gauss parameter `D` has value zero mod `P`, hence eventually becomes square there. The NR identity `DH=V²+P` remains a valid certificate; it never required `D` to be nonsquare mod `P`.
