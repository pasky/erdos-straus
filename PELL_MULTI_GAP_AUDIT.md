# Audit: compressing several integral negative-Pell inputs

(§0 block: z74 Opus xreview fix)
```
Result: bounded outputs of fixed polynomial (or rational, avoiding 0/0) formulas on r integral negative-Pell witnesses
        equal those of a finite menu on the fundamental witness, with NO new coefficient denominators.
Label: PROVED. Finite cancellation tests CERTIFIED (exact integer arithmetic; independently recomputed).
Scope: compression only; for rational menus the small-fundamental-unit regime remains open (as the note states).
Review: cross-model (Claude Opus), data/z74/xreview/REVIEW_PELLNOGO_OPUS.md — ACCEPT-WITH-FIXES (§0 only).
```

**PROVED.** Fix `r`, a finite menu, and an output height exponent `h`. At an ordinary nonsquare prime `p`, every tuple of integral outputs `|m_i|≤p^h` obtained from polynomial formulas on `r` integral negative-Pell witnesses is obtained from a finite menu of polynomials on **one** witness, the fundamental one. The new polynomial coefficients need **no additional common denominator**. The same compression works for fixed rational functions, with **rational**, not necessarily regular, output formulas whose denominators are nonzero at the hit. No upper bound on an input witness. The exponent bound and finite menu below depend on the original formulas and h, not on p. This is a value-at-hits result, not a symbolic identity on the product of conics. Rational outputs of bounded multiplicative height work as well, using `bA−aB` for `m=a/b` with `max(|a|,|b|)≤p^h`.

**Height and group.** Use units of the order `ℤ[√p]`, not half-integral units of the maximal order. If there is a norm `−1` witness, the least unit `ε>1` in the positive real embedding has norm `−1`. Indeed positive units are discrete on compact subintervals of `(0,∞)` by recovering the two integer coefficients from `w` and `±1/w`; division by powers of the least one generates all of them. Thus `ε=x₀+t y₀>√p`, `t=√p`, and each witness unit is `δ_i ε^{n_i}`, with `δ_i=±1`, `n_i` odd, possibly negative. For ES, the output bound is automatic: sorted `a≤b≤c` give `a≤3p/4`, `b≤2pa/(4a−p)`, `c=pab/(4ab−p(a+b))≤pab`. In particular `c≤p⁵`. Nothing here bounds the heights of numerators and denominators of an input rational function *evaluated* at the witnesses.

**The gap check.** For each output `m` use `A−mB=0`, with `B=1` for a polynomial. Substitute `x_i=δ_i(ε^{n_i}−ε^{−n_i})/2`, `y_i=δ_i(ε^{n_i}+ε^{−n_i})/(2t)`. Clear one common scalar denominator and power of p. This produces a labelled sum `Σ c_α ε^{α·n}=0` with a bounded number S of terms; coefficients are algebraic integers in `ℤ[t]` and both conjugates have size `≤p^C` for fixed C, since m is small. Keep labels of **both** A and B even where coefficients combine to zero. Across a gap `L−l>0` in the sorted exponents, normalize the upper tail by `ε^{-L}`; its terms have nonnegative powers. If its value T is nonzero, `|σ(T)|≤S p^C` because `σ(ε)=−1/ε`, whence `|T|≥1/(S p^C)` by the nonzero integral norm. But the lower terms give `|T|≤S p^C ε^{l−L}`. Thus both sides of any gap with `ε^{L−l}>S²p^{2C}` vanish separately. Differences of successive vanishing tails give vanishing of every cluster separated by such gaps. In particular, cutting only at gaps greater than a fixed integer G with `p^{G/2}>S²p^{2C}` for all `p≥2` suffices; each cluster has width `≤SG`. This also deals with a negative tail: conjugation reflects a zero to a zero, including the norm-minus signs. Neither tail need vanish as a formal Laurent polynomial.

**Bounded replacement, retaining the original modulus.** For every output and each cluster record its labels and all differences `(α−β)·n` therein (including coincident exponents). These records and input signs have finitely many possibilities. Solve **all** recorded integer linear equations at once with `n'_i≡1 mod 2`. The coset of solutions is nonempty (contains n); choose one fixed representative `n*` per feasible record. At any replacement in that coset, each labelled cluster is multiplied by a single power of ε, hence stays zero at this specific p and m. A collision between *different* translated clusters does no harm. For polynomials take `n'=n*`. The new witnesses, up to the recorded signs, are coefficients of `(x₀+t y₀)^{n'_i}`: for negative exponents use `ε⁻¹=−x₀+t y₀`, then reduce `t²=P`. This is an **integer polynomial pair**, not an expression with extra powers of 2 in its coefficient denominators. Substitute these pairs in the original menu. The bounded signed replacements need not be positive inputs: what the resulting formulas take as input is the positive fundamental pair; the outputs at the hit are exactly the original positive integers. Thus the regular one-witness no-go on `1 mod M` applies with the original coefficient modulus (see `PELL_POLYNOMIAL_MENU_AUDIT.md`). Choosing a few small exponents *independently* for different terms without preserving their differences is not justified.

**Avoiding 0/0 for quotients.** A single `n*` can make a denominator zero. For example `1+(y₃−1)/(x₁−x₂)` at `p=5, y₃=1` gives 1 for distinct positive `x₁,x₂`; replacing both corresponding exponents by the same one satisfies the cleared equality but gives `0/0`. Write the solution coset as `n*+Λ`; let `D` be the product of all original output denominators after substitution. It is a Laurent sum with at most J labels, nonzero at n. Choose a fixed vector `v∈Λ` separating all distinct exponent linear forms of D **restricted to Λ** (avoid finitely many proper hyperplanes in the lattice; for rank zero take `v=0`). Merge labels with equal restriction, including their factors at `n*`. At least one merged coefficient is nonzero because D is nonzero somewhere on the coset. Along `n*+kv` this is now a sum of distinct exponentials with bases `ε^{β·v}`. The Vandermonde determinant shows it cannot vanish at all `k=0,…,J−1`. Include those finitely many replacements: one preserves every output and every nonpole simultaneously. All choices of `n*,v,J` require only the record/lattice and fixed supports, **not** the values of the fundamental unit. This proves one-fundamental-witness rational compression without an unjustified division by a zero cluster. Equivalently for a single quotient one may select a cluster with nonzero B-part and divide the two sums after removing the common power; rationalize over `t↦−t` only where the selected denominator and its conjugate are nonzero at the hit.

**Height limitation.** On `ε>S²p^{2C}`, every distinct exponent is already separated. For a quotient at least one grouped B-coefficient is nonzero; its grouped A/B coefficient ratio in `ℚ(√p)` equals m. There are finitely many such groupings/signs, and averaging the ratio with its conjugate gives one of finitely many rational functions of `P` alone, regular at the hit. The one-parameter rational-function integrality test (polynomial division) then leaves only polynomial identities at unboundedly many hits, after the appropriate fixed modulus for their coefficients. This gives the usual rational-menu conclusion **on the large-fundamental-unit regime**, or conditionally outside a sparse exceptional set if the requisite prime/progression height estimate is supplied. Unconditionally, a reduced nonidentity on one conic still has a thin projection; the unresolved inference is to discard *rational identities* there by the regular degree argument. For example `(P/2,P(1+Y)/2,P(1+Y)/(2Y))` is a positive nonconstant rational ES identity at `P,Y>0` (not an integral triple at odd primes). Compression alone supplies no estimate `o(B/log B)` for the remaining small-unit primes; a density-zero statement among all integers is insufficient. No analogous reduction here for arbitrary rational Pell **inputs** (not powers of the integral fundamental unit), unbounded r, or unrestricted output height.

**Exact cancellation tests.** Put `x_j` for the real coefficient of `ε^j`, j odd. At `p=5`, `ε=2+√5`, so `ε⁴−18ε²+1=0`. For *every* odd `N>0`, the upper tail of `x_{N+4}−18x_{N+2}+x_N+7` vanishes, and its reflected tail vanishes, giving 7. With `p=17, ε=4+√17` the same two tails need not vanish: the coefficient 18 would have to be 66. Even at 5 replacing ε by `ε³` as the spacing generator breaks this cancellation. Thus equality at a hit cannot be promoted to a symbolic coefficient test. For a quotient, `(x_{N+4}+x_N)/x_{N+2}=ε²+ε⁻²=4x₀²+2` despite the absence of an exponent-zero term in numerator or denominator for positive N. At `p=176401` one has `x₀=420,y₀=1`, value 705602. Finite replay by integer pair multiplication at all three primes and `N=1,21,101` verified these identities. Scope: F1 no square target or Pell generator there; F2 no numerator-8 obstruction; F3 nonsquare positive nonspecial D would also admit the compression whenever there are integral negative-Pell units; F4 no new ordinary ES zero implied.
