# Prescribing the field at the ES-failing prime

**Result.** Let `k` be any countable characteristic-zero field of infinite transcendence degree containing a square root of `−1`. In the joint external PID model of IOpen + Dirichlet + TS + NR + Lagrange, one can retain the square-unit marked ES-failing prime `P=X²+Y²≡1 mod 840` and prescribe `R/PR ≅ k` **as a field**, not just up to elementary equivalence. This replaces, rather than supplements, the parity-valuation invariant of `LOGIC_QR_AT_P.md`. In particular the arbitrary-field statement cannot promise a nonsquare for algebraically closed `k`.

**Invariant and scheduling.** Embed `κ=Frac(A/PA)` in `k` at each finite node of the rational tower, extending the embedding of its parent. Initially this is `ℚ(i)(X)`; choose `X` transcendental and choose the image of `Y/X`. The existing stages in `LOGIC_JOINT_NR_TS.md` and `LOGIC_FOUR_SQUARES.md` have purely transcendental residue fraction-field extensions at `P`. Rows have a surviving pivot, cones have their `D≠0` chart even at `s̄=0`, and ordinary polynomial stages are free. A circle never targets a rational associate of the already Gaussian-split `P`; in the generic residue field it has chart `uw=s̄≠0`. Four squares has chart `uw=s̄−zt` even if `s̄=0`. Extend each embedding by choosing fresh algebraically independent elements of `k`. No step needs `k` to contain the *ordered* fraction field. Each node has finite transcendence degree, and different branches need not choose elements of `k` independent of one another.

Along every history service a fixed enumeration of `k`. If the requested `α` is already in the image, skip. If transcendental over the image, add an ordinary polynomial variable above the old ordered field and send its residue to `α`. Otherwise identify `κ` with its image, put `E=A/PA`, and let `g(T)=Tⁿ+∑c_j Tʲ` be the minimal polynomial, `n≥2`. Choose `0≠b∈E` clearing all `c_j`. The polynomial `bⁿg(T/b)` is monic irreducible over `κ` and has coefficients in `E` (`b^{n−j}c_j∈E`). Lift it **monically** to `f∈A[T]` and form

```
B=A[V,H]/(f(V)−PH).
```

Modulo `P` the fraction field is `κ(bα)(H)`: the monic quotient embeds in its generic field, so `P` remains prime. Send `V` to `bα` there and `H` to a transcendental over `κ(α)` in `k`. This includes `α` in the new *field* image, even if `1/b` is not yet an element of the residue ring.

**Checks on the modification.** The whole ring is free over `A[H]` with basis `1,…,V^{n−1}`. At an old rational prime `r` other than `P` up to associates, this same freeness after reduction makes localization at `P` injective; that chart is a polynomial domain over `(A/r)[1/P]`. Over `A[i]` the factors `X±iY` of `P` are distinct simple primes: the generic algebra at its divisor is `κ×κ`, the constant extension is finite étale, and both factors are nonunits. On either component the old residue fraction field is canonically `κ` **over `E`**. Thus the reduction of `f`, whose coefficients come from `A`, is irreducible on both components; their quotients are domains by monicity, and both Gaussian factors persist. On inverting `P` one has a polynomial UFD, over either constant field; Nagata gives the unlocalized UFDs. Their units stay constant: in the localization, every unit is a scalar times powers of the inverted primes, and all those orders must be zero for a global unit.

In a nonzero normal form `∑_{j<n} Vʲ h_j(H)`, substitution `H=f(V)/P` gives block degrees `j+n deg h_j`. They are distinct modulo `n`, so the highest cannot cancel: the only elements constant in `V` are in `A`. No condition on `f(0)` is used. Taking `V` above the old field preserves the bounded-elements invariant. For any positive ES triple at an old target, the residue at `V=∞` drops exactly the reciprocals with nonconstant denominators; the constant reciprocals already sum to the entire old right side. Positivity prohibits dropping any, so all three descend to `A`.

At the ordinary marks `P` is a unit; a new tail chooses a generic integral `V` and computes `H`, without requiring integrality at every exceptional place of old coefficients. On `P≠0` this edge of the finite tree is a free line, also after the auxiliary square pullback. All `k`-embedding decisions can be fixed on the tree before assigning marks; the actual circle history is then selected as before. Thus the finite-prefix avoidance, parsimony, prime normalization, existing arithmetic witnesses and ES descent of the joint construction still apply. The union embeds in `k` on the residue fraction fields and its image contains every enumerated element. Finally `A∞/PA∞` is a field by Bézout and primality, and `R/PR` is a characteristic-zero field by the same argument. Since `A∞=R⊗ℚ`, localizing `R/PR` at standard integers gives `A∞/PA∞` without changing it. This proves exactness.

**A pseudofinite witness with an actual nonresidue.** Enumerate the finite Galois subextensions of `ℚbar/ℚ`; let `L_j` contain the first `j` and `ℚ(ζ_{j!})`, in an increasing sequence of finite Galois extensions. One can choose increasing ordinary primes `p_j>2^j` **split completely in `L_j` and explicitly ES-solvable**. Here is a way that needs no uniform ES density estimate. For each `j` choose an auxiliary prime `q≡3 mod 4` unramified in `L_j`. Then `L_j∩ℚ(ζ_q)=ℚ`: every nontrivial subfield of the prime cyclotomic extension is ramified at `q` (total ramification). In the compositum use Chebotarev with Frobenius trivial on `L_j` and `−4 mod q` on `ζ_q`. This constant choice means the automorphism `ζ_q↦ζ_q^(−4)`, *not* a requirement for `−4` to split in `L_j`. For any resulting large `p`, set `c=(q+1)/4`, `b=(1+pc)/q`. These are positive integers and `4bc=1+b+pc`, giving the ordinary solution

```
4/p = 1/(pbc) + 1/(pc) + 1/b.
```

Thus `p_j≡1 mod j!` and all the primes used have witnesses, without assuming ES in general.

Take a nonprincipal ultraproduct `K=∏𝔽_{p_j}/𝒰`. It has characteristic zero, and every rational polynomial splits there by eventual complete splitting, ignoring finitely many bad reductions. Its relative algebraic part is therefore a copy of `ℚbar`. Let `u` be a sequence of nonzero quadratic nonresidues; its class is nonsquare by Łoś, hence transcendental since every nonzero algebraic element is square. `K` is uncountable: each binary sequence is encoded by its first `j` bits as an element of `𝔽_{p_j}`, distinct sequences giving eventually distinct codes. Choose countably many algebraically independent elements, and take a countable elementary subfield `k ≺ K` containing them, `u` and the algebraic part, by downward Löwenheim–Skolem. This `k` has the **entire complete first-order field theory of this ultraproduct**, not just the same universal theory or the same squareclass group. There is no single complete theory shared by all finite fields. Quadratic character here means `0`, square, or nonsquare; there are exactly two nonzero squareclasses, as in every odd finite field. One may initialize `X↦1+u` to name the nonresidue `X−1` in the actual model. Every standard prime `ℓ` (indeed every nonzero rational constant) is square mod `P`, while `P` is a square unit at `ℓ`. This matches both directions of standard-prime reciprocity at `P`, including the supplements since `P≡1 mod 8`. It does not match the specific congruence `P≡1 mod j!` for all `j` (impossible for an infinite `P` in this PID), nor transfer positive ES witnesses from the integers to `R` by elementary field equivalence.

**Classical comparison.** Macintyre–Marker, *Primes and their residue rings in models of open induction*, APAL **43** (1989), 57–77, DOI `10.1016/0168-0072(89)90025-0`, already prescribe characteristic-zero fields of infinite transcendence degree as residue fields in open induction (see the review quotation in `data/z66/ie1/lit_ie1.md` §4). No novelty claim for field prescription alone. The checks here concern retaining the **same ES-failing prime** and the joint axioms, with `i∈k` required by this norm-plane base.
