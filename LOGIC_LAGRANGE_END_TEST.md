**PROVED (one successor).** Let `R₀` be a countable discretely ordered external PID with IOpen and Lagrange's four-square theorem, e.g. the combined PID prime countermodel. There is a countable proper ordered end extension `R₁` with IOpen and Lagrange, and **one** `h>0` divisible by every positive element of `R₀`. Consequently one common multiple handles all intervals of old height, including simultaneously every standard polynomial height at the old bad prime. That prime remains irreducible and admits no ES triple. Here “prime” in the arithmetic sense is absence of a nontrivial positive factorization; for this construction the old prime also remains a ring-theoretic prime. **Not proved:** that the construction can be iterated starting at `R₁`, full bounded common multiples, or preservation of Bézout.

**1. Local algebra.** For every nonzero prime ideal `(q)` of `R₀`, take its positive generator `q`, the DVR `(R₀)_(q)`, its completion `V_q` and fraction field `K_q`. One must include the nonstandard primes. At `q=2`, `R₀/2^j R₀=ℤ/2^jℤ` for standard `j` by division with remainder from IOpen and discreteness. Thus `(2)` is prime and `V₂=ℤ₂`. The continuous four-square map on the compact `ℤ₂⁴` has closed image, containing every nonnegative standard integer by ordinary Lagrange; it is onto.

At every other `q`, `2` is a unit. Apply **old** Lagrange to `q`. Some coordinate in the representation is a `q`-unit: otherwise `q²|q`. Reduction gives a nonzero isotropic vector, which lifts to an **exact** isotropic vector `u∈V_q⁴` with a unit coordinate by one-variable Hensel (derivative twice that coordinate). Choose `b` with `u·b=1`; replace `b` by `b−(b·b)u/2`, now isotropic with the same pairing. Then `u+(a/2)b` has norm `a` for **every** `a∈V_q`. This uses neither finiteness of the residue field nor a global integral representation of a negative old number. In all completions any fractional `a` is represented in `K_q` by first scaling by an even power of `q` and then scaling back.

**2. Build the relative tower.** Write `F=Frac(R₀)`, `K=rc(F)`, and `O` for the convex hull of `F` in each ordered ambient field. Begin with `F[h]` with `h>F` and (separately) a family of `F`-algebra maps `e_q(h)=0` to `K_q`. Maintain `A∩O=F`, and the compatible embedding of the ordered residue field of `rc(Frac A)` into `H=rc(Cauchy completion of K)`, as in `LOGIC_BEZOUT_COLLECTION.md` §1. `K` is order-dense in `H`, `F` cofinal, and countable residue images admit fresh transcendental residues in any open interval there. Dovetail two types of tasks at finite stages.

* Relative floor approximation: for `α` not `O`-close to `A`, take `Y=α+t` with fresh residue, as in the cited §2. This is a free polynomial adjunction preserving `A∩O=F`; give `Y` value zero at all marks. Targets already `O`-close require no variable.
* Four squares for **every** old positive `s∈A` outside `O`: adjoin `u₁,…,u₄` with `Σuᵢ²=s` and use the normal form linear in `u₄`. Put `r=√s` in the real closure and take `u_j=r t_j` for `j≤3` with algebraically independent fresh relative residues in a small box inside the unit ball, then `u₄=r√(1−Σt_j²)`. The fresh-residue construction may be performed three times, with interval restrictions. In the normal form divide by the maximum magnitude of its coefficients **after weighting each by** `r^degree`. At least one normalized coefficient has nonzero residue; the residue expression `f(z)+√(1−Σz_j²)g(z)` cannot vanish, since `1−Σz_j²` is nonsquare in the residue rational-function field (a smooth squarefree nonconstant polynomial). Thus the representation is injective and has the same relative valuation as that maximum. Every nonzero old coefficient is outside `O` or a nonzero `F`-constant, and `r` exceeds all of `F`: positive degree forces the value outside `O`. The real-closure residue embedding extends inside `H` by §1 of the cited note. Locally, independently for each `q`, extend `e_q` by four coordinates with norm `e_q(s)`, integral whenever `e_q(s)` is integral. In particular zero causes no problem; there is **no genericity requirement at a mark**.

All evaluations are defined only on the stage **algebras**, not on their function fields; division by `h` at a mark would be invalid. Each fixed stage element has integral image at all but finitely many `q`: true for `F` by external factorization, and inductively true of a finite polynomial expression in the old elements and the new coordinates. There is *no* requirement of almost-everywhere unit image or even nonzero image.

**3. Pullback and floors.** Let `A*` be the union, `R₁={a∈A*: e_q(a)∈V_q for every q}`. Then `R₁∩O=R₀` by the PID intersection-of-DVRs test on `F`. Each `a∈A*` has a multiple in `R₁` by a nonzero old integer (here meaning an element of `R₀`, not necessarily standard), clearing its finitely many poles. Hence `A*=(R₀\{0})⁻¹R₁`. More precisely choose `m∈R₀` supported on the exceptional primes and clearing their poles. CRT finds `c∈R₀` matching `m e_q(a) mod q^{v_q(m)}` at those primes: the old quotient maps onto the DVR completion quotient by that power. Then `a−c/m∈R₁`, and the change is in `F⊂O`.

Every target `α∈rc(Frac R₁)` is in a finite-stage real closure, so has some `r'∈R₁` with `α−r'∈O` by those tasks and CRT. This is **not yet** approximation within a unit. Approximate its ordered residue in `H` by `k∈K` to within `1/4`; upstairs `|α−r'−k|<1/2`. Translate `r'` by `floor_R₀(k)` and adjust by at most one to floor `α`. This proves IOpen by the integer-part criterion. Endness and discreteness follow from `R₁∩O=R₀`. Positive elements there use old Lagrange; each other positive element of `R₁` was serviced and its local representations are integral at **all** places, hence give Lagrange in `R₁`.

For every `0<d∈R₀` the algebra element `h/d` has zero image everywhere, so belongs to `R₁`. All divisors of a positive old prime `p` are bounded by `p`. Also `(p)R₁` is exactly the kernel of reduction of `e_p` modulo the maximal ideal of `V_p`: division by `p` is allowed in `A*`, preserves integrality at all other marks, and is integral at `p` precisely for this kernel. Positive ES triples sorted `x≤y≤z` obey `x≤3p/4`, `y≤2px` and `z≤pxy` by clearing denominators and using discreteness. Endness therefore preserves failure at `p`. No general statement about external PID, universal NR/Dirichlet, or collection in `R₁` is needed.

**4. Attempts to iterate (negative).** There are distinct obstructions, not just a missing scheduling step.

* The chain of principal ideals `(h)⊊(h/2)⊊(h/4)⊊…` is strict by discreteness, so `R₁` is **never** an external PID. The local map at 2 is nonfaithful: all powers of 2 divide `h≠0`. A new faithful DVR at 2 cannot have all these properties.
* Even finite Bézout need not hold. Service `α=√h` first; no polynomial in `F[h]` is `O`-close by its leading power, so a free variable `Y` is adjoined with all marks zero. `I=(h,Y)` in `F[h,Y]` is proper and nonprincipal (coprime generators). Each later extension is faithfully flat: polynomial or free via the monic quadric normal form. Their union is faithfully flat as well. In particular `I` stays proper. More explicitly, `h` remains a prime nonunit at every stage: modulo `h` a polynomial step preserves the domain, and the monic quadric embeds in the generic quotient over its fraction field, a domain because its degree-two leading form has rank four even if the right side reduces to zero. The class of `Y` there remains nonzero by freeness. Hence the proper ideal `(h,Y)A*` cannot be principal. If `(h,Y)R₁` were principal, localization at `R₀\{0}` would make it principal in `A*`. Thus this valid successor is not Bézout; intersecting valuation rings **over the old base** gives no Bézout conclusion on the new one. (z74 Opus xreview fix: this is an existential statement about one admissible schedule — the one servicing `√h` first — not a claim that every schedule fails Bézout.)
* In fact the unmodified one-successor construction **still fails bounded common multiples at the cutoff `h`**, regardless of the first task. For each standard `j>0`, `h−j` is positive and less than `h`, and belongs to `R₁`. A nonzero `L∈A*` cannot be divisible by all `h−j`. At its finite stage, `L` is a finite linear combination in a free `F[h]`-basis: take successive products of the polynomial bases or the monic quadric normal-form bases, all containing 1. If `(h−j)|L` in the union it already divides `L` at that stage, by this same bases-with-1 property at later stages. Thus each coefficient in `F[h]` would vanish at infinitely many `j`, impossible. In fact **the cutoffs satisfying BCM in `R₁` are exactly the old ones**. For any new positive `B` and proposed `L≠0`, take a common finite stage `A` containing both. `B` is transcendental over `F` (it exceeds `F`, whereas ordered algebraic elements are `F`-bounded). Thus `Spec(A[1/L])→𝔸¹_F` via `B` is dominant and of finite type. Its constructible image contains a nonempty open by Chevalley's theorem, hence contains `j` for all but finitely many standard positive `j`. At any such fiber point, `B−j` vanishes and `L` does not: `B−j` cannot divide `L` in `A`, hence cannot divide it in the union by faithful flatness. But `0<B−j<B`. This does not assume that every fiber has an `F`-rational point. It also shows that the old cut is definable in this particular successor, not that it is elementary. (z74 Opus xreview fix: both arguments in this bullet use that every edge is free with a basis containing 1, which holds for the floor and sphere edges of this recipe, and also for the quotient-selection edges `A[X,Y,Z]/(dZ−1−X²−Y²)` of `LOGIC_BCM_HULL_AND_ODD.md` (free over `A[Y,Z]` with basis `1,X`). They do not apply to towers with Bézout row edges, whose fibre over `u=v=0` is empty, so that faithful flatness fails.)

**A precise possible replacement for PID (conditional only).** For a countable ordered base `D` with fraction field `E`, a single commutative landing ring `S` would suffice if `D⊂S` is torsion-free as a `D`-module, `S∩E=D` in `S_E=(D\\{0})⁻¹S`, `S_E=S+E`, and four squares represent **every** element of `S`. The last property gives field-denominator witnesses in `S_E` by multiplying by a square from `D`. Use one `E`-algebra evaluation to `S_E`, pull back `S`, translate by `E` using the sum property, and run exactly the relative order proof above. In particular Bezout itself is not needed. A canonical test candidate is `S=lim_m D/mD` over all positive nonzero moduli ordered by divisibility. For every `m`, `S/mS≅D/mD`: an element zero modulo `m` divides by `m` coherently by reading its residue modulo `mn` and cancelling `m` to get a residue modulo each `n`. The same cancellation shows torsion-freeness; `D` injects since a nonzero `x` is not divisible by `2x`. This proves the required intersection and sum properties. The unsupplied property is four-square surjectivity on **that inverse limit**. Old Lag supplies each finite quotient representation separately (choose a positive lift by ordered division), but not representations compatible at all levels; the quotients at nonstandard moduli need not be finite, so finite-branching compactness cannot be invoked. This isolates the iteration question even without insisting on DVRs.

The known collection end extension in `LOGIC_BEZOUT_COLLECTION.md` maintains **one** rational retraction; Lag would require that `h−1>0` map to a sum of squares at its negative landing `−1` in the ordered old field. The present independent local maps avoid precisely that contradiction but do not supply the next base with PID or CRT data. The concrete obstructions above are to these successor recipes, not an implication from IOpen + Lagrange + bounded common multiples to ES.
