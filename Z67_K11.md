## 1. Precise statements

* Family classes: the residue classes r mod m of the seven ET Prop 1.9 families (I-1, I-2, I-3, I-4; II-1, II-2,
  II-3), primitive (gcd(r, m) = 1). U := {π ∈ Ẑ^× : π lies in no family class} (closed). (Ẑ^×)² ⊆ U.
* K̄ := {π ∈ Ẑ^× : π ≡ 1 (mod 840), (π₁₁/11) = −1} (compact, open in Ẑ^×). Note: π ≡ 1 (mod 840) forces π_p to be a
  square in ℤ_p^× for p = 2, 3, 5, 7.
* **Conjecture K11 (LOGIC2 §5.6, UCOVER §5).** K̄ ∩ U ≠ ∅. Equivalently (LOGIC2 Thm D): K is not contained in a finite
  union of family classes (on its unit profiles); equivalently NOI ⊬ "ES at all large primes of K".
* **Refutation form.** K̄ ∩ U = ∅ ⟺ finitely many family classes cover K̄ (compactness). A finite covering is a
  finite, checkable certificate.
* Literature (research subagent, verified quotes in §7): Salez (arXiv:1406.6307 §3.1) filters cover 7, 8, 10 mod 11
  but not 2, 6; Tao (blog, 2011-07-07): "I do not know how to eliminate the 2, 6 mod 11 classes". No published
  statement either way on finite coverability of K.

## 2. Lemma R (reduction to smooth shadows; PROVED)

For a bound P ≥ 11 let B_P := {(π_p)_{p ≤ P} : π_p ∈ ℤ_p^×, π ≡ 1 (mod 840), (π₁₁/11) = −1} (the P-box of K̄) and call a
family class *P-smooth* if its modulus is P-smooth. Let X_P ⊆ B_P be the set of points in no P-smooth family class.

**Lemma R.** (a) K̄ ∩ U ≠ ∅ ⟺ X_P ≠ ∅ for every P. (b) X_P = ∅ ⟺ finitely many P-smooth classes cover B_P.

*Proof.* Membership of π in a P-smooth class depends only on (π_p)_{p ≤ P}. Hence π ∈ K̄ avoids all P-smooth classes
iff its P-projection lies in X_P, and every family class is P-smooth for P ≥ its largest prime factor. So
K̄ ∩ U = ∩_P {π ∈ K̄ : π_{≤P} ∈ X_P}, a decreasing intersection of closed subsets of the compact K̄ (each nonempty iff
X_P ≠ ∅, since components at p > P are unconstrained). By compactness it is nonempty iff each term is. (b) B_P is
compact and the classes are open. ∎

**Why this helps.** With *all* components at p ≤ P free (Haar measure on B_P), only P-smooth moduli occur, and their
measure series converges like a P-smooth Dirichlet series (∏_{p≤P}(1 − p^{−s})^{−1} < ∞ for every s > 0). In UCOVER's
single-prime setting (rigid 1 elsewhere) the same series needs ET's ΣI/ΣII counting, which is not explicit at ℓ = 11.
So each X_P ≠ ∅ is (if true with positive measure) certifiable by finite computation plus an explicit tail, and
X_P = ∅ for any single P would refute the conjecture by an explicit finite covering.

## 3. Pre-registered kill test (checkpoint 1)

* **K-kill (refutation).** If for some P ≤ 23 the P-smooth classes cover B_P (König tree over P-smooth levels dies),
  the conjecture is FALSE (finite covering; theorem). Expected: no (LOGIC2 open nodes grow).
* **Route-kill (proof route).** The smooth-shadow route dies if the explicit tail for P = 13 cannot be pushed below the
  surviving mass of B_13 with feasible computation (then not even X_13 ≠ ∅ is certifiable this way).
