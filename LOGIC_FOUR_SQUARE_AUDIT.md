**Result.** There exists a discretely ordered external PID satisfying open induction and Lagrange's four-square axiom, with an infinite prime `P≡1 mod 840` failing ES. The construction in §3 needs no adaptive choices, TS, NR, or norm-plane classification. The same sphere step is compatible with the finite-subtree construction of the joint PID tower. The argument proves nonprovability in **IOpen + Lagrange**, not in any bounded-arithmetic theory that also proves Lagrange, and says nothing against ES in ℕ.

## 1. The algebraic step

Let `A` be an ordered finite-type ℚ-domain with fraction field `F`. Its bounded elements are exactly ℚ. For a positive infinite `s∈A`, put `Q=Σ_{j=1}^4 X_j²`, `B=A[X₁,…,X₄]/(Q−s)`. All degrees below are in the **new** variables only.

* **Degree and descent.** The degree filtration has `gr B=A[X₁,…,X₄]/(Q)`: every relation is a multiple of `Q−s`, and its leading form is the leading form of the multiplier times `Q`. This graded ring is a domain. Indeed `Q` has rank four, whereas a product of two linear forms has quadratic rank at most two even over an algebraic closure (characteristic zero). Monicity in `X₄` embeds the quotient into its extension to `F`. Thus degree is additive on products, the degree-zero part is `A`, and minus degree extends to a valuation on `Frac(B)` trivial on `F` (this is the valuation lemma of `REVIEW_JOINT_NR_TS_OPUS.md` C2; z74 Opus xreview fix). In a reciprocal identity `Σ1/w_j=4/n` with `w_j∈B` positive and `n∈A` positive, every reciprocal of positive-degree denominator has **positive** valuation. Consequently `4/n−Σ_{deg w_j=0}1/w_j`, which belongs to `F`, must be zero. In the given ordering, a nonempty sum of omitted positive reciprocals cannot be zero. All denominators already belong to `A`. No order on the valuation residue field is used.
* **Prime preservation.** For every old prime element `r`, `B/r` embeds by monicity into the quadric algebra over `Frac(A/r)`. The same rank argument on leading terms gives geometric integrality there, **including when `r|s`**. Thus `r` remains a nonunit prime. The step is not restricted to irreducible `s`.
* **Factoriality.** Suppose `A` and `C=A[i]` are factorial with units ℚ* and ℚ(i)* respectively. Over `C`, change variables to `U=X₁+iX₂, V=X₁−iX₂, W=X₃+iX₄, Z=X₃−iX₄`. Then `E=B[i]` has equation `UV=s−WZ`. The quotient by `U` is a domain: over `Frac(C)` the equation `WZ=s` defines a torus since `s≠0`; over `C` the same algebra embeds in `C[W,W⁻¹]` via `Z=s/W`, by reducing mixed monomials. Thus `U` is prime. `E[1/U]=C[U,U⁻¹,W,Z]` is factorial; Nagata gives `E` factorial. Its units are constants (use the exponent of `U` in the localized unit, or degree additivity over `C`). Now `B=E^conj` is normal. Each height-one prime divisor of `B` pulls back under the finite étale Gaussian extension to the sum of divisors above it, principal in `E`. If `f` generates that divisor, `f̄/f` is a constant of norm one; Hilbert 90 rescales `f` by a Gaussian rational to a fixed generator. Injectivity of divisor pullback gives principality downstairs, as in `LOGIC_CIRCLE_AUDIT.md` §1. Units downstairs are ℚ*. No smoothness of the total stage is required. *Output invariant (z74 Opus xreview fix):* the step returns both `B` factorial with units `ℚ*` **and** `B[i]=E` factorial with units `ℚ(i)*`; the latter is the hypothesis of the next sphere step.
* **Order and boundedness.** Let `r=√s` in `F^rc`. Choose three bounded `z_j` in `(1/8,1/4)` with natural residues `ζ_j` algebraically independent over the residue field `k` of `F^rc` (the stages are countable). Substitute `X_j=r z_j` for `j≤3` and `X₄=r√(1−Σz_j²)`. Each nonzero normal form is `f(X₁,X₂,X₃)+X₄ g(X₁,X₂,X₃)`. Divide its substituted expression by the maximum `M` of `|a|r^d` over its nonzero monomials of total degree `d` and coefficient `a`. The residue is `f₀(ζ)+√(1−Σζ_j²) g₀(ζ)`, with not both residue polynomials zero. It cannot vanish: `1−ΣZ_j²` is irreducible by the rank-three leading form and is nonsquare in `k(Z₁,Z₂,Z₃)`. This proves injectivity and shows the substituted magnitude has the valuation of `M`. For any nonconstant normal form `M` is infinite, since a nonzero old coefficient is noninfinitesimal. Thus bounded elements remain ℚ. **Only positive infinite elements are scheduled by this operation.** A positive rational radius would not support this order argument.

## 2. Local lifting and finite prefixes

Fix a field embedding of the countable old field into `ℚ_l`. For **every** `t∈ℤ_l` the sphere of value `t` has an integral point: the image of `ℤ_l⁴` under `Q` is compact and contains the dense set of nonnegative ordinary integers by the classical four-square theorem. This covers 2 and arbitrary positive valuations. For a nonintegral `t` multiply by `l^(2a)` to obtain an integral value and scale the representing coordinates back by `l^(−a)`. They cannot all be integral in this case. No notion of local positivity is involved.

For `t=φ_l(s)` we have **`t≠0`** by injectivity. Every point of this local sphere is therefore smooth over `ℚ_l`. Around an integral point, restrict to an open neighborhood inside `ℤ_l⁴`. Some partial derivative `2X_j` is nonzero **in the field**, not necessarily a unit in `ℤ_l`. The local implicit function theorem gives an open three-parameter chart; choose the three free coordinates there algebraically independent over the countable old image. This induces a field embedding of `Frac(B)` extending the old one: the remaining coordinate satisfies its monic quadratic, irreducible over the other three indeterminates by the geometric integrality above. At a nonintegral value use the same argument without the integrality neighborhood. This proves the required conditional lift for *every* old embedding, not just for almost every prime.

For finite-prefix avoidance, over the generic point the sphere is smooth and geometrically integral, and remains so after field extension. One may shrink to a dense smooth open above `s≠0` in characteristic zero. In the finite-subtree PID method, products of sibling fibres over their common generic parent therefore remain geometrically integral and dominate each factor, exactly as for a nonzero circle. Nonzero functions required on individual nodes stay nonzero on the joint horizontal open. For any **fixed finite** subtree and finite lists, spread this open away from finitely many primes and apply Lang–Weil, then smooth lifting, avoiding the listed zeros and poles on reduction. At these prefix primes `s` can be required a unit. At the other primes and on the tail, it can have arbitrary valuation, and the preceding local argument applies. An unconditional sphere task creates no new take/skip choice; no restriction is placed on all future `s` at a fixed residue field.

## 3. Direct consequence, without an adaptive tree

Here are details isolating the nonprovability claim from other proposed model refinements. Start with `A₀=ℚ[P]`, `P` positive infinite. Use the polynomial floor and coprime row tasks of `Z73_PID_SQUARE.md` §§1–2, and dovetail the sphere task for **every positive infinite element of every stage**, not just prime elements or selected associates. New positive infinite elements are put on the lists in turn. Floor steps require only an ordered countable field and bounded old elements exactly ℚ. Row steps use coprimality in the UFD, polynomial charts and their intersection; a coprime rational pair stays coprime after finite étale Gaussian extension since height-one primes contract to height-one primes. Thus both factorialities, constant units, permanent old rational primes and bounded elements are maintained. Positive ES triples at old targets descend through floor and row steps by the polynomial-line argument (`LOGIC_ES_RIGIDITY_NR.md` §1). The new step satisfies all these invariants by §1 above. At the union `A∞` each pair has a principal ideal with Bézout coefficients and each nonunit has a finite permanent prime factorization; every element of the ordered real closure is within standard bounded distance of `A∞` by the floor schedule.

Choose embeddings of the union field into all `ℚ_l` with `P` a square unit, `P≡1 mod 840` at the relevant places, and every nonzero field element a unit almost everywhere. Additionally at **every** sphere step take all four coordinates integral at a place whenever the target there is integral. To construct them, exhaust the chain and its nonzero rational functions by finite prefixes/lists. At a prefix adjoin `t²=P`, `t≠0`, at the base (a geometrically integral punctured line). Pull up the tower, restricting to successive dense opens on which all generically smooth line/quadric fibres are geometrically integral; each stage dominates its original stage. Remove the listed zeros and poles. Set `R={a∈A∞: φ_l(a)∈ℤ_l for every l}`. The algebraic PID descent of Z73 §2 uses only the just-established finite-support valuations, not rationality of the tower. Explicitly, rational translation clears local fractional parts; bounded elements of `R` are exactly ℤ. Consequently bounded-distance approximation gives floors in `R`, hence IOpen. Clearing finite denominator support gives `Frac(R)=Frac(A∞)`. Rational scaling gives each `A∞`-prime valuation zero everywhere, hence prime in `R`; factoring leaves a scalar in ℤ and gives atomicity. An `R`-coprime pair generates the unit ideal in `A∞` and has a local unit entry everywhere. Correct its `A∞` Bézout coefficients by a rational multiple of the syzygy using CRT at the finitely many bad places, obtaining integral coefficients everywhere. This gives Bézout and hence, by atomicity, an external PID. In particular `P` itself is a positive nonunit prime in `R` with the stated congruence.

For each positive infinite `s∈R` its scheduled coordinates belong to `A∞` and at **every** place are integral by the rule. They therefore belong to `R`. Finite nonnegative elements are ordinary integers and use the classical representations; signs of coordinates can be replaced by absolute values. This proves the universal Lagrange axiom in the model.
