1. **MAJOR — §6 Corollary B1/readings; §0 Q1; §§13–15: disjoint bands do not prove the claimed no-go.** Theorem B localizes particular continuous interpolations with particular coefficients. It does not show that Poisson/Voronoi, or every “band-limited reduction,” preserves those bands. Indeed (ii) explicitly permits arbitrary bounded κ_m: choose κ_m = 1[D_m > 0]Ψ(w_m/W)e(hw_m). Then the *same* T_W(h) has the low-band expansion of (ii), as well as the high-band expansion of (iii); the difficulty has moved into κ_m. Also e((ν+m)θ_m) = e(νθ_m) on the actual remainder grid. **Fix:** retain the localization theorem, but replace every intrinsic separation/non-implication/no-reduction claim by “these particular expansions have different carrier bands; no implication is established.” Delete the assertion that logical non-implication between possibly true statements cannot be proved.

2. **MAJOR — §0(1), §4 Reading, §§13–15, especially proposed FRONTIER §6: averaged TD is not free.** Prop A bounds a nonnegative cycle count, not |T(h)|, its moments, or its average over primes. TD ⇒ R2 does not run backwards, averaged or otherwise. Nor does an integer-window estimate automatically give a prime-normalized estimate in every short prime window. The reweighting identity over U does not prove that averaging U can never help a pointwise argument. **Fix:** state only the proved integer averages and exceptional-prime count. Delete “every ... form ... worthless,” “R2/TD on average over p (free),” and the proposed closure of those methods. Averaged TD remains unproved here.

3. **MAJOR — §6 Setting (“recovers T(h)”), §0(2–3), Theorem H(3–4): smoothing/localization is not a theorem about sharp TD or CNT.** A partition in w cannot restore the s- and m-boundaries removed by fixed compactly supported Φ and φ. For full TD, supply boundary handling and extend the W argument down to U^{(1−η)/2}. For the N₂ route instead, explicitly use BILINDIV Remark 4.1's cycle-only free range and a separate smooth sufficient hypothesis, not equivalence with TD.
