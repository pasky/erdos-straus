# Literature priority audit A: universal sieve-limit claim

**Scope.** This extends `reviews/theta-lit-notes.md`, rather than repeating its source-by-source summaries. I checked the new priority targets and accessed the full Granville–Koukoulopoulos–Maynard (GKM) preprint noted below. I could not obtain full text for several paywalled/book sources; these are explicitly marked *metadata only* and are not treated as theorem-level evidence. Access date for this audit: 2026-10-04. This is a priority search, not an exhaustive proof of absence.

## Closest direct match accessed: GKM sieve weights

Andrew Granville, Dimitris Koukoulopoulos, and James Maynard, “Sieve weights and their smoothings,” *Annales scientifiques de l’École normale supérieure* 54 (2021), 997–1041; arXiv:1606.06781v4. Full text: archived PDF and extracted text listed at end.

- Introduction, pp. 2–3 (PDF pp. 3–4), equation (1.1): Selberg’s pointwise square majorant for the sifted indicator is summed to obtain a quadratic form in divisor coefficients; the best bound “stemming from this method” is obtained by minimizing that form subject to λ₁=1 and divisor cutoff. They describe the usual smoothed Selberg weights λ_d=μ(d)f(log d/log R).
- Theorem 1.3, pp. 8–9 (PDF pp. 9–10): for fixed integers k≥1 and A≥0, the 2kth moment of the particular smoothed Möbius divisor sum M_{f_A}(n;R) has asymptotic exponent E_{k,A}=max{binom(2k,k)−2k(A+1),−1}, with positive leading constant. Equation (1.15) gives a corresponding sharp-cutoff interval-divisor sum moment when R^{2k}≤x. The theorem concerns moments / concentration behavior of a specified Selberg-style family, not a universal bound for every pointwise-nonnegative majorant.
- Theorem 1.4, p. 9 (PDF p. 10): smoothness threshold conditions for those Selberg-style weights to have sieve-like even moments. Crucially, Remark 1.4 immediately after Theorem 1.3 says they consider only Selberg-style weights and merely *expect* something analogous for combinatorial (β-sieve) weights; they do not prove it.
- GKM’s opening description (pp. 1–3) concerns arithmetic averages over integers and the effect of smoothing, not arbitrary independent Bernoulli coordinates or arbitrary prime-slice densities. There is no Ψ(λ)=inf_α(αλ+Σp_i e^{-αs_i}) lower bound for all Boolean majorants. The paper is a meaningful adjacent result about limits/behavior of sieve weights but is not the campaign theorem.

## Sieve optimality / classical limitations

- Atle Selberg, “Sieve methods,” *Proc. Sympos. Pure Math.* 20 (1971), 1–42, DOI 10.1090/pspum/020/0567686. **Metadata only:** I verified bibliographic record/DOI, but could not access the chapter text here. Thus I cannot responsibly attribute an exact proposition, page, dual program, or large-κ optimality statement to it. The known Selberg Λ² optimization is a quadratic-form optimization over divisor-supported coefficients; that setup is narrower than arbitrary Boolean nonnegative majorants.
- Selberg, “Lectures on sieves,” *Collected Papers*, vol. II (1991). **Metadata only;** no text obtained, so no theorem/page-level priority claim. Same limitation applies to any alleged LP dual or assertion that all level-D sieves cannot beat a specified V(D^{c/κ}) bound.
- John B. Friedlander and Henryk Iwaniec, *Opera de Cribro*, AMS Colloquium Publications 57 (2010). AMS bibliographic/book contents metadata identifies Ch. 6 as fundamental lemma material, Ch. 7 as “Selberg’s sieve—Kvadrater er positive,” and later chapters as beta-sieve/parity/limitations material. **Book text not accessed in this audit.** Chapter headings do not establish a theorem about every level-bounded nonnegative function. I have not verified a Selberg optimality theorem, LP dual, or matching Rankin-functional inequality in those chapters.
- Iwaniec, “Rosser’s sieve,” *Acta Arith.* 36 (1980), 171–202; Ankeny–Onishi (1964); Diamond–Halberstam–Richert and the β_κ sifting-limit literature. **Not accessed at theorem level in this audit.** These are classical results about structured Rosser/combinatorial sieve sequences, sieve dimensions and sifting limits, not verified evidence for arbitrary weighted Boolean majorants. No precise β_κ bound is asserted here.
- Friedlander–Iwaniec, “On Bombieri’s asymptotic sieve” (1978), and Bombieri, “The asymptotic sieve” (1976): **not accessed**. Asymptotic sieve hypotheses/conclusions concern distribution data in arithmetic sequences; no universal Boolean-majorant result verified.
- The earlier note `reviews/theta-lit-notes.md` records a verified classical Selberg main-term formula via “Selberg’s sieve of irregular density,” arXiv:2206.03479, §1, equations (1.6)–(1.10), including the truncated Selberg denominator J and its comparison with V(z). That is a lower bound on a specific truncated quadratic-form denominator, and the cited source discusses extra dimension regularity for asymptotics; it is not the asserted extremal inequality. I did not re-download that source in this pass, so refer to the prior note for its archived/source record rather than treating it as newly rechecked here.

## Discrete moment problems and LP

- András Prékopa, “Boole-Bonferroni Inequalities and Linear Programming,” *Operations Research* 36(1) (1988), 145–162, DOI 10.1287/opre.36.1.145. **Metadata only:** publisher bibliographic record verified; full article not obtained. Its subject clearly concerns LP formulations of Bonferroni bounds, but I cannot verify which lower/index sets, dual bases, distributions, or interpolation formulas occur, and therefore cannot claim that it contains the campaign theorem.
- András Prékopa, “The discrete moment problem and linear programming,” *Discrete Applied Mathematics* 27 (1990), 235–254, DOI 10.1016/0166-218X(90)90068-N. **Metadata only:** an open-access listing points to the ScienceDirect PDF, but the endpoint returned HTTP 403. Exact theorem/page content was not read; do not infer that its moment constraints are the weighted lower set Σj_gs_g≤λ or that it gives the desired binomial-product certificate.
- András Prékopa and Zsuzsanna Mádi-Nagy, “On Multivariate Discrete Moment Problems and Their Applications to Bounding Expectations and Probabilities,” *Mathematics of Operations Research* 29(2) (2004), 229–242, DOI 10.1287/moor.1030.0064. **Metadata only:** no full text obtained. In particular, the proposed use of lower-set moment indices, independent binomial/product laws, and Lagrange/divided-difference dual bases remains unverified from the primary article. This is the closest apparent general LP/moment-problem framework among targets, but the exact relationship cannot be settled from metadata.
- Boros–Prékopa (1989), and Galambos–Simonelli, *Bonferroni-type inequalities with applications* (1996): **not accessed** in this audit; no theorem-number/page claim. Such Bonferroni work is adjacent if it optimizes probability bounds from specified intersection moments, but that alone is not a theorem about every weighted-level nonnegative polynomial under arbitrary Bernoulli biases.

**Assessment of this cluster:** LP duality is an elementary general framework for moment extremization, and the proposed interpolation certificate is a plausible finite-dimensional dual certificate. But without a primary-source theorem matching the same moment index family, positivity constraint, evaluation functional, and quantitative bound, one cannot cite “discrete moment problem” as already proving the campaign claim. Whether a special case is implicit in these papers remains open from my access.

## Approximate inclusion–exclusion and positive approximation

- Nathan Linial and Nati Nisan, “Approximate inclusion-exclusion,” *Combinatorica* 10 (1990), 349–365; Jeff Kahn, Nati Linial, and Alex Samorodnitsky, “Inclusion-exclusion: exact and approximate,” *Combinatorica* 16 (1996), 465–477, DOI 10.1007/BF01271266. **Metadata / secondary descriptions only; full papers not accessed here.** These concern estimating union probabilities from low-order intersection information (and related extremal examples). They are naturally symmetric/unweighted, fixed-order settings, not verified arbitrary weighted lower-set constraints with variable Bernoulli parameters.
- Alexander Sherstov, “Approximate Inclusion-Exclusion for Arbitrary Symmetric Functions,” ECCC TR07-116 (2007/2008). **Not accessed in full here.** The scope indicated by the title is symmetric Boolean approximation; no arbitrary-bias nonnegative-majorant expectation theorem verified.
- Approximate degree and dual-polynomial results (including Bun–Thaler work) give degree lower bounds for approximation of Boolean functions, often via signed dual witnesses. The campaign asks for a one-sided *nonnegative* low-support polynomial extremal expectation under product measure. I found no directly matching theorem text in this audit. The relation to biased OR/AND approximation and Krawtchouk/Christoffel extremal quantities is a useful search lead, not an established equivalence.

## Large sieve, smooth weights, Rankin bounds

- GKM’s Theorems 1.3–1.4 above are the most relevant accessed statement on optimality-like limitations, but only within a specific Selberg-style family and for arithmetic moment statistics.
- The prior `theta-lit-notes.md` gives a Montgomery large-sieve denominator formula and verified citation to Croot–Elsholtz, “On variants of the larger sieve,” §1, Theorems 1–2. That is an arithmetic upper bound involving a truncated squarefree sum, not a lower-bound certificate against every sieve majorant. Green, “Inverse questions for the large sieve,” *GAFA* 24 (2014), 163–176, DOI 10.1007/s00039-014-0288-1, was located bibliographically, but not inspected here; inverse-large-sieve questions are not the same function class.
- Rankin’s trick / saddle-point estimates for actual sifted-set counts (Hildebrand–Tenenbaum and standard multiplicative-number-theory treatments) estimate a particular set or Euler product. Even a sharp asymptotic for the true avoider probability would not by itself prove a lower bound for every level-D majorant unless that majorant is shown to be no cheaper than the indicator under the same product measure. No primary source was accessed that establishes the campaign’s Ψ(λ) lower bound for arbitrary prime-slice densities.

## Conclusions about priority (based on accessed material)

1. **Abstract principle:** general LP duality and discrete moment problems are unquestionably the right conceptual setting; the exact “every nonnegative level-D majorant” statement is not verified in any source I could access. The Prékopa / Mádi-Nagy–Prékopa papers are important closest leads, but their precise applicability is unresolved because their full texts were unavailable. Sparse-grid/combination interpolation, as discussed in the prior note, supplies an algebraic mechanism rather than the quantitative positive certificate by itself.
2. **Rankin-functional form for arbitrary prime-slice densities:** no located/accessed theorem establishes log(1/Ef) ≲ inf_α[αλ+Σ p_i e^{-αs_i}] uniformly for arbitrary p_i≤1/4 and all globally nonnegative weighted-level majorants. Existing sieve and Rankin results concern specific arithmetic weights, regular-dimension hypotheses, or actual sifted counts; those are materially different.
3. **Specific 3/4 cap application:** none of the accessed literature establishes that the Erdős–Straus CRT exceptional-set exponent 3/4 cannot be improved by *any* CRT-majorant sieve (as opposed to a particular classical sieve family). I found no directly matching source; this is an absence-of-match assessment, not proof of novelty.

Overall, the strongest close match is GKM’s precise analysis of moments and smoothing for Selberg-style arithmetic weights, which differs in both the admissible family and the conclusion. Exact priority remains particularly uncertain until the cited Prékopa and Mádi-Nagy–Prékopa primary texts, Selberg’s lectures, and relevant chapters of *Opera de Cribro* can be checked directly.
