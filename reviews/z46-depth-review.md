## 1. Brauer twists

### Group computation: accepted, with its stated hypotheses

The second compactification is a useful independent check on the cubic picture. Blowing up the two transverse points `(r,−r), (−r,r)`, `r²=−k`, gives geometric Picard rank four; deleting the three independent boundary classes leaves the sign lattice generated by `E₊`, with `E₋=−E₊`. This yields `H¹(Q,Z_sign)=Z/2`, not zero. β is genuinely nonconstant: its residue along the deleted hyperbola is the nontrivial character of `Q(√−k)`.

For the full group, restriction to `A²\{AB=k}` and the residue injection modulo constants reduce everything to `χ+ε(A)` on `G_m`. The arithmetic geometric-invariant part is only order two. On an exceptional divisor the condition is `χ|_L+ε(r)=0`. The possible order-four lift is correctly excluded: `2χ=η` contradicts evaluation at complex conjugation, because `L` is imaginary. This is an essential use of **k>0**, not a calculation claimed for negative twists.

For ε=1 and quadratic χ, writing `(a+br)²=c₀r` forces `a²=kb²`, hence k rational-square. Conversely `2r/s=(1±i)²` gives γ when `k=s²`. Its nonzero geometric residue separates it from β; both are order two. Thus:

- algebraic quotient: always `Z/2` for positive k;
- full quotient: `Z/2`, or `(Z/2)²` exactly for square k;
- only twist class `[n/m]=[mn]` has the extra class.

The affine-modification residues also account for the exceptional divisors; deleting the smooth origin is codimension two and does not change Br. No missing order-four extension or extra arithmetic character emerged from the audit.

### Is flipping the real lift legitimate? Yes

The potentially dangerous distinction is between **positive covering coordinates** and **positive image coordinates**. The definition imposes only the latter. If `d>0`, both `(A,B,C)` and its negative have exactly the same `(dBC,dAC,dAB)`. Thus both are permitted over the same positive real image. Changing one real component preserves adelicity and every finite-place image condition; it is not an approximation or a change to a rational point.

For positive image, each denominator exceeds `n/m`, so `1−AB/k=1−mz/n<0`. Consequently `inv∞β=1/2`. The identities `q*α=β` and `τ*γ−γ=β` then make the real sign change toggle precisely the remaining pairing. One can check the first identity starting with `α=(-C/A,-C/B)`: `(v,−v)=0` converts it to `(-B/A,-C/A)`, after which the displayed Steinberg calculation applies. No positivity sleight of hand is present.

The resulting equality is **pointwise in the adelic image**, not just a nonemptiness statement. Geometric simple connectedness upstairs makes its finite torsors geometrically constant; twisting splits these, and functoriality gives the full finite étale–Brauer comparison. This justifies closing this particular hierarchy.

**Scope to retain:** `X=U\{0}`, its usual restricted-product adeles, finite-place integrality of the image, and positivity only of the real image. Finite-place axes are allowed. Neither a smaller integral cover model, a coordinate-nonzero open subset, arbitrary new branch divisors, nor log/positive-dimensional descent is computed. “All integral obstructions are exhausted” would be false calibration.
