### tropical_newton.md

**Verdict-validity: PARTIAL.** The individual Newton-polygon limitation is genuine, but the global B1/B2 dismissal exceeds the cited theorems. I re-derived T1's difficult case directly: if the two p-divisible denominators have valuation at least 2, then x>p/4 implies x≥(p+1)/4, while y,z≥p²; hence Σ1/u_i≤4/(p+1)+2/p²<4/p, a contradiction. T2 is also correct as an injectivity statement: all labelled finite valuations reconstruct each positive rational number, so they determine its residues too. Consequently the claim that valuation data can *never* recover cofactor residue classes is valid for an individual local Newton polygon, not the complete adelic collection. Neither POINTWISE_SIZE's finite formal-run hypothesis nor redteam-automorphic-2 Thm 2.2(iii)'s fixed Θ₀ hypothesis follows for a growing-support adelic certificate. Calling its lift the original problem identifies the missing theorem; it does not disprove such a theorem. **Strongest open sub-question:** can joint valuation constraints force, at each prime p, some p/4<x≤3p/4 and positive δδ′=p²x² with δ≡δ′≡−px (mod 4x−p), as x varies through that growing interval? This is the precise integral lift, not separate local solvability. **By-product novelty:** T1's prime-adic patterns are probably folklore, already implicit in ET parametrisation; T2 is elementary unique factorisation, not new arithmetic information. **Promise: 1/5.**
