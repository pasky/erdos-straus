### tech_maynard_theta.md

**Verdict-validity: DISAGREE** with the claimed structural collapse, not with “no working transfer.” Lemma 1.1 follows by multiplying p+A by 4C; independent enumeration verifies both dual forms for 2≤n≤150 and vanishing at odd squares. But §2 misquotes [Maynard, Hypothesis 1/Theorem 3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/1405.2593#S3.Thmthrm1): the paper explicitly permits sparse ambient sets without the asserted |𝒜(x)|≥x/(log x)^O(1) hypothesis. Its distribution/nonconcentration assumptions remain substantial; this correction does not establish them for ES. In §3, available power-scale error *upper bounds* are promoted to a universal error lower bound. `CONDITIONAL_ES.md` Proposition 2.3 constrains bounded-multiplicity detectors’ main terms on almost all primes, not every weighted method’s accuracy. Remark 1.2 establishes only that positivity suffices when m=1; it does not establish that optimizing weights cannot make positivity provable. **Strongest open sub-question:** construct weights on a growing, full Type I∪II family giving a square-sensitive main term ≥1 and a uniform remainder below half that main term, without assumed unweighted positivity. **By-product novelty:** Lemma 1.1 is correct folklore; m=1 positivity is elementary, not a weights-impossibility theorem. §2 also misquotes ET Theorem 1.1: Σ_{p≤N}f_II(p)≍N log²N, not N log³N (the latter sums over integers). The block-law evidence does not verify its constant: the stated unrestricted C-cutoff gives log(p/H), whereas log(p/H²) needs an additional B≥A restriction absent from the experiment. **Promise: 2/5.**
