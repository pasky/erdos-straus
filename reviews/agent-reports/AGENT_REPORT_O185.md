## SHARP
1. **Exact dictionary (PROVED): fixed-slice coverability of an exponential family ⟺ an exponential local–global principle.**
   For n_m = k·2^m + 1: a finite covering of all large m by ET classes + divisor classes exists iff the limit set Λ_k ⊂ Ẑ avoids the
   sterile set; the square part of that condition is exactly a Skolem / Bertók–Hajdu-type Hasse principle (EH_k) for the mixed equation
   k·2^x + 1 = (unit)² (EXPONENT_COVERING Thm 1.4, Prop 2.2, §3).
2. **Why B1 disappears (PROVED, structural):** B1's square-mimicking point exists because the parameter space of a polynomial/progression
   family is Ẑ = Π_q Z_q, a product over target primes, so local squares glue (Prop 2.1). An exponent μ ∈ Ẑ couples the primes through
   ord_q(2), and local squares need not glue: a Sierpiński covering by quadratic characters. **B1 is the S → all-primes limit of an S-unit
   obstruction** (§5.1): for {k·u + 1 : u ∈ ⟨S⟩}, the integer obstruction points are the finitely many S-unit solutions of k·u + 1 = v².
3. **Certified infinite ES families inside Mordell's hardest classes (PROVED, computer-assisted, independent checker):** ES holds for every
   n = k·2^m + 1 (all m ≥ 0) for k ∈ {9, 21, 27, 57, 171, 231, 315, 393, 495, 15015}. In particular **all 315·2^m + 1 (≡ 1 mod 2520)** and
   **all 15015·2^m + 1 (≡ 1 mod 120120, i.e. squares mod every prime ≤ 13)**. 27·2^m + 1 (6 classes, L = 12) is ≡ 529 (mod 840) for m ≡ 2 (12)
   and contains the primes 109, 27·2²⁶+1, 27·2⁵⁰+1. No prior "ES for all n of the form k·2^m + 1" was found (web check, §5.4).
4. **Exact negative (PROVED):** the only integer obstruction points for k·2^m + 1 are k = 4^j − 1 at μ = 0 (Cor 1.5). So **ES for all Proth
   numbers 3·2^m + 1 cannot be proved by any finite set of ET/divisor classes**: along m ≡ 0 (mod L), n ≡ 4 (mod 2^L − 1) mimics 2².

## Honest assessment
* The novelty is in the form and in the obstruction theory, not in new identities: each certified family lies in a finite union of
  ES-solvable congruence classes (the coverings use moduli with primes 5, 7, 13, 17, 31, 41, 43, 71, 151, 38737, …).
* Families are coverable exactly when their members are forced to be non-squares (or divisible) at a fixed finite prime set. The hard
  core of ES — n a unit square modulo everything small — is untouched. B1 is not violated; its hypothesis is absent.
* Points for the reviewer: (a) Thm 1.2's "ET classes avoid odd squares" is cited at class level (Schinzel–Yamamoto reciprocity behind
  ET Prop 1.6; ET's f_I/f_II carry coprimality conditions, so I did not apply Prop 1.6 verbatim) plus computational control; the
  coverings themselves do not depend on it. (b) §5.1's "Thm 1.4 transfers" for several exponents is sketched, not written out.
  (c) The Chebotarev/Kummer step that X₂-type neighbours of an integer point contain no unit-square point is only sketched (§4.3 states
  the PROVED part: the values 289 / −287 at levels ≤ 2 / 3).
