**D1 (MINOR, wording/citation; §0 bullet 1, Thm 1.1(3) gloss, AGENT_REPORT SHARP 1, §5(i)).** "is φ(q)^{−1}(1 + inv_∞inv_p) — the
Borovoi–Rudnick BM factor". Checked BR (Invent. Math. 119 (1995) 37–66, author's PDF at math.tau.ac.il/~borovoi/papers/hardy.pdf,
Thm 0.6 and Def. of relatively Hardy–Littlewood): BR's density δ(x) ∈ {0, |C(H)|} is a locally constant function on
X(𝔸) (Kottwitz invariant), proved for affine homogeneous spaces of semisimple groups with an asymptotic count as the height
T → ∞. U_n is a log K3 surface, not a homogeneous space, and here n is fixed with finitely many integral points; no
BR-type theorem exists for it (BL Thm 1.1 even says there is no BM obstruction to existence). So the factor has the
*shape* of BR's density 1 + Σ_{β}∏_v inv_v β evaluated at an integral adelic point; it is not "the BR factor".
Repair: say "of Borovoi–Rudnick shape (BR Thm 0.6 is for homogeneous spaces of semisimple groups and does not apply to
U_n)" everywhere the phrase "the Borovoi–Rudnick factor" occurs (§0, §5(i), SHARP 1). The §1 bullet already contains the
correct caveat; the summary lines should not drop it.

**D2 (MINOR, novelty; §0, §5 "New and PROVED (i)").** The arithmetic content of Thm 1.1 — χ_{−4uB}(−(u+B)) = −1, hence
Type II hits force (−uB/p) = −1 and no hits at squares — is classical: it is Yamamoto's quadratic-residue condition
(Yamamoto 1965, Mordell Ch. 30), which BL Cor 1.3 explicitly says it "unifies", and it is the mechanism of Elsholtz–Tao
Prop 1.6 (§4 of 1107.1010 — Jacobi-symbol argument). What is new is only the bookkeeping remark that, in the
orthogonality expansion over χ mod 4uB, this is the single character χ_{−4uB} (and that O260's "genus = BM" was
wrong). Repair: label Thm 1.1(1)–(2),(4) "PROVED (classical: Yamamoto; BL Cor 1.3; ET Prop 1.6 mechanism)" and claim
novelty only for the expansion/bookkeeping statement (3) and the refutation of O260's strong form.
