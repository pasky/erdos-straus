                                                             ARITHMETIC PROGRESSIONS

                                                   Abstract. We prove a version of the prime number theorem for arithmetic progressions
                                                   that is uniform enough to deduce the Siegel–Walfisz theorem, Hoheisel’s asymptotic for in-
                                                   tervals of length x1−δ , a Brun–Titchmarsh bound, and Linnik’s bound on the least prime in
                                                   an arithmetic progression as corollaries. Our proof uses the Vinogradov–Korobov zero-free
                                                   region, a log-free zero density estimate, and the Deuring–Heilbronn zero repulsion phenom-
                                                   enon. Improvements exist when the modulus is sufficiently powerful.
