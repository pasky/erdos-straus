Theorem 1. Fix ε>0. Then for p large there is a Γ orbit C(p) in X*(p) for which |X*(p)\C(p)|≤p^ε (note that |X*(p)|∼p²), and any Γ orbit D(p) satisfies |D(p)|≫(log p)^(1/3).
Theorem 2. Let E be the set of primes for which the strong approximation conjecture fails. For ε>0, the number of primes p≤T with p∈E is at most T^ε, for T large.
