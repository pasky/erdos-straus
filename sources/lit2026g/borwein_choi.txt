Abstract: “We show that there are at most 19 integers that are not of the form xy+yz+xz with x,y,z≥1. Eighteen of them are small and easily found. The remaining possibility must be greater than 10^11 and cannot occur if we assume the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis.”
