Theorem 1. Suppose d is a fundamental discriminant. Then for some δ > 0 depending only on Ω
(i) #A_d∩Ω/#A_d = μ(Ω) + O(|d|^{-δ}) as d → −∞ and
(ii) Σ_{C∈A_d} |C∩Ω|/Σ_{C∈A_d}|C| = μ(Ω) + O(d^{-δ}) as d → +∞.
The implied constants and δ are ineffective.
