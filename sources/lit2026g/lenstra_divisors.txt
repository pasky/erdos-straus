H. W. Lenstra, Jr., “Divisors in Residue Classes,” Mathematics of Computation 42 (1984), 331–340.

Theorem statement (JSTOR/source search excerpt): “Let r, s and n be integers satisfying 0 < r < s < n, s > n^{1/3}, gcd(r,s)=1. Then there exist at most 11 positive divisors of n that are congruent to r modulo s, and there is a polynomial algorithm for determining all these divisors.”
