Theorem 1.2.3 (Bourgain, Gamburd, Sarnak [BGS16a]). The following are true.
(a) Let Ebgs denote the set of primes for which Γ does not act transitively on X∗ (p). For any ǫ > 0, we have
#{p ∈ Ebgs | p ≤ x} = O(xǫ )
(b) Let C(p) be the largest orbit of Γ on X∗ (p). Then for any ǫ > 0, we have
|X∗ (p) − C(p)| ≤ pǫ
Theorem 1.2.5 (See Theorem 5.5.4). For every p ≥ 3, every Out+ (Π)-orbit on Epi(Π, SL2 (Fp ))−2 / GL2 (Fp )
has cardinality divisible by p. In particular, every Γ-orbit on X∗ (p) has cardinality divisible by p.

In particular, every Γ-orbit must have size at least p, so combined with Theorem 1.2.3(b), this establishes
