Loughran, Daniel; Mitankin, Vladimir. “Integral Hasse principle and strong approximation for Markoff surfaces.” arXiv:1807.10223v3, 29 Apr 2019; IMRN 2021, no. 18, 14086–14122, DOI 10.1093/imrn/rnz095. Source https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.10223 (accessed 2026-10-30). Theorems 1.1–1.5: for Um: x²+y²+z²−xyz=m, almost all m fail weak/strong approximation; BM obstruction to integral Hasse principle only when squareclass m−4 lies in <±1,2,3,5>; count of BM Hasse failures ≍B^{1/2}/sqrt(log B) for |m|≤B; ≫B^{1/2}/log B locally soluble m with no BM obstruction but no integral points. Explicit Br generators stated as quaternion algebras for general m. No positivity restriction. Not ES fibres. Verdict: k≠0 ES yes/no: no; positivity-constrained BM: no.

Ghosh, Amit; Sarnak, Peter. “Integral points on Markoff type cubic surfaces.” arXiv:1706.06712v3, 30 May 2022; Invent. Math. 229 (2022), 689–749, DOI 10.1007/s00222-022-01114-z. Source https://arxiv.org/pdf/1706.06712 (accessed 2026-10-30). Abstract: for Vk: x₁²+x₂²+x₃²−x₁x₂x₃=k, almost all k satisfy integral Hasse principle, infinitely many fail; Markoff morphisms yield finitely many orbits. Theorem 1.2(i): infinitely many Hasse failures, at least ≍ K/(log K)^{1/2} among |k|≤K. Includes sign/orbit analysis but no positive-orthant BM claim. Verdict: k≠0 ES yes/no: no; positivity-constrained BM: no.

Colliot-Thélène, Jean-Louis; Wei, Dasheng; Xu, Fei. “Brauer–Manin obstruction for Markoff surfaces.” arXiv:1808.01584v4, 10 Jul 2019. Source https://arxiv.org/pdf/1808.01584 (accessed 2026-10-30). Abstract: for Um (same Markoff equation), systematically compute Brauer group and integral BM obstruction; strong approximation away from any finite set fails; for m≠0,4 Brauer group does not control strong approximation. Theorem 1.1: if Um(A_Z)≠∅ then integral points are not dense away from any finite set; Cor. 1.2 translates to BM sets. They study existence/local-global failures and approximation, not positive-integer-only points. Verdict: k≠0 ES yes/no: no; positivity-constrained BM: no.

Dao, Quang-Duc. “Brauer–Manin obstruction for integral points on Markoff-type cubic surfaces.” arXiv:2202.07142v4, 15 Dec 2023. Source https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.07142 (accessed 2026-10-30). Abstract/Theorem 1.3: relative character varieties x²+y²+z²+xyz=ax+by+cz+d; positive proportion of family has no algebraic BM obstruction to integral Hasse principle but fails strong approximation, explained by BM; also BM-unexplained counterexamples. Not ES surface or positivity. Verdict: k≠0 ES yes/no: no; positivity-constrained BM: no.
