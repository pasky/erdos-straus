H. W. Lenstra, Jr., “Divisors in Residue Classes,” Mathematics of Computation 42 (165), Jan. 1984, pp. 331–340. Primary source PDF: https://pub.math.leidenuniv.nl/~lenstrahw/PUBLICATIONS/1984b/art.pdf. Accessed 2026-10-30.
Theorem (p. 332): if 0<r<s<n, s>n^(1/3), gcd(r,s)=1, there are at most 11 positive divisors of n congruent to r mod s; a polynomial-time algorithm finds all. Abstract says it is not known whether this extends to significantly smaller log s/log n. This is an upper bound and algorithm, not existence.
