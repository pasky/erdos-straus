N. A. Carella, “Upper Bound of the Least Quadratic Nonresidues,” arXiv:2106.00544v9, revised 2025-10-09, https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00544; HTML https://arxiv.org/html/2106.00544v9. Accessed 2026-10-30.
Theorem 1.1 claims n(p)≪(log p)^(1+ε) unconditionally. NOT ACCEPTED as theorem here: the displayed proof has a decisive gap. In Lemma 5.1, estimate (5.4) bounds terms using 1/|sin(πuv/p)| ≪ p/(uv); but v ranges over 1,…,p−1 and the denominator depends on uv modulo p. This inequality fails when uv>p (and is undefined at p|uv); no reduction or treatment of the multiples is provided. The asserted error bound, hence Theorem 1.1, does not follow. (The displayed v9 proof in §5 is the basis of this diagnosis.)
