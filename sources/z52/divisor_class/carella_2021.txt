N. A. Carella, “Upper Bound of the Least Quadratic Nonresidues,” arXiv:2106.00544v9, revised 2025-10-09, https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00544; HTML https://arxiv.org/html/2106.00544v9. Accessed 2026-10-30.
Theorem 1.1 in v9 claims n(p)≪(log p)(log log p)^(1+ε) unconditionally. NOT ACCEPTED as theorem here: the displayed proof has a decisive gap. In Lemma 7.2, estimate (7.6) starts with 1≤v<p/x, where uv<p for u≤x, but then extends the v-sum to 1≤v<p and applies 1/|sin(πuv/p)|≪p/(uv), asserting uv<p. The assertion is false over the enlarged range; no bound for these added terms is supplied. The asserted error estimate, hence Theorem 1.1, does not follow. (v9’s Theorem 1.1 conclusion and Lemma 7.2 proof are the basis of this diagnosis.)
