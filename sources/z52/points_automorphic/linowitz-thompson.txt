Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.6356 (accessed 2026-10-30)
Benjamin Linowitz and Lola Thompson, “The sign changes of Fourier coefficients of Eisenstein series,” arXiv:1305.6356v1, submitted 28 May 2013.
Abstract: They determine how often the p-th Fourier coefficients of an Eisenstein newform begin with a fixed sign sequence; an Eisenstein newform is uniquely determined by the signs of its Hecke eigenvalues for any set of primes with density greater than 1/2.
