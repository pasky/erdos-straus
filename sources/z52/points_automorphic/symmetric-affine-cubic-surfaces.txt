“Integral points on symmetric affine cubic surfaces,” arXiv:2204.13472v2, full HTML accessed 2026-10-30. (Paper author details in arXiv metadata; source is the full text.)

Theorem 1.1 / §8: for a cubic polynomial f(u)=u³+a₂u²+a₁u+a₀ over Z, the authors prove no integral Brauer–Manin obstruction to the Hasse principle for the affine surfaces f(u₁)+f(u₂)+f(u₃)=n; when 3a₁−a₂²=0 this is unconditional for all n, and otherwise their general statement is for all but finitely many n under the stated nonzero-cubic parameter condition. Theorem 8.1 gives Br(U_n)=Br(Q) and no integral BM obstruction outside an explicitly defined exceptional set A₃. The paper also gives a family where weak and strong approximation fail (Example 10.9).

This is a BM/non-obstruction result, not an assertion that an integral point exists for every n: a BM set can be nonempty while the integral locus is empty for reasons not detected by BM. The authors explicitly frame the conclusion as absence of integral BM obstruction.
