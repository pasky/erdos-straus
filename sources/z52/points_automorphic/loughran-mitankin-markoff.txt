Daniel Loughran and Vladimir Mitankin, “Integral Hasse principle and strong approximation for Markoff surfaces,” arXiv:1807.10223v3 (29 Apr 2019); IMRN 2021, no. 18, 14086–14122, DOI 10.1093/imrn/rnz114.
The paper studies integral Hasse principle and strong approximation for Markoff surfaces using Brauer–Manin. Its explicit results include Corollary 5.11: under the stated local-solubility condition, existence of a prime p>5 dividing m−4 with m−4 nonsquare in Q_p rules out a Brauer–Manin obstruction to the integral Hasse principle. Other results give explicit BM failures/strong approximation phenomena. Statements concern a family of Markoff parameters and frequencies/obstructions, not every member having an integral point.
