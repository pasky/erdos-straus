Source: https://arxiv.org/html/1901.08503v3 (accessed 2026-10-30)
Florian Wilsch, “Integral points of bounded height on a log Fano threefold,” arXiv:1901.08503v3 (21 May 2025); IMRN 2023, no. 8, 6780–6808, DOI 10.1093/imrn/rnac048.
Theorem 1.1 (source paper): For the two specified open subsets U_1,U_2 of the blow-up X of P^3 along a conic, the counting functions of integral points with log-anticanonical height ≤B have asymptotics N_1(B)=(20/(3ζ(2)))B log B+O(B) and N_2(B)=c B log B+O(B(log log B)^2), c=(20/3)∏_p(1−2/p²+1/p³). These are global height counts on fixed varieties, not a uniform lower bound for a varying prescribed fiber/parameter.
