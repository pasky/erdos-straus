Tim Santens, “Manin’s conjecture for integral points on toric varieties,” arXiv:2312.13914v2 (1 Aug 2025); arXiv record cites no journal publication.
The paper formulates an integral log-Fano height-counting conjecture and proves it for toric varieties (Theorem 1.1). The proof uses harmonic analysis on universal torsors; counting is asymptotic as B→∞ on fixed toric varieties, with stated accumulating/analytic obstructions handled in the theorem's hypotheses. This is not pointwise existence for every parameter n on a non-toric cubic surface.
