Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2503.08800v4 (accessed 2026-10-30)
Robin Zhang, “Positive integral points on acyclic cluster varieties,” arXiv:2503.08800v4 (28 Sep 2026; submitted 11 Mar 2025). No journal reference listed.
Theorem B(2): exact numbers of positive integral points on the finite-type affine varieties associated to generalized Cartan matrices; in particular exactly 4,400 and 26,952 for E7 and E8. Proof combines effective height bounds and exhaustive computation. The paper also gives infinitely many positive integral examples of specified Mordell–Schinzel/log K3 and threefold equations (Corollary A). Exact classification/finite search or explicit infinite families rely on cluster/frieze recurrence structure; this is not a lower bound for every fiber of an unrelated ES family.
