Damián Gvirtz-Chen and Giacomo Mezzedimi, “Non-thin rational points for elliptic K3 surfaces,” arXiv:2404.06844v1, full HTML accessed 2026-10-30.

Abstract and Theorem 1.1: if a K3 surface over a number field admits two distinct elliptic fibrations, then after a finite extension its rational points are not thin (potential Hilbert property). The paper also classifies geometric elliptic K3 surfaces with a unique elliptic fibration: 92 families in ranks 3–12 plus rank-2 cases (Theorem 4.5); in particular, Picard rank >12 implies at least two elliptic fibrations.

The proof uses lattice geometry, elliptic fibrations, covers and descent of the Hilbert property. This is about rational points after finite field extension on projective K3 surfaces, not integral points on affine log-K3 surfaces and not positivity or pointwise existence for a varying ES parameter.
