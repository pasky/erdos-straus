Bright, Martin; Loughran, Daniel. “Brauer–Manin obstruction for Erdős–Straus surfaces.” arXiv:1908.02526v2, 12 May 2020; Bull. London Math. Soc. 52 (2020), no. 4, 746–761, DOI 10.1112/blms.12359. Source: arXiv PDF https://arxiv.org/pdf/1908.02526 (accessed 2026-10-30).
Theorem 1.1: for n≥2 there is no Brauer–Manin obstruction to existence of natural-number solutions of (1.1), the original Erdős–Straus equation. Theorem 1.2: for odd n and u∈N³ a solution, ∏_{p|n}(-u1/u3,-u2/u3)_p=−1. Corollary 1.4 gives nonexistence for certain natural-number solutions when n is an odd square. Theorem 1.8: (U_n(R)^+ ×∏_p U_n(Z_p))^Br is nonempty, but is not the entire space; thus obstruction to strong approximation for natural-number solutions. Theorem 1.6 computes Br U_n/Br Q with an explicit transcendental element (not the asserted quaternion class on a k=4 fibre).
Scope: U_n is the original k=0 Erdős–Straus surface, not a nonzero-k deformation. Positivity is incorporated as the positive real locus in the approximation statement, but BM does not obstruct existence of a natural-number solution. Verdict: treats k≠0 fibres: no. Positivity-constrained BM: yes, but proves no existence obstruction for the original family; no positive-integral-point nonexistence result matching R.
