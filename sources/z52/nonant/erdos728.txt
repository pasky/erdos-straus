Source: Nat Sothanaphan, “Resolution of Erdős Problem #728: a writeup of Aristotle’s Lean proof,” arXiv:2601.07421v5, submitted 26 Jan 2026; accessed 30 Oct 2026 at https://arxiv.org/html/2601.07421v5.

Abstract excerpt: “For any constants 0<C1<C2 and 0<ε<1/2 there exist infinitely many triples ... a! b! | n! (a+b−n)! ... and C1 log n < a+b−n < C2 log n.” The proof uses a binomial divisibility reduction, Kummer carry counts, and a counting argument choosing carry-rich, spike-free m in each [M,2M]. Main result is Theorem 1; the formal Lean proof is described in §6. It is infinitely-many, not every-object. The paper itself credits Pomerance with a similar extension and says the results are close (Appendix “beyond this proof”), so AI attribution is not a new mathematical technique claim.
