Bourgain, “Pointwise ergodic theorems for arithmetic sets,” Publ. Math. IHÉS 69 (1989), 5–41; EuDML primary record https://eudml.org/doc/104056, full paper linked at Numdam. Evidence is pointwise ergodic convergence for specified arithmetic averaging sequences in measure-preserving systems, not equidistribution of every orbit nor existence at a chosen arithmetic prime. (Exact sequence-specific hypotheses require full paper check.)

Einsiedler–Lindenstrauss–Michel–Venkatesh, “Distribution of periodic torus orbits and Duke’s theorem for cubic fields,” arXiv:0708.1113v2 (3 Feb 2010), Ann. Math. 173(2) (2011), 815–885, DOI 10.4007/annals.2011.173.2.5, https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.1113. Abstract: equivalence classes of periodic torus orbits become uniformly distributed; suitably interpreted, ideal classes of a cubic totally real field equidistribute in a modular 5-fold. Proof combines subconvexity, measure classification, local harmonic analysis.

Lindenstrauss–Mohammadi–Wang, “Effective equidistribution for some one parameter unipotent flows,” arXiv:2211.11099v2 (23 Sep 2025), https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.11099. Abstract: polynomial-error effective equidistribution for unipotent subgroup orbits in arithmetic quotients of SL2(C) and SL2(R)×SL2(R), using Margulis functions, incidence geometry, spectral gap. ArXiv page opened; Annals 2026 publication page also opened: https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2026/204-2/p04.

Barroero–Widmer, “Counting lattice points and o-minimal structures,” arXiv:1210.5943v2 (27 Apr 2013), https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.5943. Abstract: sharp estimates for lattice points in fibers of a definable family; geometric error terms controlled by projection volumes. This yields conditional lower bounds if volume dominates error, but no blanket positivity theorem: positive volume alone does not settle a specified thin fiber.

Ax–Kochen / Ershov: modern contextual source opened today, Ketelsen–Dittmann, “Composition Ax-Kochen/Ershov principles and tame fields of mixed characteristic,” arXiv:2601.05790v2 (15 Jan 2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05790. Abstract states composition AKE holds for tame equal-characteristic coarsenings but may fail in mixed characteristic; valued-field theory need not be determined solely by underlying field, residue field, value group. It does not assert integer ES transfer.
