Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10474-020-01077-8 (accessed 2026-10-30)
J. Pintz and I. Z. Ruzsa, “On Linnik’s approximation to Goldbach’s problem. II,” Acta Math. Hungar. 161 (2020), 569–582, DOI 10.1007/s10474-020-01077-8; published 2020-07-22.
Abstract: every sufficiently large even integer is a sum of two primes and K powers of two with K=8, unconditionally. It notes Linnik first proved existence with unspecified large K; earlier explicit K=54,000. This is every-even-n, with abundant Goldbach pair representations (heuristic order n/log²n times sparse-sum weights), not an O(polylog n)-witness theorem.
