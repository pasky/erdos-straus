D. R. Heath-Brown and B. Z. Moroz, “Primes represented by cubic forms” (2002). Theorem 1.1 assumes an irreducible integral binary cubic form. It proves infinitely many prime values of f unless all values are even; in the all-even case it proves infinitely many prime values of f/2. The PDF does not use “admissible” as the theorem's hypothesis. The paper generalizes Heath-Brown's x^3+2y^3 result. The theorem's irreducibility hypothesis is stated in the source as irreducibility in ℤ[x,y]; the all-even exception is in Theorem 1.1 and Lemma 2.1.
Family-level infinitude; no prescribed target n.
