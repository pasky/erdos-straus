D. R. Heath-Brown and B. Z. Moroz, “Primes represented by cubic forms” (2002). Theorem 1.1: every admissible integral binary cubic form (irreducible over Q; no prime divides all coefficients) represents infinitely many primes. The paper generalizes Heath-Brown's x^3+2y^3 result. Note: source PDF's theorem header/body was checked; details such as the full definition of admissible are stated in the paper's introduction.
Family-level infinitude; no prescribed target n.
