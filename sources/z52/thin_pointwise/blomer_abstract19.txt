Valentin Blomer, “Ternary quadratic forms, and sums of three squares with restricted variables,” in The Anatomy of Integers, CRM Proc. Lecture Notes 46 (2008). Abstract describes a survey on representations by positive integral ternary forms, improved uniform asymptotic formulas for representation numbers, and applications to Gauss-type three-square theorems with almost-prime, squarefree, or smooth numbers.
Abstract alone does not state precise theorem hypotheses or per-target universality; do not elevate this to a pointwise all-n theorem.
