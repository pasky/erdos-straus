Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05396 (accessed 2026-10-30)
Jori Merikoski, “On primes represented by aX^2+bY^3,” arXiv:2503.05396 v1, submitted 2025-03-07.
Abstract: for coprime positive a,b, assuming a conjecture on Hecke eigenvalues along binary cubic forms, proves an asymptotic formula for primes ax²+by³ with x≤X^{1/2}, y≤X^{1/3}. Inputs include sieve, real quadratic/indefinite binary quadratic form theory, Weil exponential-sum bound, GL(2) spectral methods.
Conditional family-count, not prescribed-integer pointwise theorem.
