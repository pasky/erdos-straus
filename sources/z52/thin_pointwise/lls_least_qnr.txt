    We next single out the case when H is the trivial subgroup comprising of just
the identity. Here the problem amounts to getting bounds on the least prime P (a, q)
in the arithmetic progression a (mod q) (where we assume that (a, q) = 1). A cele-
brated (unconditional) theorem of Linnik gives that P (a, q) ≪ q L for some absolute
constant L. The work of Heath-Brown [10] shows that L = 5.5 is an admissible value
for Linnik’s constant, and this has been recently improved to L = 5.2 by Xylouris
[17].
   Under the assumption of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, Bach and Soren-
son [4] showed that

and they also derived the explicit bound
(1.2)                           P (a, q) ≤ 2(q log q)2 .
