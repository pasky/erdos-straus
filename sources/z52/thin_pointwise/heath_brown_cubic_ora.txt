D. R. Heath-Brown, “Primes represented by x^3+2y^3,” Acta Math. 186 (2001), 1–84. Theorem excerpt as indexed in the Oxford paper record/search source: “There are infinitely many primes of the form x^3+2y^3 with integer x,y.”
This is an infinitude/count over represented values, not a theorem for every prescribed integer. ORA identifies paper authorship/date and gives the full text link.
