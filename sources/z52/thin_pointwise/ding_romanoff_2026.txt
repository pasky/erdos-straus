Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03662 (accessed 2026-10-30)
Yuchen Ding, Huixi Li, Junfeng Li, “A Romanoff-type theorem for P₂+{a^a:a≥1},” arXiv:2607.03662 v1, submitted 2026-07-04.
Abstract: P₂={m: Ω(m)≤2}; P₂+{a^a:a≥1} has positive lower density. The proof uses a Romanoff second-moment method and a new average bound for a singular factor controlling arithmetic correlations among shifts a^a.
Introduction §1 also explicitly states Romanoff (1934): {p+2^k} has positive lower density; and Erdős (1950) used covering systems to construct an arithmetic progression, common difference 11,184,810, with no term expressible as p+2^k. These are historical claims cited to the original papers, verified here through this secondary source. This is positive-density / failure-set information, not every-n. An individual n's expected witness count is not specified as polylogarithmic by the abstract and may not be inferred from positive density.
