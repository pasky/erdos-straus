Theorem 1.2: “Zaremba’s conjecture holds.” Conjecture 1.1 defines this as existence of an absolute M such that every natural q is the denominator of a reduced fraction a/q with all partial quotients <= M. Source explicitly notes M is large absolute, not the conjectured 5. This is pointwise for every q; no expected count at each q stated in the theorem.
