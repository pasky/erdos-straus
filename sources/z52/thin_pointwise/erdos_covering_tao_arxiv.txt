Source: https://arxiv.org/html/0802.3361v4 (Terence Tao, “A remark on primality testing and decimal expansions”; accessed 2026-10-30). This is a secondary source, but it explicitly cites the original papers.
Introduction: Erdős (1950), “On integers of the form 2^k+p and some related problems,” constructs an infinite arithmetic progression of odd m for which |m−2^i| is composite for every i. Cohen–Selfridge (1975), “Not every number is the sum or difference of two prime powers,” modify this to an AP where |m−2^i| and m+2^i are both composite for every i. This source does not state precisely that every member fails to equal p+2^k, so do not overread: it verifies the covering obstruction for related prime-power forms and supports the known covering-failure analogy, not a stronger unverified formulation.
Tao's own Theorem 1.2: for fixed K, at least c_K N/log N primes p in [N,(1+1/K)N] have |kp±ja^i| composite simultaneously for all 1≤a,j,k≤K and 1≤i≤K log N. The proof uses a partially covering set of congruences followed by the Selberg sieve upper bound.
