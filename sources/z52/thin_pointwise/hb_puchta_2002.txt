Theorem 1 (opened manuscript): “Every sufficiently large even integer is a sum of two primes and exactly 13 powers of 2.” Theorem 2 states the same with exactly 7 powers of 2 assuming the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis. (Manuscript conclusion confirms 13 unconditional and 7 on GRH.)
Expected witness count stated: theorem guarantees at least one representation, not an asymptotic or expected count.
