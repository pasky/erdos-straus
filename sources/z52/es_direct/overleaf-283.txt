Abstract: “Let p∈Q[x] be integer-valued on Z, have positive leading coefficient, and have no fixed divisor on the positive integers. We prove that, for each α∈Q_{>0}, all sufficiently large integers m can be written as Σ_i p(n_i), where the n_i are distinct arbitrarily large positive integers satisfying Σ_i 1/n_i=α. Consequently, if p∈Q[x] is zero or has positive leading coefficient, then {p(n)+1/n:n∈N} is strongly complete. The proof combines the Roth–Szekeres–Graham theorem on complete polynomial sequences with reciprocal-preserving switches and a finite congruence correction.”
