Source accessed 2026-11-01: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.32302v1
Yichuan Cao and Xiaoping Xu, “Prime Numbers of the Form 24m+1 and Erdös-Straus Conjecture,” arXiv:2610.32302v1, submitted 2026-10-23.

§3, paper's own statement: “Claim. Z_+ = union_{r=1}^{56} S_r. We have used AI to test the claim for m <= 10^11 and it holds. But we failed to get a logic proof.” It then says this claim implies ES.

§4: the analogous claim for Howerton's fourteen wild primes was tested for m <= 10^7, but “we failed to get a logic proof.”

Abstract: gives 56 solvability polynomials; numeric test indicates they imply ES. It also claims numerical evidence for the Howerton conjecture and formulates a version of Goldbach modulo 24.
