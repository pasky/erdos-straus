Accessed 2026-10-30: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32140
Shisheng Li, “Unit fractions with semiprime denominators: an elementary proof of Erdős Problem #306,” arXiv:2609.32140v1, submitted 2026-09-26, 13 pp.; author identifies a human–AI collaboration and supplies Lean ancillary files. Abstract exact scope: “every positive rational number a/b with b squarefree” is a finite sum of distinct unit fractions whose denominators are products of two distinct primes. Method: reduce to small targets; choose a complete bipartite graph on prime intervals together with 2 and primes dividing b, plus a tuned prime segment; use a finite Fourier sum to count subgraphs with reciprocal sum congruent to a/b mod 1; positive main term for small integer frequencies, negligible remainder by divisor counting and a no-wrap-around Chinese remainder argument; small total mass forces equality. The abstract says Lean 4 formalizes the proof apart from a cited Ramanujan inequality.

This is not the Erdős–Straus conjecture. The Erdős Problems #306 page rendered on 2026-10-30 still lists #306 without a proof/update (site page does not show status under its post-Oct-6 policy); GitHub README table has #306 as open, despite the new arXiv claim. Marked for independent proof checking; do not call the database status settled.
