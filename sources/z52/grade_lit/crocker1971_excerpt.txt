            It has been shown by different methods that there is an
       infinity of positive odd integers not representable as the sum
       of a prime and a (positive) power of 2, thus disproving a
       conjecture to the contrary which had been made in the
       nineteenth century. The question then arises as to whether
       or not all sufficiently large positive odd integers can be re-
       presented as the sum of a prime and of two positive powers
