1. Ilya D. Shkredov, “On some results of Korobov and Larcher and Zaremba’s conjecture,” arXiv:2603.14116v2, revised 2026-06-24. PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.14116v2. Local PDF: shkredov-2603.14116.pdf (SHA-256 in README_pointwise.md). PDF page 1, Corollary 1:
“There is an absolute constant M ⩾ 2 such that for any prime (or square–free) number p there exists a, (a, p) = 1 such that a/p = [0;c1, . . . , cs], cj ⩽ M, j = 1, . . . , s.”

Same, PDF p. 6, Theorem 8:
“There is an absolute constant M such that for all M ⩾ M and any q ∈ Z there are at least q^(2w_M−1−o(1)) positive integers a, (a, q) = 1 with a/q = [0;c1, . . . , cs], cj ⩽ 100M, j = 1, . . . , s.”
The source defines Z on p. 4 (preceding Theorem 6) as its specified restricted modulus class; it is not every q. Theorem 8 is a power-size numerator count for q in Z, not a polylog count.

Same, PDF p. 6, immediately after Theorem 8:
“The expected number of numerators a in (22) is q^(2w_M−1−o(1)) similar to (16) and (19) … In contrast to Theorems 6, 7 in Theorem 8 our methods allow us to obtain a lower bound of the form q^(1−O(1/M)).”

Same, PDF p. 7, Remark 9:
“In early May, Xin Zhang informed the author that the Bougain–Gamburd–Helfgott–Sarnak–Varjú theory had been significantly advanced by him in collaboration with Jincheng Tang … consequently, Lemma 14 holds for any q and any bounded M. It follows that Corollary 1 is valid for any q (although the remaining results of the paper remain unchanged), and thus we now possess a proof of Zaremba’s conjecture in full generality …”
Thus the all-q claim is explicitly Corollary 1 plus Remark 9; the power count of Theorem 8 does not extend to all q in this version.

Same, PDF p. 8, Corollary 16:
“Let q ∈ Z, Λ1, Λ2 ⊂ Z/qZ be sets, I = [N] and A = I ⊔ Λ1, B = I ⊔ Λ2. Then there is an absolute constant κ > 0 such that |A ∩ B−1| − |A||B|/q ≪ √(|A||B|) N−κ.”
This is the pointwise (per modulus in Z) correlation estimate for the product-inverse incidence that underlies Theorem 8; its sets are structured, thick Cantor/interval constructions, not ES’s tied-modulus/polylog candidate family.

2. Xin Zhang, “Expansion in SL₂(Z/qZ) and Zaremba’s conjecture,” arXiv:2605.02518v2, revised 2026-05-08. PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.02518v2. Local PDF: zhang-2605.02518.pdf (SHA-256 in README_pointwise.md). PDF p. 1, Theorem 1.2:
“Zaremba’s conjecture holds.”
Definition immediately above: every natural q has a reduced a/q whose partial quotients are bounded by an absolute M; Remark 1.3 says M is effectively computable but large, not the conjectured 5.

Same, PDF p. 2, Remark 1.4:
“Bourgain-Varjú proved a spectral gap theorem in SL_d(Z/qZ) with respect to a fixed finite generating set S and an arbitrary modulus q. However, in the consideration of Zaremba’s conjecture, we need to deal with a generating set S that depends on q (see (3.5)), in particular, |S| ≈ q^(2τ) for some small but absolute τ > 0, and the dependence of the spectral gap produced in [BV12] is not good enough for our consideration here. We naturally need a theory in the spirit of Helfgott’s triple product theorem in SL₂(Z/pZ) [Hel08].”

Same, PDF p. 3, Theorems 2.1 and 2.2 (pointwise expansion/growth inputs, paraphrased only here): Theorem 2.1 gives |A·A·A| > q^δ|A| under a power-size lower/upper bound on |A| and uniform non-concentration on linear trace varieties modulo every sufficiently large divisor q₁|q. Theorem 2.2 gives bounded generation modulo a small exceptional divisor under the analogous uniform non-concentration hypothesis. These are uniform-in-q expansion inputs, not a lower bound for a polylog-sized ES set.

Same, PDF p. 6, Theorem 3.6, with notation defined in §3:
“Let A, B, S be as in (3.2) and (3.5). We have
Σ_{s∈S} Σ_{a∈A} Σ_{b∈B} 1_{sa=b} = φ(q)/q² |A||B|N + O_ε(q^ε√(|A||B|)N^(1−η)),
where η > 0 is some absolute constant and ε is arbitrarily small.”
PDF p. 7, immediately following (3.7): “the error term in (3.7) is smaller than the main term if we take τη > 1/M.” This quantitative main-term-dominance condition is the decisive pointwise step. §4, pp. 11–12 outlines the transfer from the correlation count to a bounded-partial-quotient numerator.

3. Yichuan Cao and Xiaoping Xu, “Prime Numbers of the Form 24m+1 and Erdös-Straus Conjecture,” arXiv:2610.32302v1, submitted 2026-10-23. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32302. In-repo API/full-text record: sources/z52/es_direct/2610.32302.txt. §3, authors’ verbatim statement:
“Claim. Z_+ = union_{r=1}^{56} S_r. We have used AI to test the claim for m <= 10^11 and it holds. But we failed to get a logic proof.”
The paper says the claim would imply ES. This is explicitly an unproved computational claim, not a theorem/proof; it does not alter open status.
