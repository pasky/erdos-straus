\begin{theorem}[Planar exterior count]
\label{thm:exterior-count}
There is an absolute \(\tau>0\) with the following property.  Fix
\(m\in\Z\setminus\{0\}\) and a bounded set
\(\Omega\subset\mathcal H_m\).  For every primitive totally real quartic
field \(K\), every order \(\OO\subset K\), every ordering of its real
embeddings, and every chosen system of fractional \(R\)-ideal
representatives for the ordinary classes, let \(M\) be defined by
Equation~\eqref{eq:ext-multiplicity}.
For all sufficiently large \(X\), every rectangle
\(\mathcal R\subset\Omega\) whose two side lengths are at least
\(X^{-\tau}\) satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{\substack{z\in F,\ \Tr z=m\\z\in\mathcal R}}M(z)
 \ll_{m,\Omega}\kappa\sqrt D\,\operatorname{area}(\mathcal R).
 \label{eq:ext-planar-count}
\end{equation}
The implied constant and the threshold for \(X\) are uniform in the
field, the order, the embedding ordering, the ideal representative system,
the position of the rectangle, and its side lengths and aspect ratio.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
Let $(K_i,\OO_i,\sigma_i)$ be any sequence in which $K_i$ is a primitive
totally real quartic field, $\OO_i$ is an order in $K_i$, and $\sigma_i$ is
an ordering of its real embeddings.  If $|\Disc(\OO_i)|\to\infty$, then
\[
 \int_{X_4} f\,d\mu_{\OO_i,\sigma_i}
       \longrightarrow \int_{X_4} f\,dm_4
       \qquad\text{for every }f\in C_c(X_4).
\]
There is no escape of mass: for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is a compact
$\mathcal K\subset X_4$ such that
$\mu_{\OO_i,\sigma_i}(\mathcal K)\ge1-\varepsilon$ for all sufficiently
large $i$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Entropy criterion for two-sided tubes]
\label{thm:rig-tube-criterion}
Let \(\xi_i\) and \(\xi\) be \(a(\R)\)-invariant Borel probabilities
on \(X_4\), with \(\xi_i\) converging weak-* to \(\xi\).
Suppose that \(t_i>0\) tend to infinity and that \(\eta>0\).
Assume that for every compact \(\Omega\subset X_4\) there are an open
identity neighborhood \(B\subset\mathrm{SL}_4(\R)\) and a constant
\(C_\Omega\) such that
\[
 \xi_i\bigl(xB^{(-t_i,t_i)}\bigr)
 \le C_\Omega e^{-2\eta t_i}
 \qquad(x\in\Omega,\ i\ge1).
\]
Then \(h_\xi(a(1))\ge\eta\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Positive-entropy measure classification]
\label{thm:rig-ekl-classification}
For \(n\ge3\), let \(A_n\) be the group of positive diagonal matrices
in \(\mathrm{SL}_n(\R)\).  Let \(\nu\) be an \(A_n\)-invariant and
\(A_n\)-ergodic probability on
\(\mathrm{SL}_n(\R)/\mathrm{SL}_n(\Z)\), with the left action.
If \(h_\nu(a_0(1))>0\) for some one-parameter subgroup
\(a_0:\R\to A_n\), then there are a
closed connected subgroup \(L\subset\mathrm{SL}_n(\R)\) containing
\(A_n\) and a point \(x\) such that \(Lx\) is closed and \(\nu\) is
the \(L\)-invariant probability on \(Lx\).
\end{theorem}
```

\begin{theorem}[Product rule]\label{thm:product}
Let \(V\) and \(W\) be pattern groups, let \(U=U_\alpha\) be a
one-dimensional root group, and suppose that multiplication gives a
coordinate decomposition
\[
                 V=WU,\qquad W\mathrel{\triangleleft}V.
\]
Suppose also that some \(a_0\in A\) fixes \(U\) by conjugation and that
the automorphism
\[
                 \Delta(w)=a_0^{-1}wa_0
\]
strictly contracts \(W\).  Then, for almost every augmented state
\(\omega\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:product-rule}
 m^V_\omega
   \ \propto\
   ( (w,u)\longmapsto wu)_*
          \bigl(m^W_\omega\otimes m^U_\omega\bigr).
\end{equation}
Here \(\propto\) denotes equality up to multiplication by a positive
constant.
\end{theorem}
```

# Torus-packet equidistribution in prime, quartic, and sextic degrees

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Equidistribution of Prime-Degree Torus Packets with Arbitrary Local Type](../../preprints/Equidistribution-of-Prime-Degree-Torus-Packets-with-Arbitrary-Local-Type-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization proves equidistribution of volume-weighted torus packets for totally real number fields of every fixed prime degree at least five. For any sequence of full lattices whose multiplier-order discriminants tend to infinity, the packet measures converge weakly to Haar probability measure and form a tight family, so no mass escapes. Arbitrary local homothety types are allowed, and the fields may vary along the sequence.
