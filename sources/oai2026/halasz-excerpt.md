# External input: Halász, not an OpenAI manuscript

Granville–Harper–Soundararajan, *A new proof of Halász's theorem, and its
consequences*, Compositio Math. 155 (2019), 126–163, arXiv:1706.03749v1,
§1.1, Corollary 1.2. Full HTML visited 2026-10-21:
https://arxiv.org/html/1706.03749v1

Short statement excerpt (formula typography normalized):

> For given f ∈ C(κ), define M=M(x) by
>
> max_{|t|≤(log x)^κ} |F(1+1/log x+it)/(1+1/log x+it)| := e^(−M)(log x)^κ.
>
> Then
>
> Σ_{n≤x} f(n) ≪_κ (1+M)e^(−M)x(log x)^(κ−1) + (x/log x)(log log x)^κ.

Here F(s)=Σf(n)n^(−s), and C(κ) is the class with
|Λ_f(n)|≤κΛ(n), where −F′/F=ΣΛ_f(n)n^(−s), with absolute convergence for Re s>1.
A Dirichlet character belongs to C(1). This is a published input, not a
consequence of the unrefereed collection.
