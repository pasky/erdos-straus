                                                 We study the Kronecker symbol st for the sequence of the convergents s/t of a
                                                 purely periodic continued fraction expansion. Whereas the corresponding sequence
                                                 of Jacobi symbols is always periodic, it turns out that the sequence of Kronecker
                                                 symbols may be aperiodic. Our main result describes the period length in the
                                                 periodic case in terms of the period length of the sequence of Jacobi symbols and
                                                 gives a necessary and sufficient condition for the occurrence of the aperiodic case.

Let L be such that sk /tk is critical with respect to L. Then there is a number r1 ≥ 0 such
that sk /tk is critical with respect to 2r1 L but not with respect to 2r1 +1 L. Put k1 = k
