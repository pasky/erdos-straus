# Provenance: Jacobsthal literature for §58

Fetched 2026-08-30 for the §58 literature audit.

- `costello-watts-2013.pdf` — Fintan Costello and Paul Watts, *A short note on Jacobsthal's function*, arXiv:1306.1064v1 (2013). Downloaded from <https://arxiv.org/pdf/1306.1064>. SHA-256 `04fe7b680a3eaccf4153f636c02bf572f449ff386914f38beb4d87ad9ab91ef9`. Page 1 explicitly states the formulation attributed to Iwaniec: if `k = omega(n)`, then `g(n) <= X (k log k)^2` for an absolute but unspecified constant `X`, where `g(n)` is the least length forcing an integer coprime to `n` in every interval.
- `ford-green-konyagin-maynard-tao-2014.pdf` — Kevin Ford, Ben Green, Sergei Konyagin, James Maynard, and Terence Tao, *Long gaps between primes*, arXiv:1412.5029 (downloaded from <https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.5029>). SHA-256 `6a2c86f06946315f2abafb11b25c60bef9ca780921e4b0c1f55a144430c48145`. Pages 2–3 define `j(n)` as the maximal gap between integers coprime to `n`, identify `Y(x)=j(P(x))-1`, and state that Iwaniec gives `Y(x) << x^2`; the paper's results are lower bounds for maximal gaps.
- `ford-konyagin-maynard-pomerance-tao-sieved-sets.pdf` — Kevin Ford, Sergei Konyagin, James Maynard, Carl Pomerance, and Terence Tao, *Long gaps in sieved sets* (2018 preprint). Downloaded from <https://math.dartmouth.edu/~carlp/longgaps.pdf>. SHA-256 `c5d8df94544bbe0ce34fe9b4fef1549082acec194ec8822f88d228608b6f04f5`. Definition 1 and Theorem 1 treat a bounded set of residue classes at each prime and prove large gaps in a one-dimensional sifted set; they do not give an upper bound for the first survivor of a system involving composite moduli.

Primary-source retrieval note: Iwaniec's *On the problem of Jacobsthal*, Demonstratio Mathematica 11 (1978), 225–231, DOI <https://doi.org/10.1515/dema-1978-0121>, is marked open access by the publisher. Direct PDF and legacy-PDF requests on 2026-08-30 were redirected to De Gruyter Brill's automated human-verification page and returned no PDF bytes. No fake primary copy is archived here. The exact bound used in §58 was therefore checked in the accessible full Costello–Watts paper, and its primorial specialization was independently checked in the accessible full Ford–Green–Konyagin–Maynard–Tao paper.
