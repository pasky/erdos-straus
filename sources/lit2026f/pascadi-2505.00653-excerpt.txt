A. Pascadi, "On the exponents of distribution of primes and smooth numbers", arXiv:2505.00653 (2025).
[accessed arXiv HTML]
Abstract: primes and smooth numbers equidistributed in APs to moduli up to x^{5/8-o(1)}, triply-well-factorable weights for primes (and improvement for well-factorable linear sieve weights); removes Selberg-conjecture dependence of Lichtman (2309.08522) and Pascadi (2304.11696). Uses Pascadi's exceptional-Maass large sieve (2404.04239) + Watt.
Theorem 1.3. a != 0, A,eps>0. Assume (i) Q <= x^{5/8-eps}, lambda_q triply-well-factorable of level Q, or (ii) Q <= x^{3/5-eps}, lambda_q upper-bound well-factorable LINEAR sieve weights of level Q. Then
  sum_{q<=Q,(q,a)=1} lambda_q (pi(x;q,a) - pi(x)/phi(q)) <<_{a,A,eps} x/(log x)^A   [form as in Maynard II].
Corollary 1.4. #{p<=x: p,p+2 prime} <= (3.203+o(1)) Pi_2(x).
Theorem 1.5. a != 0; y in [(log x)^C, x^{1/C}], Q <= x^{5/8-eps}:
  sum_{q<=Q,(q,a)=1} |Psi(x,y;a,q) - Psi_q(x,y)/phi(q)| <<_{eps,A,a} Psi(x,y)/(log x)^A.
Corollary 1.6. #{n<=x: P+(n),P+(n+1) <= y} << x rho(u)^{1+5/8-eps}.
Author: "5/8-eps is now the best exponent of distribution for both primes and smooth numbers, in essentially any setting relevant for sieve theory"; no better with quadruply-well-factorable weights by current methods.
Regime: fixed a; average over q<=Q with factorable weights (primes) / absolute values (smooth). Upper-bound sieve applications only.
