J. Maynard, "Primes in arithmetic progressions to large moduli I: Fixed residue classes", arXiv:2006.06572 (2020); Mem. Amer. Math. Soc. 306 (2025), no. 1542.
[accessed arXiv PDF via reader; verbatim modulo formatting]

Theorem 1.1. Let a in Z, eps>0, Q1,Q2 with Q1 Q2^2 < x^{1-100eps}, Q1^12 Q2^7 < x^{4-100eps}, Q1^20 Q2^19 < x^{10-100eps}. Then for every A>0
  sum_{q1<=Q1,(q1,a)=1} sum_{q2<=Q2,(q2,a)=1} | pi(x; q1q2, a) - pi(x)/phi(q1q2) | <<_{a,eps,A} x/(log x)^A.
Corollary 1.2: a fixed, Q=x^{1/2+delta}, 0<delta<1/42: holds over moduli q<=x^{1/2+delta} having a divisor d in a "convenient" range; abstract: expected asymptotic for all but O(delta Q) moduli q~Q.
Corollary 1.4: q1<=x^{1/21}, q2<=x^{10/21-eps}: level 11/21.

Quoted background (from same paper, Section 1):
Theorem A (BFI, Acta Math 156 (1986)). a in Z, delta>0, Q=x^{1/2+delta}:
  sum_{q in [Q,2Q],(q,a)=1} |pi(x;q,a)-pi(x)/phi(q)| <<_a delta^2 x/log x + x(loglog x)^{O(1)}/(log x)^3.
Theorem B (BFI [Acta Math 156, Theorem 10]). A>0, lambda_d well-factorable of level Q < x^{4/7-eps}:
  sum_{q<=Q,(q,a)=1} lambda_q (pi(x;q,a) - pi(x)/phi(q)) <<_{a,A,eps} x/(log x)^A.
Theorem C (Zhang, Polymath). a in Z, 0<delta<7/300, Q=x^{1/2+delta}; exists eta(delta)>0:
  sum_{q<=Q,(q,a)=1, p|q => p<=x^eta} |pi(x;q,a)-pi(x)/phi(q)| <<_A x/(log x)^A.

Method: Heath-Brown identity -> convolutions; Linnik dispersion; Deshouillers–Iwaniec Kloosterman bounds (Kim–Sarnak), Zhang-style for x^{1/4}^4 configurations.
Regime: FIXED residue a, AVERAGE over moduli q ~ x^{1/2+delta} (absolute values, but needs a convenient factor). Nothing for a single modulus.
