# López (2022; arXiv version 2024)

**Citation:** Miguel Ángel López, “Structure and form of the solutions of the Erdős-Straus conjecture,” arXiv:2206.10319 (first posted 2022; v4, 2024), https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.10319 ; HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2206.10319v4.

**Verified quotes** (v4, Abstract; Theorem in §2; §3 “Notes on results”):

> “We classify all solutions that satisfy that they are of the form (du,dv,duv) ...” (Abstract)

> “There exists a solution for p with the form (du,dv,duv) if and only if there exists t ≥ 0 and a divisor w of k+1+t such that w ≡ −1 mod (3+4t).” (Theorem, part c; p=4k+1)

> “The answer is negative: the first value not to have one is p=193. ... up to the value p=4369, only two prime numbers do not have a solution of this type: 193 and 2521.” (§3)

**Relevance (inference):** The form `(du,dv,duv)` is exactly the b=1 Type II line up to permutation of denominators: from `(ad, acdp, cdp)`, reorder as `(ad, cdp, acdp)=(du,dv,duv)` with `u=a`, `v=cp`. Thus López's divisor criterion is a direct published characterization of this line, not merely a nearby family. Its bounds report only the initial failures through 4369; it does not assert the proposed complete exception set through 10^8.
