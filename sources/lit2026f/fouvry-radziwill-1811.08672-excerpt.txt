É. Fouvry, M. Radziwiłł, "Level of distribution of unbalanced convolutions", Ann. Sci. Éc. Norm. Supér. (4) 55 (2022), 537–568; arXiv:1811.08672.
[accessed arXiv HTML v1]
Abstract: essentially arbitrary sequence on x integers convolved with tiny Siegel–Walfisz sequence on exp((log x)^eps) integers has (weak) level x^{1/2+1/66-eps}. Consequences: tau_k to moduli x^{1/2+1/66}, E2 numbers in APs, sieve weights of level x^{1/2+1/66} to moduli x^{1-eps}, Brun–Titchmarsh for almost all q~x^{1-eps} (constant < 4). Tools: Bettin–Chandee trilinear Kloosterman fractions, Duke–Friedlander–Iwaniec bilinear.
Corollary 1.1. |alpha_m|<=tau_k(m), |beta_n|<=tau_k(n), beta Siegel–Walfisz, MN/2<=x<=4MN:
  sum_{Q<=q<=2Q,(q,a)=1} | sum_{x<mn<=2x, mn≡a (q)} alpha_m beta_n - (1/phi(q)) sum_{x<mn<=2x,(mn,q)=1} alpha_m beta_n | <<_A x(log x)^{-A}
 if (i) exp((log x)^eps)<=N<=Q^{-11/12}x^{17/36-eps}, 1<=|a|<=x/12  [=> Q up to x^{1/2+1/66-3eps} for N<=x^eps];
 (ii) N<=x^{7/90-eps}, Q<=x^{53/105-eps}, |a|<=x/12; (iii) N<=x^{101/630-eps}, Q<=x^{53/105-eps}, |a|<=(x/4)^{eps/1000}.
Corollary 1.2. k>=1: uniformly Q<=x^{17/33-eps}, 1<=|a|<=x/12:
  sum_{Q<=q<=2Q,(q,a)=1} | sum_{x<n<=2x,n≡a(q)} tau_k(n) - (1/phi(q)) sum_{(n,q)=1} tau_k(n) | <<_eps x/(log x)^{1-eps}.
Corollary 1.3: same for multiplicative g, |g|<=tau_k, with 1_prime·g Siegel–Walfisz.
Regime: residue a essentially arbitrary (|a|<=x/12), ABSOLUTE values averaged over q~Q<=x^{17/33}; weak (log-power) saving.
Follow-up (abstract only): T. Wright, "Trilinear Kloosterman fractions I: partially fixed moduli and unbalanced convolutions", arXiv:2604.25177 (2026): same estimate with N-range enlarged for Q<=X^{1/2+1/66-delta}, and Q up to X^{45/89-eps} with wider N.
