E. Kowalski, Ph. Michel, W. Sawin, "Bilinear forms with Kloosterman sums and applications", Ann. of Math. (2) 186 (2017), 413-500; arXiv:1511.01636 (v5).

Abstract: We prove non-trivial bounds for general bilinear forms in hyper-Kloosterman sums when the sizes of both variables may be below the range controlled by Fourier-analytic methods (Polya-Vinogradov range). Applications: second moment of cusp forms twisted by characters mod primes; distribution in APs to large moduli of certain Eisenstein-Hecke coefficients on GL3.

Notation: B(K,alpha,beta)=sum_m sum_n alpha_m beta_n K(mn); Kl_k(a;q)=q^{-(k-1)/2} sum_{x1...xk=a} e((x1+..+xk)/q) (normalized; |Kl_k|<=k).

Theorem 1.1 (General bilinear forms). Let q be a prime, c coprime to q. Let 1 <= M <= N q^{1/4}, q^{1/4} < MN < q^{5/4}. Let N in [1,q-1] be an interval of length floor(N), alpha=(alpha_m)_{m<=M}, beta=(beta_n)_{n in N}. For any eps>0,
 B([xc]^*Kl_k, alpha, beta) << q^eps ||alpha||_2 ||beta||_2 (MN)^{1/2} ( M^{-1/2} + (MN)^{-3/16} q^{11/64} ),
implied const depending on k, eps.
Remark 1.2: Polya-Vinogradov type bound (MN)^{1/2}(q^{-1/4}+M^{-1/2}+N^{-1/2}q^{1/4}log q) nontrivial only for N >> q^{1/2}log^2 q. For M=N, (1.2) nontrivial as long as M=N>q^{11/24}; at M=N=q^{1/2} saving q^{-1/64+eps}.
NB: n must range over an INTERVAL (beta arbitrary on it); m arbitrary support in [1,M].

Theorem 1.3 (Special bilinear / type I). q prime, c coprime to q, 1<=M<=N^2, N<q, MN<q^{3/2}; |alpha_m|<=1, N interval of length N:
 B([xc]^*Kl_k, alpha, 1_N) << q^eps ||alpha||_1^{1/2}||alpha||_2^{1/2} M^{1/4} N (M^2N^5/q^3)^{-1/12}.
Remark 1.4: nontrivial for M=N > q^{3/7}; saving q^{-1/24+eps} at M=N=q^{1/2}. (For k=2 slightly stronger: Blomer-Fouvry-Kowalski-Michel-Milicevic Prop 3.1.)

Theorem 1.7. f holomorphic primitive cusp form level 1. For any eta<1/102, any PRIME q <= x^{1/2+eta}, a coprime to q, A>1:
 sum_{n<=x, n=a (q)} (lambda_f*1)(n) - (1/phi(q)) sum_{n<=x,(n,q)=1}(lambda_f*1)(n) << (x/q)(log x)^{-A}.
(Pointwise in a single prime modulus; exponent of distribution 1/2+1/102.) d3 analogue: Friedlander-Iwaniec 1985, Heath-Brown 1986, FKM 2015 (prime moduli).
