## Primary references opened

1. **Bright–Loughran**, Martin Bright and Daniel Loughran, “Brauer–Manin obstruction for Erdős–Straus surfaces,” *Bull. Lond. Math. Soc.* 52 (2020), 529–543, arXiv:1908.02526v2. [Abstract/metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.02526); [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1908.02526v2). Locally archived at `../lit2026/audit-bright-loughran-1908.02526.txt` and `../bright-loughran-1908.02526.pdf`.
   - Corollary 1.3 excerpt (full text): “there exists i≠j such that …” the relevant ratio of two denominators is a quadratic nonresidue for prime inputs.
   - Corollary 1.4 excerpt: “If n is an odd square, then there are no natural number solutions” of either standard Type I or Type II.
   - Corollary 2.7 / 2.9: on the desingularization and the singular surface, respectively, the Brauer quotient is `Z/2` generated by the indicated quaternion class.
   - Theorem 1.2 / Theorem 1.8: ordinary Brauer–Manin does not obstruct the stated integral ES points. Appendix A explicitly compares the character conditions with Yamamoto’s lemmas.
   - Relevance limit: these statements do not compute the campaign’s twisted cover `Y_d` or its finite étale descent set.

2. **Elsholtz–Tao**, Christian Elsholtz and Terence Tao, “Counting the number of solutions to the Erdős–Straus equation on unit fractions,” *J. Aust. Math. Soc.* 94 (2013), 50–105, arXiv:1107.1010. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.1010). Archived PDF `../elsholtz-tao-1107.1010.pdf`; extracted text `../lit2026/audit-elsholtz-tao-1107.1010.txt`.
   - Propositions 2.2 and 2.6 are titled “Description of Type I solutions” and “Description of Type II solutions”; these give the standard normalized parameterizations used in A.
   - Visit to experimental HTML returned a broken/minimal image extraction; exact proposition references were verified in the locally archived PDF extraction, not inferred from that failed HTML page.

3. **Yamamoto**, Koichi Yamamoto, “On the Diophantine equation `4/n=1/x+1/y+1/z`,” *Memoirs of the Faculty of Science, Kyushu University, Series A* 19 (1965), 37–47. [Bibliographic record in the opened Bright–Loughran full text, ref. 24](https://arxiv.org/html/1908.02526v2). Archived scan OCR at `../o101/yamamoto-1965-kyushu.ocr.txt`.
   - Bright–Loughran Appendix A identifies Yamamoto Lemmas 2–4 as reciprocity conditions on prime ES solutions and derives them from their Corollary 1.3. This is the known arithmetic antecedent; the no-factoring oracle reduction is not stated there.

4. **López**, Miguel Ángel López, “A Complete Congruence System for the Erdős–Straus Conjecture,” arXiv:2404.01508v3 (2024). [Metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01508); [opened full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.01508v3). Archived full extracted text `../lit2026f/r270-lopez-2404.01508v3-html.md`.
   - Theorem 1 gives a divisor/congruence characterization for solutions `(du,dv,duv)`; Theorems 4 and 6 characterize Type B congruences; Theorem 7 characterizes Type A; Theorem 8 packages these as polynomial forms.
   - The text states that “the square of every natural number lacks both Type I and Type II solutions,” citing Schinzel and Yamamoto / ET.
   - Scope: solution structures/congruences, not F_n, H₄, fixed-denominator pivot graphs, trapped components, or the dyadic marked folding identity.
