## 2. PROVED: the probability estimate has the required order of quantifiers

Let `S` now include the one Haar unit prime `ℓ`; all other active primes have uniform additive Haar law on `1+q^K Z_q`, `K≥1`. For each exact cylinder use `N=∏_{q∈S}q^max(v_q(m)−K_q,0)`, `K_ℓ=0`. If any frozen digit disagrees, probability is zero. Otherwise at `q≠ℓ` the conditional digit factor is exactly `q^−max(v_q(m)−K_q,0)`, also when retained and peeled powers overlap. At `ℓ|m` it is `1/φ(ℓ^v)` rather than `1/ℓ^v`. So the unconditional probability is `1/N` times `ℓ/(ℓ−1)` if `ℓ|m`, times 1 otherwise. Intersect survival with the ℓ-nonresidues; do **not** renormalize to that half without a factor 2. With only `ℓ` active the excluded mass is `O_ε(∑_{k≥1} ℓ^(−k(1/5−ε)))=o(1)` as `ℓ→∞`; choose a large `ℓ≡3 mod 4` outside 840 and obtain initial surviving mass `δ>0`.

On unfreezing a new `q`, couple old/new draws by keeping the old active coordinates and changing only `q`. Every old survivor that becomes a hit lies, in the new draw, in a class with `q^(K+1)|m`. It is immaterial that some **old hits may disappear**. Fix the enlarged finite support `S` *first*. Use `N≥(q^(K+1)/512)^(1/11)`. The loss is bounded by the corresponding tail of `O_ε(∑_{N supported on S} N^(−1/5+ε))`. For `0<ε<1/5` the full sum is `∏_{p∈S}(1−p^(−1/5+ε))^−1`, finite; its tail tends to zero independently of the depths. The product is **not** bounded uniformly over growing `S`; no such bound is used. Choose the new depth at step `i` after fixing its support to spend `<δ/2^(i+1)` (`i≥1`), respecting 840. In particular no explicit good small `ℓ` or computable practical depth is claimed.

For the infinite product, finite cylinder laws eventually agree. `U` is closed (complement a union of cylinders); `U` intersected with the NR-at-ℓ residue condition is still closed. The limit probability is **at least** the limsup, not at most. Finally every local law is nonatomic, so the countable union over coordinates and algebraic numbers is null even after restricting to a positive-measure survivor set. This proves (E) in the stronger no-exact-zero form for nonzero unary polynomials, **not** finite valuation support. `P−1` is already a counterexample to the latter.

## 3. Model interfaces checked (relative to the cited tower and section lemmas)

* Use the **line/row-only** tower of `Z73_PID_SQUARE.md` §1, not the deformation tower with extra centers. `P−1` stays nonunit prime at each stage. After setting `P=1`, at least one primitive row entry survives. On the nonempty pivot chart the next stage is a polynomial line over a localization; the two charts cover with dense overlap when both exist. Inductively the fiber is geometrically integral and rational and its projection is dominant. It is enough to use an affine-line **torsor** trivial on the pivot chart, not global polynomial coordinates. On that fiber a Dirichlet output `a+mY` is nonzero since not both coefficients vanish there. Finitely many nonzero outputs and pivots can be avoided simultaneously at a rational point of the resulting dense rational chart (ℚ infinite).
* At each finite prefix choose such a point, discard its finite denominator and nonunit primes, and enlarge cutoffs monotonically. Congruence to this point on the free parameters, with `P=π_q≡1 mod q`, preserves all units/integral generators in that prefix. The ℓ place can use an empty prefix. Distinct primes need **not** use compatible rational points. Lift the prefix algebraically independently over `ℚ(π_q)`; at every tail Dirichlet variable impose the conditional rule on the actual earlier values (at most one forbidden residue, even at 2). This ensures embeddings into each field, eventual integrality for each fixed element of `A`, integrality of each `Y` everywhere, and output units everywhere for globally Bézout-coprime `R` inputs. Fair scheduling covers these inputs by persistence of stage primes. No embedding of the entire field in the restricted adeles is needed or available via these maps: `1/(P−1)` is not integral almost everywhere.
* For nonzero `a,b∈R`, let `(a,b)A=hA`, `u=a/h,v=b/h`. The serviced union provides `Uu+Vv=1`. Off finitely many primes all four terms' factors are integral, so `min(v_q(u),v_q(v))=0` there. Embeddings guarantee finite valuations at the other primes for nonzero inputs. Multiply `h` by a rational of precisely the **residual minima**, either sign of valuation allowed. This gives `d∈R` and a normalized pair primitive everywhere. The Bézout coefficients over `A` have finitely many exceptional primes. There an integral solution exists since a residual entry is a unit; all solutions differ by a scalar times the primitive syzygy. One rational scalar close enough to the local corrections and integral elsewhere gives coefficients in `R`. This proves finitely generated ideals principal; it does not normalize `h` to a unit at all marks. Every standard `q` is prime in `R` since its evaluation kernel mod `q` is `qR` (divide by `q` at all other places without cost), and constants surject. Infinitely many such primes divide `P−1≠0`, excluding external PID.
* Clearing negative valuations and translating by a rational only require eventual integrality, so the bounded-approximation/floor proof survives. `P` and the serviced Dirichlet primes for actual `R` tasks are permanent `A` primes and already units at all marks: division by them in `A` preserves `R`-integrality. `Y∈R` preserves the progression. For ES, at a finite stage containing a putative triple, use `LOGIC_MARKED_SECTIONS.md` with `W` empty at the top: good integral models off finitely many places and the open conditions of integrality of just the marked triple at the others are feasible by the embeddings. ET's finite class cover of its large primes forces this profile into a family (ordinary Dirichlet in the simultaneous finite cylinder if it avoided each). Contradiction.

## 4. PROVED relative to the same inputs: local NR holds; universal NR actually fails here

`NR(p)` below denotes the **consequent** of `Z65_LOGIC2.md` §1's NR sentence. At `P` choose `d=ℓ`. Both `−1` and `π_ℓ` are nonsquares mod `ℓ`, so `−π_ℓ` has a standard root `x`. Then `(x²+P)/ℓ∈R`; at all other places division is by a unit. With a standard inverse `v` of `π_ℓ mod ℓ`, also `u=(1−vP)/ℓ∈R`. These are full ring witnesses, not just a local root; `ℓ≡3 mod 4`.

**Counterexample to a global reading in this very ring.** Put `B=8ℓ`, `Q=1+B(P−1)`. This is a prime element of `ℚ[P]` (nonconstant linear), hence a permanent prime of `A`. It is `1 mod q` at *every* prime: at `q≠ℓ` use the imposed profile, and at `ℓ` use `ℓ|B`. It is `1 mod 8` in `R`. Therefore it has valuation zero everywhere, stays prime in `R`, is positive infinite, and `Q(π_q)` is a square unit at every place (Hensel for odd `q`, the `1 mod 8` criterion at 2). This alone rules out standard witness moduli (use a prime `s≡3 mod 4` dividing one), but does **not** by itself rule out a nonstandard one. Here is the latter step:

1. Suppose there are `d=4k+3>0`, `x²+Q=dy`, `ud+vQ=1` in `R`. Include **all** `d,k,x,y,u,v` at one finite stage. By the same (E) section argument as for ES, get rational polynomials in `T` satisfying these identities with `P=T` and all witness values integral at the marks. On the real stage over `P>1`, `x²+Q>0`; hence `d` never vanishes. This locus is semialgebraically connected by the line/row induction (remove codimension ≥2 then lift to a real line bundle, as in `Z73_PID_SQUARE.md` §3). The sign is positive at the ordered point and so on the section for `T>1`.
2. Take a common coefficient denominator `D` of the witness polynomials. At any ordinary integer `n≡π mod D` all their values are integers, since evaluation differences are integral at primes of `D` and the coefficients are integral elsewhere. For each prime power in `BD` choose a residue of a local square root of `1+B(π_q−1)` modulo that power. CRT gives arbitrarily large integers `t` with `t²≡1+B(π−1) mod BD`. Thus `n=1+(t²−1)/B` is an integer `>1` with `n≡π mod D` and `Q(n)=t²` **exactly**. This uses marked-section integrality, not parsimony, nor a simultaneous primality conjecture.
3. Specialization at `n` produces ordinary integers with `d>0`, `d≡3 mod 4`, `d|x²+t²` and `(d,t)=1`. A prime `s≡3 mod 4` dividing `d` then has `(x/t)²≡−1 mod s`, impossible. Thus `¬NR(Q)` even for unrestricted ring witnesses.

Consequently this is **not** a countermodel to ES over a theory *including universal NR*. It refutes the implication “a certificate at the ES input suffices” over the stated base axioms. It neither settles that stronger theory nor refutes an external-PID strengthening by a different construction. The draft's square-class-change warning also checks out: `−4d≡g mod ℓ` forces `(−d/ℓ)=−1`; the odd character of the negative discriminant of `ℚ(√−d)` has conductor dividing `4d`, and at `q≡−ℓ⁻¹ mod 4d` takes value `χ(−1)χ(ℓ)=+1`. Take a fresh odd `q` by ordinary Dirichlet. Then `−4d` is a square unit at `q`, but II-2 with `a=1,f=ℓq` now hits. Square-class preservation is no substitute for the tail estimate.
