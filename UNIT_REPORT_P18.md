## Fidelity judgment calls

- Kept source §47's antichain proposal for chronology, but placed the §49 refutation immediately after it so no reader can mistake (47.16) for open.
- Used `A*` only for the historical §47 formulation and `A^dagger` for source §49's exact reduced family; they denote the same implication antichain at those stages.
- Condensed explanatory prose and finite diagnostics, not theorem quantifiers or hypotheses. In particular, Theorem 49.5 says **every** admissible even `m`, not merely some `m`.
- Did not infer `mu^dagger = Theta(Lambda)`; the paper states only `Theta(L^2) <= mu^dagger <= O(Lambda)` and records the missing lower bound.
- Limited Theorem 49.4 to the uniform profile it actually refutes; small-`g` restricted or averaged profiles are not declared false.
- “Dormant” applies only to the complete-system ordinary hierarchy/factorial-moment axis. It is not used to close the pair endpoint, `H_PF'`, (33.16), or every conceivable pointwise minorant.
- Referred to the §39 record as “unconditional” only together with its CLAIMED/PROVISIONAL label; no external validation is implied.
- Preserved Theorem 50.1's raw/nonprimitive boundary and did not add coprimality of `a,b`.
- Stated `H_SPF(A)` as quantitative and sufficient, not logically weakest; removing the log-power bound is explicitly weaker.
- Kept Bateman–Horn, Chebotarev, Elliott–Halberstam, and Duke limitations as assessments of standard outputs, not logical independence claims.
- Theorem 50.6 yields only an active/unforced slice under GRH, never positivity.
- All §49/§50 finite data remain Computational/informational and support no growth law or hypothesis.
