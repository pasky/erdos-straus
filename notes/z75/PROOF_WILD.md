## 2. Phase 2: two-target signed cycle — sufficient, but not necessary even with no cutoff

Write r=kx−n, in the range n/k<x≤3n/k, with r>1 and gcd(r,nx)=1. Set D={a mod r : a divides x, a>0}. If a/b=−1 or −n modulo r for divisors a,b of x, use respectively d=nx·a/b or d=x·a/b. In either case d divides (nx)² and d≡−nx mod r, so y=(d+nx)/r and z=((nx)²/d+nx)/r are positive integers giving the completion. No ordering relative to x is needed.

**Signed-cycle criterion (PROVED).** Suppose n^h=−1 mod r for the first h>0 with n^h∈{±1}, and r>2. If a coset of ⟨n⟩ modulo sign is entirely represented by D, then there is a completion. Indeed if D meets −D we are done. Otherwise choose the signs of the consecutive representatives so that they lie in D. If there is no −n ratio, a step must keep the sign; after h steps the initial orientation has reversed, a contradiction. Equivalently every twisted cycle needs a missing projective class upon failure. For k=4 and n an odd square, r≡3 mod 4 and −1 cannot be a square modulo r (Jacobi −1); the twist itself is impossible. This test uses exact ratios, not a claim that character escape suffices.

**Discard as universal.** For each of 193, 2521, 3361, 5569, 9601 the test fails at *every* admissible r≤2p (`wild_deep.txt`), not just at r≤511. These primes are 1 mod 4 so r=1 does not arise. The code enumerates every r=4x−p, the entire projective subgroup until its first return and all divisor classes. This rules out complete twisted-cycle forcing on the proposed denominator interval, not partial cycles, other ratios or eventual success. B4 in `FRONTIER.md` §3 already warns against transparent coset-cover arguments; our small-cycle certificate is not identical but the concrete misses suffice. It never coupled distinct r's. The global quantifier over x does not itself supply edges between different cycles.

## 3. Phase 2: folding a positive path at a pivot

HJ words here have **all letters ≥2**. Their continuant K is the tridiagonal determinant with those diagonal entries and −1 on both off-diagonals (K(empty)=1). Standard recurrence K(P,c)=cK(P)−K(P minus last), where the formal predecessor of the empty word has determinant 0. For a pivot write the word P,c,S with both arms nonempty. Let L=K(P), a=K(P minus last), R=K(S), b=K(S minus first); L>a≥1 and R>b≥1 are coprime pairs by the recurrence. Expansion gives n=cLR−aR−bL. If ab divides c and t=c/(ab)≥2, **fold** to

`(U,t,V)=(bL,t,aR),  n=tUV−U−V`.

This is not an HJ identity preserving the slope. It preserves the determinant. Conversely all lifts of an oriented folded triple are obtained by b|U, a|V with L=U/b>a, R=V/a>b and the two coprimalities: the reverse of P is the minus expansion of L/a, and S that of R/b, unique by ceiling Euclid. The middle letter is c=tab. The case a=b=1 includes the trivial length-three lift. Each triple has ≤2τ(U)τ(V) lifts allowing either orientation, i.e. n^{o(1)} for bounded-by-n endpoints. **B3 applies**: the large population of long positive words cannot be counted as independent successful starts.

**Discriminant character.** For n=p prime, let Q be the denominator of the full HJ fraction p/Q, i.e. its tail continuant. Put L'=K(P minus first), a'=K(P minus first and last) (0 for the formal predecessor if P has length 1). Then L'a−La'=1 by the continuant identity and Q=cL'R−a'R−bL', whence LQ=L'p+R. Thus χ_p(Q)=χ_p(LR). The folded character is χ_p(tU−1)=χ_p(UV)=χ_p(ab)χ_p(Q), since (tU−1)V=p+U. Arms are smaller than p by monotonicity. Only the folded character uses (S), only at p≡1 mod 4; a sign-changing move is useful when the output is negative. This is not the square-class-preserving signed detour ruled out in §3 of the frontier. It still has to find the integral fold.

**Pivot separation lemma (PROVED).** Two foldable pivots cannot have another letter between them. At i<j−1 the right subtail determinant b_i includes the letter c_j; K of a nonempty word is at least every letter in it because adjoining either end strictly increases K. Likewise a_j≥c_i. Foldability gives c_i≥2b_i≥2c_j, c_j≥2a_j≥2c_i, impossible. Hence at most two folds per word, necessarily adjacent if distinct.

**Matching theorem (PROVED).** Make a simple graph of the unoriented folded triples (identify exchange of endpoints); join distinct triples when they are folds of a common word. Every vertex has degree ≤1, even allowing *all* positive words and lifts. In fact a distinct neighbour, if it exists, is computable without enumerating words.

Proof: by separation the common word is P,c,d,S with nonempty outside arms, determinants (L,a),(R,b) as before. Simultaneous foldability reads c=t·aR and d=k·Lb, t,k≥2. Set U=LR and e=ab. The two triples, oriented with their smaller endpoint first, are

`(U,t,e(kU−1)),   (U,k,e(tU−1)),    n=e(tU−1)(kU−1)−U`.

Indeed the arm continuants next to c,d are dR−b and cL−a respectively. Since 1≤e<U and k,t≥2, neither larger endpoint can be mistaken for U. From either triple recover U as its smaller endpoint, then e=(n mod U), the ordinary positive remainder when a switch exists. For the triple (U,t,V) there is now only one candidate k=(V/e+1)/U. It is a distinct neighbour exactly when e≠0, k is integral ≥2, k≠t, and one can factor U=LR, e=ab with 1≤a<L, 1≤b<R, gcd(L,a)=gcd(R,b)=1. Sufficiency follows by building the two arms and c,d as above. This also handles the ambiguity from many lifts or reversal: they all give the *same* other triple. QED.

The ratio of the two characters (their product) is **χ_p(Ue)**: the product of the two large endpoints is e²(tU−1)(kU−1)=e(p+U). In particular it can be −1, but a repeated unfold/refold walk just shuttles between the two vertices. This kills an unbounded descent by this move alone, not arbitrary determinant-preserving surgery.

## 4. Actual sign switches arbitrarily far into square-mimicking progressions

**Progression theorem (PROVED relative to Dirichlet).** For every M≥1 and H there is a reduced progression of primes p≡1 mod lcm(M,840) with a sign-changing pair as in §3, common endpoint U>H, one *non-certifying* middle letter 2 and the other middle letter unbounded along the progression. In particular the flip is real in the Mordell-hard domain. This is existence of progressions of *successes*, not a cover.

Put m=lcm(M,840). Take an odd prime q>max(5,H) congruent to 2 mod 5 and −1 modulo every odd prime dividing m except 5, by CRT/Dirichlet. Use U=2q, e=5, outside arms L=2,a=1,R=q,b=5, k=2. Vary t in

`p=A(Ut−1)−U,   A=5(2U−1)`.

Since gcd(A,U)=1 the progression p≡−A−U mod AU is reduced. Its intersection with p≡1 mod m is nonempty: gcd(AU,m)=10, since 4q−1≡−5 modulo each odd prime of m other than 5, 4q−1≡2 mod 5 and v₂(U)=1. Also −A−U≡1 mod 10. CRT gives a reduced progression; Dirichlet supplies infinitely many primes, eventually t>2. For them χ_p(Ue)=(2/p)(q/p)(5/p)=(5/q)=−1: p≡1 mod 840, p≡5 mod q and q≡2 mod 5. Moreover the k=2 word has χ_p(2U−1)=(p/(2U−1))=(−2/(2U−1))=+1: p≡−U modulo 2U−1 and 2U−1≡3 mod 8. Thus the t-word has character −1 and supplies the given 4-specific (S) certificate. No primality of 2U−1 is needed. The argument cannot start at an odd square. Example of the local fold pattern: 19609=5(2·26−1)(3·26−1)−26. This example is hard mod 840, not 1 mod 840; the theorem's CRT subfamily is narrower.

**How far this goes.** Every fixed q here merely gives a congruence family (B1). The progressions can be made compatible with arbitrarily much *finite* square data by changing q first; the quantifiers are ∀M ∃progression, not ∀p ∃q. At a *given* p this construction needs q|(p−5) **and** the cofactors to line up. E.g. neither 193 nor 9601 has *any* distinct adjacent switch, not just none of this special form. `FRONTIER.md` §3–4: HJ length three, its character criterion and endpoint forest are prior (`LOWER_TAIL_ASTRA.md`); the contribution here is the lift/switch/matching classification and sign-changing family, not a new equivalence for ES. No external priority claim.

## 5. A quantitative requirement on a way out

**Rational-overlap lemma (PROVED).** Drop the requirement that the common unfolding be an integral HJ word. Two arbitrary distinct triples sharing an endpoint U, with other endpoints V,W and middle letters t,k≥2, obey s=tU−1, h=kU−1, N=n+U=sV=hW. Write e=N/(sh)=A/B in lowest terms. Then V=e h, W=e s, and B divides gcd(s,h) which divides |t−k| (s,h are units mod U and their difference is (t−k)U). The first divisibility holds because B divides both s,h by integrality of V,W. At primes p the character switch is χ_p(UAB) provided the endpoints are units: VW=eN. When e is integral **even without any arm restrictions**, both other endpoints exceed U for U≥2; every path of such switches stays at the same smaller endpoint, a single tied modulus.

To actually move *through* U on an increasing forest path V<U<W requires denominator B>kA and t≥2k+1. Indeed V=A(kU−1)/B<U gives (kA−B)U<A≤V<U; hence kA≤B. But B divides kU−1 and is coprime to k, excluding equality. Also W>V implies t>k so B≤t−k. Hence B=2 is impossible. B=3 forces A=1,k=2: such a crossing ends on a terminal middle letter and cannot repeat outward. This strengthens the specific pure-pivot obstruction into a restriction on rationally relaxed exchanges; the known Fibonacci endpoint growth in `LOWER_TAIL_ASTRA.md` §4 already implies a logarithmic depth bound.

**Conductor steering.** Do not try to concatenate integral flips. To cross several endpoints, search *backwards* from a t=2 edge using divisors B of the difference of middle letters; after j backwards crossings from a terminal 2, the entering letter is ≥3·2^j−1. The rational upgrade really adds switches: at 193 the crossing 2<5<22 has (t,k)=(20,2), e=2/9 and opposite word characters, despite an empty integral matching. At 8,813,281 there are five sign flips in six increasing crossings, but the smallest denominator among those five is already 281. At 2521 there is **no increasing crossing at all**. So don't conjecture even the rational upgrade is universal. The remaining plausible experiment is to switch which endpoint is shared *without* requiring the two words to meet in one vertex: e.g. a two-pivot block replacement preserving the determinant but changing both outer determinants. Demand a transition already at 2521 and discard any rule whose states all retain a common smaller endpoint. The sign test χ_p(UAB) is a comparator for the one-vertex baseline, not an existence theorem. The known arbitrarily long monochrome forest paths preclude forcing by depth alone. No positive lower bound at one arbitrary prime follows.
