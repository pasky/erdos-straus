## 1. The affine norm conic: an exact quotient, not one bit

Use additive squareclasses and bits, and quotient always by the **image** from constants on the indicated torsion. Set `K=F(i)`, `X: x²+y²=s`, `U=x+iy`, over an ordered characteristic-zero field `F`. Write `{a,b,…}` for mod-2 Galois cup products. There is a natural description

```
 Br(X)[2]/im Br(F)[2] = ker(F*/2 → H³(F,μ₂), a ↦ {a,-1,s}) / J,
 J=0 if s is not a K/F norm,   J=⟨-1⟩ if it is.
```

Here and below the characteristic-two coefficient twists are identified via −1 (coefficients of order two, not field characteristic two). Indeed the geometric fibre is a punctured line, with geometric μ₂-cohomology only in degrees 0 and 1, both trivial Galois modules of order 2. The degree-one generator is the geometric double cover `T²=U`. Its transgression is `{−1,s}`: lift `g` on the cover by `T↦T` if `g(i)=i`, and `T↦√s/T` otherwise, acting on constants by `g`. The factor of the composite relative to the lift of `gh` is the sign `χ_s(g)χ_{-1}(h)` in bit notation. This is the descent obstruction (the transgression of an invariant torsor). By the module rule for the spectral sequence the only outgoing differential from `(1,1)` is therefore cup with this class. The filtration in total degree two gives the stated kernel modulo constants **before** dividing by Pic/2. Call its coordinate `a` the parameter of a μ₂-lift. Over `K`, the Laurent-ring description makes this lift modulo constants the symbol `(a,U)`: cup with the Laurent unit represents the geometric generator.

For the Pic check use Galois descent from the Laurent PID, with units `cU^n`. A cocycle has `N(c)s^n=1`; coboundaries shift the exponent by even integers and Hilbert 90 removes the constant ambiguity. Thus `Pic(X)=0` in the nonnorm case and `ℤ/2` otherwise; invariant units are just `F*`. In the norm case identify the circle with its group `T₁=R¹_{K/F} G_m` by a rational point. The nontrivial Pic class has cocycle of odd Laurent exponent, so its Kummer boundary has parameter −1: in taking square roots of the transition units the odd geometric Kummer class occurs precisely on the nontrivial coset of `Gal(Fbar/K)` (equivalently use the Kummer map of the unit cocycle to geometric units modulo squares). This proves the formula. Alternatively in this case restrict at the rational point: over `K` the infinity residue is the restriction of `a`; at zero residue the class is constant on the complete rational conic, hence in the image of 2-torsion constants. The residue of `a=-1` vanishes and the kernel of restriction on squareclasses is exactly `⟨-1⟩`.

In particular the norm kernel on `K*/2` is the image of `F*/2` (if `N(z)=b²`, write `z/b=u/ū`, so `[z]=[b/Nu]`). This does not establish that every such residue lifts. Even `Br(C̄)[2]` need not be the image of `Br(F)[2]`: take torsion **after** quotienting `Br(F)` by the conic class. For the inert prime circle below the quotient computed above has infinite rank. For clarity the vertical residues of this corestriction are sums of residue norms from the Gaussian divisors over each vertical divisor (the tame corestriction formula).
