# The −1 generator on the square limit

## 1. W1: generate the target, rather than searching subproducts

**Idea (Assessment).** Use many distinct primes in the *exponent*, not primes dividing the evaluated cyclotomics. Each prime gives a reusable ratio (1+x^j)/(1+x). On a Mersenne modulus their ratio group is independent of the unknown stripped exponent. The small integer identity 9=3² collapses the common coset of all the plus-values. A doubling orbit telescopes to a negative sign.

**Theorem A (PROVED rel. Dirichlet and the ET square-unit obstruction).** Use h,k as in §0, put N=2^h−1, u=2N=k−1. There is an effective L such that for every positive s divisible by L, n=k·2^s+1 has an interior good word and an explicit three-unit-fraction solution. This sequence of n (even with any finite initial segment deleted) is not covered by any finite collection of Elsholtz–Tao Prop. 1.9 classes and fixed prime-divisor classes. For example take every h=5^a, a≥1: 2^{2·5^{a−1}}≡−1 mod 5^a, by induction raising to the fifth power. Or take every prime h≡5 mod 8, by the supplement to quadratic reciprocity. Thus m=(h+1)/2 is unbounded. No requirement that n be prime.

*Proof of the group assertion.* Work in (ℤ/N)^×. Write B_i=1+2^i with index read modulo h (the power is reduced first). Every B_i is a unit: if an odd prime divisor of 2^h−1 divided 2^i+1, the odd order of 2 at that prime would be even. Let I=(ℤ/h)^×. Form H=⟨B_i/B_j:i,j∈I⟩. All B_i have the same image g in the quotient by H. Since 1,3∈I and B_3=B_1² as integers, g=g², i.e. every B_i∈H. Also B_1/B_{−1}=2 ∈H. Choose t>0 with 2^t≡−1 mod h. Then

    ∏_{j=0}^{t−1} (1+2^{2^j}) = 2^{2^t}−1 ≡ 2^{-1}−1 = −2^{-1} mod N.

All indices here are units, so −1∈H. This is a group calculation, **not** an assumption of random residues of divisors.

*Uniform exponent supply.* For each j∈I choose N distinct odd primes ℓ≡j mod h, all distinct across rows; take their squarefree product D and L=4D. This excessive but computable supply uses only Dirichlet. For L|s remove from s/D its full part supported at primes dividing h; call the remaining integer r. Then 4|r, (r,h)=1, and s/(Dr) is odd. Set x=2^r. The distinct factors Φ_{2ℓ}(x)=(1+x^ℓ)/(1+x), ℓ|D, have joint product dividing x^D+1 | 2^s+1 by the cyclotomic factorisation for odd D; they need not be coprime as integers. Their residues are B_{rj}/B_r. As j runs over I these generate H (ratios to any one base suffice). Each generator occurs N times, so every element of H is a subproduct: in a group of order ≤N reduce each signed multiplicity modulo its order. Choose such a product A≡−1 mod N. No prime-factor search in n+u. In particular A>1. Each selected factor is 1 mod 16 since 16|x. As N≡−1 mod 16 and n≡1 mod 16, we have A≡7u−1 mod 8u by CRT (8u=16N). Hence t'=(A+1)/u is a positive integer ≡7 mod 8; v=(n+u)/A is integral and ≡7 mod 8. Thus n=t'uv−u−v with all letters ≥2, and 8|n+t' gives the negative Jacobi sign by `Z64_SDEEPF.md` Thm 2.1. Directly D'=(t'v−1)/4 is positive integral and 4/n=1/(AD'n)+1/(uD')+1/(AuD') by multiplication. The D' formula uses **4**, not 8 as a general theorem (in these particular congruences t'v≡1 mod 8 as well; see the screen below).

*No finite covering.* For any menu modulus M choose arbitrarily large s divisible by L and by the order of 2 modulo the odd part of M (omit the condition for odd part 1). At the odd part n≡k+1=2^{h+1}, a square unit since h is odd; at the dyadic part n≡1. This is a unit square mod M, outside every class by `EXPONENT_COVERING.md` Thm 1.4/Cor. 1.5. This excludes finite ET/fixed-divisor covers of the restricted sequence itself, not arbitrary algorithms. ∎

**Corollary A.1 (PROVED rel. Mertens in fixed progressions).** For each such h, the same construction gives (S) and ES for a set of exponents of natural density one, even relative to any fixed exponent progression. Indeed, reserve two disjoint pools of odd primes ≡1 mod h each with divergent reciprocal sum (e.g. alternate them in ascending order), and one pool in each other reduced residue class. For density-one s there are at least N distinct prime factors from each required class (use the first pool for 1), and at least one prime ≥5 from the reserve pool. For clarity, on a finite subpool disjoint from the fixed progression modulus the divisibility indicators have exact limiting independent Bernoulli laws by CRT. Their mean is the reciprocal sum, their variance at most the mean; Chebyshev followed by increasing the pool proves the assertion, also on a progression. Select N primes per class dividing s to define D, but do not select the reserve prime. It survives in r, giving r≥5 and 16|x, the only use made of 4|s/D in that step. The discarded h-part is odd. This goes beyond smooth exponents containing one frozen D; no density statement for *prime values of n*. A fixed progression subfamily has the noncovering property when it contains the square-limit progression, not necessarily for every progression.

**Screen (Assessment).** F1: an actual word has negative Jacobi sign, impossible at odd squares; the limiting square is never used as a point with a word. F2: the congruences even give 8|t'v−1 on this particular progression, hence an 8/n identity there; this is not a numerator-uniform pointwise theorem! All n here exceed 1,590,284,203,561 already from the crude prime supply, and no assertion about R₈ or any c=1 line is used. F3: the proof is direct for composites as well. F4: it does not posit (★) or Type A at their zeros. The §4 tied-modulus, scale, averaging, signed-reflection, relaxation, privacy, genus and bounded-witness screens: all work is at the given n; the residue target is obtained exactly from mutually available integer factors at one moving level, not from a character, a mean, or a fixed menu. No density of primes, BM implication, near-square pointwise claim or extension away from the thin family. Scope vs z77: fixed base AND fixed offset while the odd multipliers vary; exponent progression only. Assessment: the off-core gap is serious; this does not decide whether the proposed s≥C log k theorem is true.

## 2. W2: how changing the boundary affects the repair

**Idea (Assessment).** On a line-arrangement open, prescribe the jump ratios of translated units as well as the original coordinates. Prime selection is then a multiquadratic compatibility problem, not a divisor problem. Parallel deletions already produce unbounded-rank nonabelian geometric fundamental groups. Conversely a *single* deletion at a contracting divisor can detect old repairs for every valuation depth.

**Lemma B0 (PROVED rel. unramified cyclic-algebra evaluation).** Delete `kCu−1=0` as well as the coordinate hyperplanes from S: `kuBC=n+u+B`. Every z77 one-prime repair at the diagonal `P=(1,(n+1)/q,(q+1)/k)` with q∤n(n+1), odd q, jump E≥2 is now rejected already by a quaternion downstairs. Indeed z=kCu−1 equals q at P; at the new point with `B'=q^{E−1}(n+1), C'=C`, u'=(n+B')/(kB'C−1) reduces to −n and z'≡−n−1 is a unit mod q. Choose any integer quadratic nonresidue L at q. The symbol (L,z) on the open has invariant difference 1/2 at q, zero difference elsewhere. The global sum at P was zero. Equivalently on the C-fibre z((q+1)B−1)=1+(q+1)n, undoing the denominator changes the valuation of z by an odd number, not a multiple of mM. This kills *every depth in this repair*, not all adeles. The deleted divisor is harmless for positive integral points since kuC≥k. Attempt to pin z instead of C and use the torus fibre was not justified: z=q forces (n+u)/q integral at q, but q= kCu−1 and u not arbitrary. No global obstruction inferred.

**Theorem B (two pencils; PROVED rel. purity, Kummer theory, Chebotarev and z75).** On S° use the affine-plane coordinates (u,B), deleting the three lines uB(n+u+B)=0. Delete in addition finitely many rational lines of the following two types, avoiding duplicates:

    vertical: u+a=0,                r=(a−n)/(a+1);
    concurrent: u+n+bB=0,          r=(b−n−1)/b.

Assume the displayed ratios are defined and nonzero. Form the multiquadratic field K generated by √(−n) and the square roots of all r. Suppose complex conjugation on ℚ(ζ_k) acts trivially on K∩ℚ(ζ_k). Then for every M the whole Br[M] of the resulting open V fails to obstruct in the outer-integral positive domain. Even if its geometric fundamental group is nonabelian. This is **downstairs BM**, not étale-BM or Chabauty–Kim on the new opens. The field test is an explicit sufficient condition, not asserted necessary for BM nonemptiness.

*Proof, prime selection.* Enlarge M to an even multiple, write M=2^v h, h odd. By the compatibility hypothesis an automorphism of Kℚ(ζ_k) exists which fixes K and acts by conjugation on ζ_k. Adjoin over ℚ the unique real h-th roots of −n and all r to obtain a field F of odd degree: at each successive real field the minimal polynomial of the chosen root divides a polynomial with just one real root, so its degree is odd (nonreal roots occur in pairs). The multiquadratic compositum with the cyclotomic k-field is Galois of 2-power degree and linearly disjoint from F. Extend the automorphism by the identity on F and then to a normal closure. Chebotarev gives infinitely many q≡−1 mod k with all −n,r squares and h-th powers, hence M-th powers since v₂(q−1)=1. Avoid all primes at the finite bad constants, their denominators, kM and n(n+1). This includes nonzero b and a+1 etc.

*Proof, changing values.* Use the z75 patch at P in B0, with E=M, B'=q^M B₀, C'=(q+1)/k and u'=(n+B')/(kB'C'−1). At q the normalized value of u changes by an M-th power (u'≡−n), B by q^M and n+u+B=kuBC by their product. The vertical-unit ratios reduce to r. For a concurrent unit use the exact identity `u+n+bB=B(kCu−1+b)`. Its ratio divided by q^M reduces to (b−n−1)/b. Hensel proves these ratios are M-th powers in ℚ_q. By enlarging the excluded finite set P avoids all added lines (at P their values are a+1 or B₀(q+b)). All other components are P; the treatment of positivity, original-model integrality and restrictedness is as in z75. The ratio calculation applies to **all arrangement units simultaneously**, not just to one selected character.

*Proof, completeness of the Brauer test.* For a rational affine line arrangement, Br modulo constants is generated by (χ,f_i) for arbitrary constant characters and (f_j,f_i) for quadratic symbols on distinct defining affine linear polynomials. Inductively adding the ith deletion adds residues in H¹ of a rational affine line with finitely many rational punctures. Over the algebraic closure the puncture characters give copies of ℚ/ℤ(−1); their invariant part is order two per finite puncture. The H¹ of the constant field splits by a rational point. For each finite puncture choose **one** earlier nonparallel line passing through it; its restricted polynomial has a single simple zero there, independently spanning its geometric slot by quadratic Kummer. This residue is realized by the pair symbol, and constant residues by (χ,f_i); purity gives the induction, starting from Br(𝔸²). If the original class is killed by even M, its residues are killed by M; after the pair symbols the residual constant character is killed by M too. Subtract these M-torsion lifts and iterate. Thus high-order cancellations do not require control of unbounded characters here. All local evaluations are unchanged when each f_i changes by an M-th power. ∎

**Consequence and test bed (PROVED rel. B).** Infinitely many arbitrarily large arrangements pass: take any distinct v∈ℚ∩(0,1), a=(n+v²)/(1−v²), b=(n+1)/(1−v²). Both ratios are v², so the field criterion is exactly the old nonsquare-kernel test (z75). Both deletions avoid all positive real points, hence no positive integer solution is discarded. The map u:V→𝔸¹ minus the vertical punctures (including u=0) has surjective geometric fundamental-group map: restrict the base away from the finitely many pencils' intersection coordinates and the fibre is a punctured affine line varying locally topologically trivially; loops in the further-punctured base lift up to paths in a connected fibre, and generate π₁ of the original base. With any added vertical line this already has a free nonabelian quotient. On U° take the pullbacks of the same forms via u=nx/z, B=nx/y. The z75 morphism S°→U° with denominators (uBC,nuC,nBC) pulls these units back identically; hence functoriality transfers nonobstruction for all Br[M] to the corresponding U open, including the **actual full numerator-8 failures 131 and 241**. On X° take any deletions pulling back under c=1 to these lines or avoiding the image, again by functoriality; includes 4545. These conclusions concern outer-integral adeles, not S-unit integral points. Unlike B0 this is a blindness theorem, not an explanation of failure.

**Screen (Assessment).** No integer count or positivity inference from a genus. F1: √−n in the field test is incompatible with q≡−1 mod 4 on squares. F2/F3/F4: bounded blindness works deliberately at nonsquare failed inputs, for k=8 outside twice-squares too, irrespective of primality; no witness for R, Type A or ES is inferred. Infinite conductor, but fixed exponent; passes checklist #19 only by changing the geometric opens. Not a claim about mixed pencils away from the singular reduction, nonabelian upstairs classes, or B2.

## 3. W4: an unramified correspondence cannot change the level

**Idea (Assessment).** Do not guess a coordinate move. Classify the output *units* of any putative algebraic move after arbitrary finite étale input preprocessing. On a cofinal Fermat cover the units are monomials, and four monomials have only one nondegenerate additive relation. Test the resulting finite symmetry group on integral points, including when the endpoint is prescribed in advance.

**Theorem C (PROVED rel. the four-line π₁ computation in z75 and the Newton polytope product formula).** Fix n,n′>0, k≥4 integral. Write W_n for the all-nonzero-coordinate open of `kabd=n+a+b` over ℂ. Consider a connected finite étale cover π:Y→W_n, of arbitrary degree, and *any regular morphism* f:Y→W_n′. If f is nonconstant and has any positive real output (all three coordinates positive), f factors as π followed by a permutation of four homogeneous coordinates, normalized for n′. In particular:

* at n=n′, the only positive-to-positive **integer** moves at any positive integer input are the identity and a↔b (neither changes d);
* for n′=p prime, p>n, **no** move of this kind sends any positive integral input to a positive integral output. This holds even if the cover and map are chosen separately for every endpoint p. No conclusion for positive-dimensional parameter spaces, ramified covers, maps defined only after removing more divisors, or constant outputs already encoding a solution.

*Geometric rigidity.* Identify W_n with `W={[X₀:X₁:X₂:X₃]: ΣX_i=0, ∏X_i≠0}` via `[n:a:b:−kabd]`. Its inverse scales the first coordinate to n and recovers d=(n+a+b)/(kab). Coordinate-wise m-th powers from the Fermat surface complement dominate every connected finite étale cover for some m: π₁(W)=ℤ³ topologically, generated dually by the coordinate ratios (`notes/z75/DISPROOF_OBSTRUCT.md` §2); the powered cover corresponds to mℤ³. A sign convention in the Fermat equation is immaterial; over ℂ rescale to `1=X^m+Y₁^m+Z^m` on the affine torus. Every regular unit on this open is a constant times a Laurent monomial in X,Y₁,Z. Indeed its divisor on the smooth projective Fermat compactification is supported on the four irreducible plane section curves cut by the coordinates (smooth Fermat plane curves, lines for m=1). These are all hyperplane sections and their degree is m, so a principal combination has coefficient sum zero. A monomial in coordinate ratios realizes exactly this divisor, leaving a constant.

Pull the target coordinate ratios to the cover and clear constants: the relation in the torus is a Laurent polynomial Q with at most four nonzero monomial terms, divisible by `P=1−X^m−Y₁^m−Z^m` (the defining irreducible equation). If Q is identically zero, group the four original terms by equal exponent. Each group sums to zero. If there is one group the projective point is constant; otherwise there is a vanishing proper subsum, impossible at a positive output since n′,a′,b′>0 and the fourth term is minus their sum.

If Q≠0 its Newton polytope is the sum of the simplex of P and another polytope, so is three-dimensional. With at most four terms it is itself a tetrahedron. Its normal fan refines that of P, hence coincides with it (both have four maximal cones, no cone can be subdivided). Thus after a Laurent monomial translation `Q=c₀+c₁X^l+c₂Y₁^l+c₃Z^l`, l a positive integer and all coefficients nonzero: the four facet normals fix the three coordinate lower bounds and an upper bound on the sum. This polynomial vanishes on the affine Fermat hypersurface by density, including where coordinates vanish. At the axis points all m-th roots of unity give m|l and c₁=c₂=c₃=−c₀. On Z=0, X^m=Y₁^m=1/2 gives `2(1/2)^{l/m}=1`, so l=m. Consequently Q was a monomial multiple of P, up to scalar, and each target homogeneous coordinate is the same factor times one of the four base coordinates. This is equality of maps on the cofinal cover, hence on Y by surjectivity.

*Integrality.* At a positive input the three positive coordinates must map to the three positive slots (changing the common sign would yield three negatives). Let w∈{n,a,b} be sent to the first slot and scale by n′/w. Then `d′=w²d/(nn′)` since the product of the other two positive coordinates before scaling is nab/w. At n′=n and w=a we have n/a= kbd−1−b/a ≥(kd−1)b−1 ≥4d−2>√d, so 0<d′<1. Similarly for w=b. For n′=p>n observe a,b<n by the same inequalities and d<n since `n/d=kab−(a+b)/d ≥4ab−a−b≥2`. Integrality would require p|w²d, impossible. This step uses actual sizes, not generic rational points. ∎

**Endpoint tower verdict / screens (Assessment).** A backwards-selected finite-étale frame cannot make even its last increasing step to a prime. Iteration at fixed n only swaps the two factors. The numerical profile or selection of an endpoint does not affect either conclusion. This goes beyond multiaffinity (`Z70_CLASS.md` Remark V): the degree and number of candidate correspondences at the prescribed endpoint are unrestricted. No assertion that all arithmetic moves are algebraic and unramified there. F1–F4: no existence inference on squares, composites, R₈, Type A or any of the 27 Mordell zeros; in fact the no-go works identically on the 4 and 8 surfaces. No averaging or genus assumption and no B2 hypothesis. It explains failure of a *class of descent proofs*, not cessation of the zeros. A surviving possibility is a correspondence ramifying **inside** this open, with lifts arithmetic at every endpoint.
