### 1. The prime-to-p part of a prime-power tower has no inter-layer gluing obstruction

**Theorem 1 (PROVED, elementary).** Let p be an odd prime, h=p^j, H=2^h−1, B_a=1+2^a (indices mod h), and R_h the **full** subgroup generated by B_a/B_b on equal-gcd orbits. Write N_i=(2^{p^i}−1)/(2^{p^{i−1}}−1), 1≤i≤j. On the part of order prime to p, R_h is the *direct product* of its projections onto the N_i. In particular −1 belongs to R_h iff it belongs to every individual layer projection. This is a result about integral specialization, not a reflected-product identity at abstract roots of unity. It permits every gcd orbit; it need not split the unit-index-only group.

Proof. The layers are pairwise coprime: p never divides 2^{p^i}−1 (2^{p^i}≡2 mod p by Fermat), and at each prime divisor of N_i the order of 2 is precisely p^i. The B_a are units by odd order. For the last extension put d=p^{j−1}, M=2^d−1 and N=H/M. Assume j≥2. Every nontrivial old generator r=B_a/B_b can use 1≤a,b<d and gcd(a,d)=gcd(b,d), so it lifts to the new R and, conversely, each new generator projects into the old R. For the latter assertion equal gcd at h implies equal gcd at d, including the singleton zero orbit. Put

    W_a = (∏_{t=0}^{p−1} B_{a+td}) / B_{pa}.

The quotient W_a/W_b is legal: matched numerator indices have the same p-valuation (<v_p(d)), and pa,pb have equal gcd with h. In CRT coordinates this quotient is (r^p/r',1), where r'=B_{pa}/B_{pb} in the OLD ring. Indeed on N, w=2^d is a primitive p-th root at every residue prime; ∏(1+xw^t)=1+x^p there, including at prime powers (the differences of roots are units). On M all p lifts agree. Now apply the same construction at the **same extension** to r', reducing its two indices modulo d, and continue. Eventually both indices become zero mod d, the ratio is one and there is nothing to lift. Multiplication with powers p^{k−1},p^{k−2},…,1 cancels intermediate denominators and gives (r^{p^k},1)∈R_h, with k≤v_p(d). Explicitly, writing r_i=B_{p^ia}/B_{p^ib} on M and choosing the corresponding lift V_i=(r_i^p/r_{i+1},1), one has

    ∏_{i=0}^{k−1} V_i^{p^{k−1−i}} = (r_0^{p^k}/r_k,1) = (r^{p^k},1).

This finite chain is why no assumption on a discrete log of 2 mod p is needed. For an arbitrary old product one can use the common power p^{v_p(d)}.

If x in the old R has order prime to p, invert this power on ⟨x⟩: (x,1)∈R_h. Every prime-to-p element y in the new-layer image has a lift of order prime to p (remove the p-primary part by finite-group primary decomposition). Cancel its old component using the isolated old subgroup. Induction proves the direct product assertion. This argument isolates **old** signs even at 7,23,… without finding a sign on a new primitive layer. The analogous exponent is not invertible on a cubic obstruction at p=3. ∎

### 4. Round 2: the orbit calculation and the index it actually measures

**Theorem 6 (PROVED, elementary).** Put S=(ℤ/h)×, σa=2a, o=ord_h(2), t=φ(h)/o, and I=ker(ℤ[S]→ℤ) (sum of coefficients). The *formal* quotient controlling the telescope of differences of unit-index factors is

    I/(σ−1)I ≅ ℤ^{t−1} ⊕ ℤ/o.

In particular permutation by 2 does not, on its own, imply vanishing of the defect even when it is transitive. Choose one origin per σ-orbit. Map v∈I to its orbit sums (of total zero) and to Σ_{C,i} i v_{σ^i C} mod o, where C stands for the chosen origin. This is onto: differences of origins give the free coordinates; e_{σC}−e_C gives phase 1. If all orbit sums vanish we can solve v=(σ−1)y integrally around each cycle by successive differences. Then the phase of v is Σy mod o. Adding a constant to y on one orbit changes its sum by o and nothing in v. Thus y can have augmentation zero precisely when the phase vanishes. This proves the formula.

For specialization first take the **full** modulus H=2^h−1, D_a=2^a−1 (indices mod h), which is a unit when a∈S. Let C be generated by D_a/D_b for a,b∈S and R₁ by B_a/B_b there. The exponent map sends I onto C and (σ−1)I onto R₁, by D_{2a}/D_a=B_a. Hence C/R₁ is the formal quotient above **modulo the image of the specialization relations**. This description is exact, not an assertion that the free coordinates survive. Since D₁=1, all unit-index D_a belong to C. If 3∤h, B₃/B₁=3=B₁ kills the phase generator after evaluation, and all unit-index B_a lie in R₁. In each doubling orbit all D_a then agree modulo R₁. Since D_{−a}=−2^{−a}D_a and 2=B₁/B₋₁, negative orbits differ only by the sign. In particular, for 3∤h, C=R₁ in the transitive case; if S=±⟨2⟩ with two orbits then C/R₁ is generated by the sign and has order at most 2. No assertion about other units of H follows from this argument.

**Primitive layer refinement (PROVED).** Let h=p^j with p≥5 prime, and now compute in U(N)=(ℤ/N)×, N=Φ_h(2). On this layer the **entire** projected gcd-ratio group equals the projected R₁. Indeed for a=p^v b with p∤b, 0≤v<j, at N we have

    B_a = ∏_{t=0}^{p^v−1} B_{b+t p^{j−v}}.

All factors on the right have unit indices (and already belong to the projected R₁ since 3∤h). The displayed product is the odd-degree factorization ∏(1+xw^t)=1+x^{p^v}, valid here because 2 has order h at each prime of N and the roots differ by units. The zero-index orbit supplies no ratio. Write C_N for the projected C, so [U(N):proj R_h]=[U(N):C_N]·[C_N:proj R₁]. For example on **every** 5^j the second factor is exactly 1, since 2 has order 4·5^{j−1} by LTE; this is an exact index theorem *inside the specialized circular subgroup*, not in U(N).

For h=7^j or 23^j the order of 2 is p^{j−1}(p−1)/2 (LTE from orders 3,11 and nontrivial lifts at p²), odd. Here the second factor is 1 or 2, and one can replace its sign test by a rational-prime test: **p∈proj R₁ iff −1∈proj R₁**. In fact Q=⟨2⟩, D_a∈proj R₁ for a∈Q. Multiplying D_a over Q and −Q gives

    p = Φ_h(1) = −2^{−Σ_{a∈Q}a} (∏_{a∈Q} D_a)^2   (mod N).

Use the factorization over the primitive h-th roots at N (unit differences) and |Q| odd. Thus −p is even a square in proj R₁ since 2 has odd order. This identifies a rational test at every height; it does not establish membership of p. By Theorem 1, the bare sign belongs to R_h iff this rational-prime test passes on every layer up to j. The arguments here concern the ratio groups; they do not replace the exceptional-factor and density tests by an index statement.

### 5. Two exact statements on the full ambient group

**Theorem 7 (PROVED).** Let 3≤d|h be odd, M=2^d−1, and let A be the full part of H supported on primes dividing M (all their powers in H). In **U(A)** the image of R_h is exactly the full preimage of R_d under reduction to U(M). In particular its ambient index equals [U(M):R_d] at *every* multiple h of d, independently of how thick the prime powers become. This includes exact index 6 on 7^{1+v₇(h)}73^{1+v₇₃(h)} for 9|h (LTE; here d=9), and index 2 on the full 7-component for 3|h (d=3). For each of the 199 certified conductors d it propagates its exact index, on its old support, through infinitely many h.

Proof. Equal-gcd pairs reduce to equal-gcd pairs at d. Conversely a∈[1,d) of gcd g with d has a lift with gcd g with h: lift the unit a/g mod d/g to a unit modulo h/g and multiply by g. Thus the image at M is exactly R_d. Moreover 2^d∈R_h is already 1 mod M. At each q|M, if e=v_q(M) and E=v_q(H), then LTE gives ord_{q^E}(2^d)=q^{E−e}. As these orders are pairwise coprime their product is the kernel size A/M. Hence the entire thickness kernel is generated by this single element at A. This does **not** assert that its lifts have trivial coordinates at new primes. Exactly, with J=H/A, the full global index is the old index times [U(J):{y:(1,y)∈R_h in U(A)×U(J)}]. No distribution of CRT coordinates of an arbitrary global unit is assumed.

**Theorem 8 (PROVED, squarefree prime insertion).** Suppose h=pd, p odd prime, d>1 odd, p∤d(2^d−1), M=2^d−1, N=H/M. Put f=ord_d(p). In particular the **p-primary** part splits, in contrast with the prime-power extension of Theorem 1. This can be iterated where the hypotheses hold; it does not give unconditional 2-primary splitting at squarefree conductors.

Proof. gcd(M,N)=gcd(M,p)=1. For an old pair a,b with equal gcd with d, W_a/W_b of Theorem 1 still belongs to R_h, although the lifts a+td and b+td must now be **reordered**: exactly one index on each side is divisible by p, and the old gcd is constant on each list. **Noncontainment (PROVED rel. z78 §5).** For every m>11 with m≡11 mod τ of §6 the class P₉∣s is **not** contained in the unshifted stripped cyclotomic word domain, even allowing the prefactor c|k and unbounded degree. **Strengthening (PROVED).** Even after adjoining the dynamic gcd rule of §6 and any *fixed bounded* set of prime-divisor tests, these three mechanisms (off-zero fixed rows, these divisor rules, unshifted extraction) cannot cover every exponent for all large m of the progression.
