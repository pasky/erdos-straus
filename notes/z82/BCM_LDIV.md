## 2. The BCM tower already fails plain divisor successors

## 3. A necessary residue-field test even for infinitely many graded axes

**PROVED (rel. integer-part floors).** Let `D` be an end-initial signed subring of an integer part `R` of `K=rc(Frac R)`, `F=Frac D`. Let `H>0` in `R` be divisible by **each** positive `d∈D`. For example it follows from BCM at a bound above the proper initial subring. Write `k` for the residue field of the convex hull of `F` in `K` (positive units are of size between `1/c` and `c`, `c∈D⁺`). If every nonempty set of divisors of `H²` above an arithmetic threshold has a least element, then

```
 { res(t/H) : 0<t|H², t/H a relative unit }  =  k^{>0}.
```

Here residues of old scalars embed `F` in `k`. This surjectivity refers to the **order valuation**. For the proof `H` exceeds every member of `D` (otherwise `H∈D` and `H+1` cannot divide it). For every `f=a/b∈F^{>0}` we have an actual divisor `Hf=a(H/b)` of `H²`, with complementary divisor `b(H/a)`. If `z` is a relative unit, set `y=⌊Hz⌋∈R`. A least divisor `t>y` exists since `H²>y`. We have `t/H≥z`; and for each `d∈D⁺` there is `f∈F` with `z<f≤z+1/d` by rounding `dz` strictly upwards. That floor lies in `D` by relative boundedness and initiality. Thus `Hf>y` and `t/H≤f`. This proves the residue assertion (vary `d`), not actual equality of `t/H` to `z`. The much stronger immediate conclusion at a rational residue `z∈F⁺` is that the next divisor above the *exact* `Hz` must differ from it by a positive amount relatively infinitesimal compared to `H`, since `Hz` itself is disallowed there.

**PROVED (unit rank consequence).** As a test on a proposed graded architecture, localize to `U=(D∖0)⁻¹R[1/H]`. If `√2∉F` then `U* / F*` must have **infinite rational rank**; already the classes of positive divisors of `H²` normalized by `H` generate such rank. This only needs the plain threshold least principle just used. The residues include all `a+√2` for ordinary positive integers `a`. The embedded `F` has intersection `ℚ` with the copy of `ℚ(√2)` in `k` since any nonrational member generates that field. Take positive even ordinary `a_i≥4`, each divisible by all previously occurring odd prime factors of `a_j²−2`. Some new odd prime `l_i` divides `a_i²−2` since the latter has 2-adic order one and exceeds 2. At `l_i`, `√2` has distinct roots `±a_i` modulo `l_i`. In the split quadratic field places the two valuations of `a_i+√2` differ (one positive, one zero); for all other `a_j+√2` they are both zero, since their norms are units there, including later terms by the choice of `a_j`. Thus the classes of these positive residues are multiplicatively independent modulo `F*`. A relation among their lifts up to `F*` would reduce to one among residues. ## 4. What can be localized about numerical leastness?

**PROVED (rel. z80 §§1,7,18; guarded prime reduction in `IE₁⁻`).** Let `V(s)` be any parameter-free `E₁` predicate.
