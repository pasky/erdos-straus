## 9. Horizontal descent of a separable binary configuration

**PROVED (rel. purity of branch locus on smooth varieties in characteristic zero, the geometric étale fundamental group of affine space and of a smooth affine three-dimensional sphere, and §3.2).** Let `A` be any finite upper stage used here, including joint polynomial edges. A binary form of degree `d≥3` with coefficients there and discriminant in `K*` is `H∘T` for `T∈SL₂(A)` and `H` over `K`. This assertion is about the *upper* algebra; it is not by itself an arithmetic equivalence.

**Constant splitting field.** In the function field of the stage, take the finite Galois splitting extension of the geometric roots in the projective line. It is unramified at every height-one prime of the upper algebra: the zero scheme in the projective line of a form with unit discriminant is finite étale (over each DVR pass to a chart avoiding the residual roots, then the monic separable criterion; the residue fields contain the infinite `K`). Taking the Galois splitting field preserves this property. More generally any finite Galois extension unramified at those primes becomes trivial after extending the constants to `K^alg`. To see this, remove the last edge. Its generic fibre over the previous function field `E₀` is affine space, an affine line on a row, or a smooth affine rank-four sphere at nonzero target. All of its height-one primes are height-one primes of the total ring after localization. Purity on this smooth generic fibre makes the normalization in the extension finite étale (finiteness by finite type over the field). Over an algebraic closure its geometric fundamental group is trivial. For the sphere one may reduce to the complex quadric `Σz_i²=1`: writing `z=x+iy` identifies it with the tangent bundle of `S³` by `x=√(1+|y|²)e`, `|e|=1`, `y⊥e`. It retracts to the simply connected `S³`. Use Riemann existence and invariance of the geometric étale fundamental group under algebraically closed extension in characteristic zero (the split quadric is defined over the algebraic numbers); affine space is simpler. Thus the finite Galois cover over `E₀` is from a finite Galois **constant extension of `E₀`**, by the fundamental group exact sequence, since the generic fibre is geometrically connected.

This smaller extension is itself unramified at every old prime divisor. Each old prime element persists prime and with multiplicity one; the new height-one DVR above it restricts with index one. A ramified old extension would enlarge its value group at any prolongation (the smaller extension is Galois, so all its prolongations have the same index). This contradicts unramifiedness upstairs by restricting a prolongation in the composite field. Residue characteristic is zero, so no inseparable residual obstruction at these DVRs. Descend these *divisorially unramified fields*, not necessarily covers on singular total spaces. At the seed the same affine-line argument ends at `K`. Consequently a finite Galois scalar extension `L/K` splits the binary form. All our upper algebras after any such scalar extension remain UFDs with precisely the coefficient-field units: apply §3.2 over `L`, using finite flat integrality for the row pivots, geometric integrality/persistence of primes and monic sphere contraction. The stages are geometrically integral (also after base change) by the same generic-fibre and chart arguments.

**Descent matrix.** We need a scalar point of the finite upper stage, *not* a specialization of the completed arithmetic ring. Such a point over `K` exists even with the sphere edges. In detail each finite stage has Zariski equations given by its successive charts. The generic ordered realization has all sphere targets positive and meets a smooth real chart on every earlier edge. Existence of such a simultaneous real point descends by real-closed field elementarity to `rc(K)`. The `K`-points are dense in the real topology on these positive-target charts: `K` is order-dense in `rc(K)` by PA floors after scaling. On a polynomial edge approximate freely, on a row vary the base in its nonzero-pivot open and solve for the pivot coordinate continuously. On a sphere vary the base slightly and choose a nearby real point on the new fibre by multiplying the old point by the positive square root of the target ratio. Every positive `K`-target is a sum of four squares in `K` by scalar PA (clear a square denominator); projection from such a `K`-point gives a rational parametrization, with dense `K`-points on that real sphere. This lifting of arbitrarily close approximants proves density inductively on the finite opens, keeping all requested strict inequalities. Fix a resulting scalar point `o`. Nothing here requires its arithmetic integrality or the ordered generic parameters to be algebraic.

Over `A_L` factor `G` into linear rows `l_i` times a unit. Indeed the UFD factorization and the discriminant product formula show the scalar factor and **every** two-row determinant are units, hence in `L*`. In the basis of the first two rows every other row has coefficients in `L` by Cramer's rule. With `E` the matrix of those first two rows, set `T=E(o)⁻¹E`. It has determinant one, specializes to identity, and `l_i=l_i(o)T` for all `i`. Hence `G=G(o)∘T`. Galois permutes the rows up to *constant* factors: another choice of the first two rows, reordered and rescaled that way, changes `E` by left multiplication by a constant matrix. Thus `T` is invariant and has entries in `A`. This use of the third and further distinct roots (constant mutual determinants) is valid for degree at least three; stable-reduction descent at scalar places below is not a two-root argument.

## 10. The arithmetic change of basis and the scalar window

is arithmetic and has the original nonzero discriminant in `C`. Choose `b₀∈B` such that **all coefficients of `Ψ_b₀` are in `B`**: these are fixed finite polynomials in `b₀` with `K` coefficients, and after clearing one scalar denominator this is a system of polynomial congruences mod that denominator. `b` witnesses it valuation-wise; z81 §12 with scalar parameters (§3.1) gives a scalar witness. In general, over a valued field, if two integral binary forms of degree ≥3 with unit discriminant are related by `M∈SL₂`, then `M` has integral entries.
