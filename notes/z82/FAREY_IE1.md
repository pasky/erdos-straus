## 2. One short vector suffices for the whole local Farey bracket

### 3.1 Precision for the scalar part

### 4.1 A vertical specialization that does not kill the indeterminate P

## 6. A PA-host restriction

**PROVED (rel. discrete order, §2).** Suppose a signed `IOpen + Bézout` ring `S` is an ordered subring of a PA integer ring `Bₕ`; view its ordered real closure in that of the host. Let `Th(n)` hold in `S`, `Q=⌊√n⌋≥1`. A reduced host fraction `i/j∈[0,1]`, `1≤j≤Q`, within **strict distance** `1/(jQ)` of a real-closure target `t∈[0,1]` of `S` must be a fraction in `S` (in fact both reduced coordinates are in `S`). Take the exact point or the two endpoints of the level-`Q` bracket at `t`. An exact point of denominator ≤`Q` different from `i/j` has distance ≥`1/(jQ)`. In a strict bracket, `i/j` cannot lie strictly inside by unimodularity and the denominator sum, valid also in the *host*. If it is strictly outside, its distance from the nearer endpoint is ≥`1/(jQ)`; hence it must be an endpoint. A Bézout row for each reduced endpoint stays a row in the host, so equality of reduced pairs means equality coordinatewise. Conversely its own fraction gives distance zero whenever `i,j∈S`.

**CONDITIONAL on host ES at P.** Suppose `S⊂Bₕ` of this kind omits `ES(P)`, `P` infinite, and has `Th(P)`. Some host integer `1≤j≤Q` is missing: otherwise all base-`Q` digits are present, hence all host integers below every fixed ordinary power of `Q` are present by the PA fixed-length radix expansion. This includes the polynomially bounded ES denominators at `P` (`z≤2P⁴` suffices, `LOGIC_COLLECTION.md` §1); no PA exponentiation in `S` was needed. For such a missing `j`, every real-closure element of `S` is separated from `1/j` by at least `1/(jQ)` inside `[0,1]`. Thus a host whose real closure is densely approximated by that of the subring, even just at this precision on these reciprocal targets, cannot give the proposed countermodel. The same radix argument excludes a convex cut containing `P` without any `Th` assumption. Purely algebraic extensions inside the ordered real closure of a PA *coefficient* field cannot add an independent arithmetic `P` either: the old PA integers already floor it, so a new discrete integer there would equal its floor. These are restrictions on architectures, not an exclusion of nonconvex subrings in general. Host ES is automatic in an elementary extension of **true arithmetic** if ES holds in ℕ; no claim that truth alone implies PA-provability.

**Assessment (remaining target).** We have crossed the discriminant `−4` barrier, also at variable discriminants involving the failing prime, and crossed the *named* rational slope gap without making its residue rational. The unbuilt edge is simultaneous shortness at `P` for every in-model residue, including those introduced after the repair. By §2 that is exactly a full Farey level for arbitrary targets. Sections 3–5 are nonstandard model results and §6 is a necessary condition only. Neither a proof of ES in `IE₁⁻` nor a countermodel of that theory has been obtained. ES in ℕ remains open.
