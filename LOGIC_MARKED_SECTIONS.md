# Polynomial sections of a row tower with prescribed finite-place values

Result: marked polynomial sections of polynomial/row towers exist under the exact-zero condition (E) (not for arbitrary marks: explicit counterexample); they give the ES profile criterion in adelically marked models, and characterize which cylinders contain an ES-failing nonstandard prime of some external IOpen PID. **Verdict.** The constant-parameter proof works for parsimonious marks. A two-step polynomial correction works under the strictly weaker *exact-zero* hypothesis (E) below, even when a polynomial vanishes **modulo** infinitely many primes. No such theorem holds for arbitrary fixed marks. There is a separate, unavoidable parsimony restriction on which profiles an external PID model can realize; a marked-section result alone does not extend all equivalences of Z65 Theorem D.

## Statement

Use the original tower over the affine line with coordinate P, not its square pullback. Write T for the independent variable on the source line. A section means a ℚ-morphism from A¹_T with **P↦T identically**. At each of finitely many steps adjoin a polynomial variable or a row `aU+bV=1` whose missing center C has codimension ≥2. Write Y for the resulting affine variety. Fix W closed of codimension ≥2, J any proper closed locus in Y over ℚ, and integral marks π_l∈ℤ_l. Assume

    (E)  for every nonzero f∈ℚ[T], f(π_l)=0 in ℚ_l for only finitely many l.

It suffices, in particular, that every π_l is transcendental over ℚ. Alternatively, valuation zero almost everywhere for every nonzero f implies (E).

At finitely many primes prescribe open neighborhoods **in the fibers over P=π_l** of (Y−W)(ℚ_l); elsewhere require an integral point of a fixed spread-out model avoiding W on reduction at that mark. Require this package to be locally nonempty at each prime. Then there exists a section s with s⁻¹(W) empty over **all** of A¹ over ℚbar, generic image not in J, and s(π_l) in the required sets everywhere. This asserts integrality at the marks, **not** integrality of all polynomial coefficients. In particular the base coordinate cannot be moved by approximation, and the generic image, not the whole section, avoids J. For example a section cannot miss the vertical divisor P=0 everywhere.

## Proof by induction

On the line take P=T. The permissible local points are already fixed, W is empty and no proper J contains the line. At the final line-bundle step π:Y→X, over X−C write Z₁ for the locus where the whole geometric line fiber is in W. Vanishing of all coefficients of defining equations in the line coordinate on the two row charts describes this closed locus. Put Z=C∪closure_X(Z₁), of codimension ≥2 by dimension of the fibers. The closure D of π(W) is proper (dimension ≤dim X−1). The closure of the analogous whole-fiber locus for J over X−C is proper too, though J itself may dominate X.

Use the model and local preparations of `LOGIC_ROW_APPROXIMATION.md`: off finitely many primes spread *both* radical inclusions identifying the chart coefficient-zero loci with Z off C. Invert the finitely many original denominators; place all primes ≤ the maximal chart degree in the exceptional set S. At all places added to S retain the original admissible conditions. At each l∈S a row chart gives an open box above a base neighborhood **inside the fixed marked fiber of X** whose points lift into the required neighborhood. Outside S original admissible points project to admissible reductions outside Z. Apply induction to get a section x of X missing Z geometrically, satisfying these marked base conditions, whose generic image is outside D and the whole-fiber locus for J. Additional forbidden generic loci are dealt with by finite union; X is integral.

The restricted row entries a(x),b(x) have no common geometric zero, hence generate ℚ[T]. Choose a particular polynomial solution and parametrize all lifts by λ∈ℚ[T] times `(b(x),−a(x))` added to that solution. This is a trivialization over the **whole** source line, with primitive direction (for a product use its free coordinate). It also gives affine-line coordinates over every ℚ_l at π_l. In `(T,λ)` coordinates let W' be the pullback of W. Its possible T-values are contained in a finite closed subset since x is generically outside D. Choose a nonzero f∈ℤ[T] vanishing on this subset (1 if empty). Over its finitely many geometric roots each forbidden λ-fiber is finite, since x misses Z everywhere. After excluding finitely many primes the section x, particular solution and syzygy are integral polynomials, and `W'⊂V(f)` holds on reductions as well: a power of f belongs to the defining ideal of W' over ℚ[T,λ], and one can clear denominators. All subsequent finite enlargements concern the parameter in this **already fixed** fiber; the base section is not chosen again.

At any prime not in the original S, the marked base row is primitive integrally (x avoids C on reduction there), and outside Z the degree bound excludes fewer than l residue-line points. Hence an admissible integral lift exists at that prime, even if the particular polynomial solution has denominators there. At S the projected open boxes supply lifts. Thus at each newly treated prime there is a nonempty open set of allowed λ in ℚ_l; it is not necessary that λ itself be integral at a denominator prime.

*Short proof under parsimony.* Include among the finitely many exceptional primes the divisors of f(π_l), i.e. the l where its valuation is not zero. Elsewhere every integral parameter works. Weak approximation with denominators only at the exceptional primes gives a rational constant λ meeting the local opens and integral outside. There are infinitely many choices; exclude the finitely many constants forbidden at geometric roots of f, and the finitely many which put the generic point in J (a proper line subset over ℚ(T)).

*Repair under (E).* Let S₁ comprise S, the denominator and bad-spreading primes of the preceding paragraph(s), and **all exact zeros** f(π_l)=0. Choose a rational constant λ₀ meeting all S₁ conditions, integral elsewhere, and excluding the finite geometric forbidden values. This preliminary section misses W over ℚbar for every T. Hence it is a good integral section over ℤ[1/S₂] for some finite S₂⊇S₁: after substitution of λ₀, the defining polynomials of W' generate the unit ideal over ℚ[T]; clear denominators in a Bézout identity for 1. This is uniform in T, not an appeal to f(π_l) being a unit almost everywhere.

Now use the polynomial parameter **λ(T)=λ₀+f(T)c**, with c a single rational constant. At S₁ require a sufficiently small c so the old conditions still hold. At each l∈S₂−S₁, f(π_l) is nonzero *in the field*, so some open set of c gives admissible lifts there, regardless of v_l(f(π_l)). Choose c meeting these finitely many opens and integral outside S₂ by CRT with denominators. For l∉S₂ the new section remains integral; if f(π_l) is nonzero on reduction, W' is absent, and if f(π_l) is zero on reduction then the section reduces to the good preliminary section. At the geometric roots of f the parameter is unchanged exactly; elsewhere W' is absent. Finally the generic J-fiber is proper over ℚ(T); the λ₀+fc for different constants c are distinct there, so only finitely many c need be excluded. This proves the assertion. No bound on how many primes divide a nonzero marked f-value is needed in this second proof.

**Failure without control of marks.** Take π_l=1 for every l, Y=A²_(P,y), W=V(P−1,y²+3), with ordinary integral avoidance at every prime. Locally choose y=0 at l≠3 and y=1 at 3. A global marked section would have y(1) rational and integral everywhere, hence an integer. Its positive integer y(1)²+3≥3 must have a prime divisor, violating avoidance there. The same example can even make W empty at the top: impose the row `(P−1)U+(y²+3)V=1` and demand marked integrality of its coordinates. No amount of polynomial interpolation elsewhere changes this fixed rational fiber. This disproves the unrestricted lemma, not necessity of (E) in every individual tower.

## ES consequences and limits

For an adelically integral stage-model as in `Z73_PID_SQUARE.md`, use the original parameter P (not t²). A hypothetical ES triple at the ordered positive infinite P is an identity in the finite-stage algebra. Prescribe integrality of the three coordinate values at the marks: spread the regular functions integrally outside a finite set, and use their given local model evaluations inside that set. On the original real tower over P>0 the locus is semialgebraically connected by the same codimension-two deletion and real line-bundle argument as on the square pullback. The positive sector of an ES identity is clopen there (each positive coordinate ≥P/4); thus the polynomial triple along a section is positive for every real T>0. This is special to the equation, not a claim about arbitrary inequalities.

If G₁,G₂,G₃∈ℚ[T] are those polynomials and D clears their coefficients, then for every positive integer n≡π mod D, each G_i(n) is integral: at l|D the congruence makes G_i(n)−G_i(π_l) l-integral and at l∤D this is automatic. Since P maps to **T**, the triple solves the identity at n, not at a polynomial reparametrization of n. Conversely polynomial solvability of a class containing a unit profile π supplies an ES triple in its model: eventual integer values on the progression imply integer values everywhere on it by finite differences and on the corresponding l-adic progressions by continuity; positive leading behavior gives positivity at P. Thus the Z65 Prop. 5.5 **profile criterion** extends to these marked models. This direction uses only the original tower and (E) plus local feasibility, not the square pullback or its Type I/II test.

For a **fixed external PID profile** one needs more than the section lemma. If all π_l are transcendental and every nonzero f∈ℤ[T] has valuation zero at almost all π_l, the parsimony construction of `Z73_PID_SQUARE.md` extends this fixed P-coordinate to the remaining transcendence basis: for each multivariate nonzero polynomial choose one nonzero coefficient polynomial in P; its specialization stays nonzero modulo almost all l. Finite-prefix zero-set avoidance in the remaining variables and local algebraic independence over ℚ(π_l) now work. For a unit profile this gives the normalized prime P and the external PID as in that proof, with the exact profile criterion.

These restrictions are intrinsic to an external PID realization of a nonstandard P (even apart from this construction). Such a P is transcendental over ℚ by ordered root bounds and discreteness. Every nonzero f(P), f∈ℤ[T], has a finite prime factorization, so involves just finitely many *standard* primes and has finite multiplicity at each. Congruences of P modulo standard prime powers imply the corresponding congruences for f(P). Thus f(π_l) is **never exactly zero** and is an l-adic unit almost everywhere. Here standard primes are irreducible by discreteness. In particular an arbitrary uncovered profile cannot simply be assigned to P in a PID. In particular the existence in a cylinder of a **nonstandard prime counterexample in some external PID model of IOpen** is equivalent to that cylinder containing a parsimonious, componentwise-transcendental unit profile in U: necessity is intrinsic parsimony plus solvability of covered profiles in IOpen; sufficiency uses the constructed PID and the section criterion. (Sufficiency is a sketch: the adaptation of `Z73_PID_SQUARE.md` §2 with φ_l(P)=π_l fixed — one nonzero ℚ[P]-coefficient per polynomial, parsimony of π for that coefficient, Schwartz–Zippel in the remaining variables, algebraic independence over ℚ(π_l) — is described above but not written out in full. z74 Opus xreview fix) This existential statement about external models is not a first-order provability dichotomy. Finite covers by polynomially solvable classes still suffice for provability as in Z65 Thm D. To deduce the *full converse for ordinary residue classes* by this PID construction one would additionally need, whenever the class cylinder meets the uncovered set U, a parsimonious componentwise-transcendental member of that intersection (or a different argument). Density in the whole cylinder, without density **in U**, does not prove it. External PID is a semantic restriction, not a first-order axiom to which compactness applies. No unrestricted class dichotomy is claimed here.
