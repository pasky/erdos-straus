# z40 memo 3 — menu II-d (one constant d, moving a): the trinomial escape route, as far as tested (O270)

* Identity (Type II, any polynomial a): (4abd − n)(4acd − 1) = n + 4a²d. With d constant and a(Y) non-constant, B = n + 4da²
  is a trinomial-type polynomial (no Capelli), and validity needs a | f′ + 1, i.e. f′(y₀) = −1 at roots of a.
* Y = 1 data: (C2) with A = a(1) ∈ ℤ[1/2]; for A, d odd the targets are the 39 boxed solutions. Y = 0 data: 2-adic.
* Even D: a must have no real roots (EXP_15 Lemma 3.1) — so the first honest II-d shapes for D = 4 (where Sophie Germain
  lives) are quadratic a without real roots.
* Odd D, linear a, quadratic factor: a 2-parameter algebraic family; all rational points found (height ≤ 40, or seeded)
  fail 2-adic integrality at Y = 0, typically through a(0) ∈ ½ℤ (y₀ = −1). Reading: the Y = 0 specialisation (n → 1)
  is where II-d dies, as for the rung-2 searches of MENU_LADDER §5.
