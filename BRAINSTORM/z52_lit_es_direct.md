## §1 Verified entries

### New arXiv item in the sweep

**Yichuan Cao and Xiaoping Xu, “Prime Numbers of the Form 24m+1 and Erdös-Straus Conjecture,” arXiv:2610.32302v1, submitted 2026-10-23 (13 pp.); accessed 2026-10-30.** The arXiv abstract and full HTML were accessed. The paper gives 56 polynomial families and says the numerical test suggests they imply ES; in §3 it explicitly states the covering claim “We have used AI to test the claim for m≤10¹¹ and it holds. But we failed to get a logic proof.” Thus this is a computationally tested conjectural covering, not an ES proof; the paper itself identifies the missing step. It additionally gives analogous numerical evidence for Howerton’s 14-wild-prime conjecture, §4, and a modulo-24 Goldbach conjecture, §5. Status: unrefereed v1 preprint; no ES proof supported. Source excerpt: `sources/z52/es_direct/2610.32302.txt`. Relation: earlier Xu family work appears in `LITERATURE_2026_UPDATE.md`; `git grep -il '2610.32302' -- '*.md'` found no older record.

**Shisheng Li, “Unit fractions with semiprime denominators: an elementary proof of Erdős Problem #306,” arXiv:2609.32140v1, submitted 2026-09-26; accessed 2026-10-30.** The abstract states that every positive rational a/b with b squarefree is a finite sum of distinct unit fractions, each denominator a product of two distinct primes. Structural proof: construct subgraphs of a complete bipartite graph on prime intervals; count reciprocal sums congruent to a/b mod 1 by a finite Fourier expansion; small integer frequencies give a positive main term, other frequencies are controlled by divisor counting and a no-wrap-around CRT; a small-total-mass bound forces equality. Abstract states the proof is formalized in Lean 4 except a cited Ramanujan inequality and describes substantial AI contribution to construction, experiments, and writing. It is not a proof of ES: neither the number of terms nor the denominator pattern is the three-term 4/p constraint. Status: v1 preprint. Important status discrepancy: the teorth database README still lists #306 open and the rendered #306 page does not discuss this preprint; see §4. Source excerpt: `sources/z52/es_direct/2609.32140.txt`. Relation: #306 appears in repo sources from the current tagged-unit-fraction inventory, but `git grep -il '2609.32140' -- '*.md'` found no prior citation.
