# bsK_skolem_dictionary — ES exponent coverings ⟺ an exponential local–global principle (O185)
**Claim (dictionary PROVED in EXPONENT_COVERING Thm 1.4/Prop 2.2/§3; principle CONJECTURE).** For n_m = k·2^m + 1, a finite covering of
all large m by fixed ES slices (ET classes + divisor classes) exists iff (EH_k) "k·2^x + 1 = (unit)² has a solution mod every M only if
it has one in Z with y = ±2^j" holds AND Λ_k has no sterile non-square point. (EH_k) is a mixed (exponential + square) Hasse principle
analogous to (not an instance of; R185 repair) Skolem / Bertók–Hajdu (arXiv:1407.6499). Global solutions: k = 4^j − 1 only (Cor 1.5).
**Screen.** B1: not violated (the family is not dense in a square class). B5/T1: respected (unit-square points are exactly the obstruction).
B2/B3/B6: irrelevant (no divisor lottery is used; members are forced non-squares at a fixed finite prime set).
**Why it might matter.** Bertók–Hajdu prove their principle for "almost all" equations; an analogous "almost all k" theorem for (EH_k)
plus a sterile-set argument would give ES for k·2^m + 1 for almost all k — a density-zero but infinite-dimensional family of ES results.
**Promise for ES itself: 1/5** (does not touch unit squares mod everything small).
