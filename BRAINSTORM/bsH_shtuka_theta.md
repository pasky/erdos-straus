# bsH_shtuka_theta — higher theta series / Shtukas with r legs: the function-field Kudla program has any number of derivatives but still quadratic parity (O181)

**ES is not solved here.** Literature memo. No script.

## Idea
KUDLA_ES: T1 = Diff-parity for the incoherent ternary collection (first derivative of an Eisenstein series). Over
function fields Yun–Zhang and Feng–Yun–Zhang interpret the r-th derivative as degrees of special cycles on moduli
of Shtukas with r legs, for every r. Is r an order-k "parity"?
## Literature (from memory, not re-checked)

theta series for unitary groups over function fields* (arXiv 2021) and *Higher Siegel–Weil formula for unitary
groups: the non-singular terms*, Invent. Math. (2024).
## Analysis (Assessment)
The number of legs r is the order of vanishing, constrained by the root number ±1: parity of r is the
incoherence (Landherr/Hilbert), still 2-torsion; the groups are unitary (Hermitian), whose Kottwitz incoherence
is ±1 (R177 D2c). The order-k incoherence of PGL₁(D) (DRINFELD_ES §6) is not the one these theorems use. And
DRINFELD_ES Thm 2.2 shows ES_q data never meet the order-k part anyway.
