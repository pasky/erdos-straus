# z20_unit_criterion — Type I families live on units of ℚ[x]/(P) (O248)
## Idea
Write Type I as C(4ABD − 1) = P(A + B). Either P ∤ 4ABD − 1 (then 4ABD − 1 | A + B: degree kills it) or A, B, D are units
mod P with 4ABD ≡ 1.
## Result (NORMFORM_CUBIC Thm 3.1, Cor 3.2; PROVED)
If P is prime and (ℚ[x]/(P))^× = ℚ^× (every homogeneous prime form), every Type I family valid at ONE point lies in ℚ[P]; the
only other solutions are one explicit never-valid family (B = b, D = −1/(4b²), C = −bP). With Thm 1.1′: polynomial ET
families for any norm form / irreducible binary form, valid anywhere, are ET identities in n. Settles POINTWISE_BINARYFORM
§4.3 (indefinite cusps) for ET families; no cone, positivity region or Hecke needed.
