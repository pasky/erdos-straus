2. **Binary Goldbach (stuck mirror)** [`BG`, 3 items] — *before:* Chudakov, van der Corput, Estermann 1938: almost all even n; Montgomery-Vaughan 1975: exceptional set <= N^{1-delta} — *after:* STILL OPEN for every n (verified to 4e18, Oliveira e Silva-Herzog-Pardi 2014) — *last step:* Not achieved: two prime variables give a main term ~ n/log^2 n but the minor arcs cannot be bounded pointwise in n (only in mean square).
