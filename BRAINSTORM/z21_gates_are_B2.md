# z21 — every Astra gate is one B2 event; what would beat it (O249 spark memo)

Status: memo. Source: ASTRA_BRIDGE §6 (table 6.1, Props 6.1, 6.2; Thm 2.1 memorylessness).
Astra relaxes the class (coset −4⁻¹K at a Type I level), Claude relaxes the box (exponents b_s + e at a window). Both
leave a divisor-in-class event at a solution-dependent modulus. Saturation (forced whole-subgroup covering) only fires at levels
e ≤ p^{O(1/log log p)} (Prop 6.1); hitting a single target in a small box is NOT excluded.
Sparks:
1. A *discrete-log* forcing principle: something that forces the target's exponent vector (not just membership)
   into a small box. Known instances: algebraic factorisations (Sophie Germain, (J)74) on thin families. Could an
   Astra-style genealogy produce a *family* of levels e_t, H_t along which the discrete log of −4⁻¹ is a fixed
   polynomial in t (so a single identity covers the family)? That is B1 unless the family is non-congruential.
2. Astra's composite-q genealogies (5209, 15241) supply levels e with H divisible by inherited factors from q. If
   inherited factors could be made to have *small order* mod the next level (ord_e(ℓ) ≤ v_ℓ(H)), the difficulty met in
   §6.2's construction (Assessment) disappears. Requires e | ℓ^k − 1 with e ≡ −p⁻¹ (mod ℓ^k): a congruence on p — check whether the genealogy's freedom
   (choice of q, m) can absorb it.
