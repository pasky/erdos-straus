# bsE_q3_explicit_constants — what is missing at q = 3 (and at r = 13, 17) (O178)

## Idea
Thm 2.5 and Thm 3.1 show that the *exponents* already beat the measure. At q = 3 (and r = 17) the obstruction is the divisor
constant. In F_q[T] divisor sums over arithmetic progressions mod T^s of length ≥ q^s are exact: Σ_{deg M = λ} d(1 + T^s M) =
Σ_D #{…} with exact terms q^{λ−deg D} for deg D ≤ λ. Replace max-divisor bounds by these averaged identities in the four
reconstruction maps, pattern by pattern, and combine with an exact union census U(m) for small s.
