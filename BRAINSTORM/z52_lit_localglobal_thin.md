# Local-global failures, thin groups, and reciprocity obstructions

## §0 Summary
- **Genuinely new since 2026-09-28:** no source-verified theorem found that changes the campaign; Shkredov and Zhang's 2026 Zaremba preprints are significant updates to the analogue literature, already recorded in `BRAINSTORM/z31_zaremba_thin_orbits.md`.
- **Transferable structural features:** expansion proves pointwise Zaremba only with a power-size, dimension-tunable menu; Chen's Markoff result combines a giant orbit with a divisibility lower bound, not an existence count; thin-group reciprocity can exclude structured families but does not create ES certificates.
- **Campaign verdict changes:** none. ES remains open; no verified every-prime representation theorem with polylogarithmic or O(1) expected count found.
## §1 Verified entries

### 1. Thin Apollonian local-global failure / reciprocity
**Citation:** J. Haag, A. Kertzer, J. Rickards, K. E. Stange, “The local-global conjecture for Apollonian circle packings is false,” *Annals of Mathematics* 200 (2024), arXiv:2307.02749, v3 PDF; source excerpt accessed 2026-10-18. **Statement:** The abstract states that the local-global conjecture is false for many packings: certain quadratic and quartic families are missed, through properties of the thin Apollonian group and quadratic/quartic reciprocity. The source's Theorem 1.2 gives density one, with O(N^(1−η)) missing; the conjecture that the remaining sporadic set is finite is open (Conjecture 1.5). **Status:** Published; local-global conjecture not proved in full. **Campaign relation:** `BRAINSTORM/apollonian_reciprocity.md`, `BRAINSTORM/thin_group_orbits.md` (prior mechanism and ES comparison; no changed verdict).

### 2. Reciprocity obstructions in semigroup orbits (including continued fractions)
**Citation:** J. Rickards, K. E. Stange, “Reciprocity obstructions in semigroup orbits in SL(2,Z),” arXiv:2401.01860v3 (2025-01-23), *Duke Mathematical Journal* (2025 advance publication); source excerpt accessed 2026-10-18. **Statement:** Abstract: semigroup orbits can avoid squares by reciprocity, not merely algebraic-set or congruence obstructions; examples include thin semigroups associated with finite-alphabet continued fractions, and the paper disproves Bourgain–Kontorovich's proposed generalization in that setting. **Status:** Published/advance publication. This is an exclusion result for specified orbits, not a full Zaremba result. **Campaign relation:** `BRAINSTORM/apollonian_reciprocity.md`, `BRAINSTORM/z31_zaremba_thin_orbits.md`, `BRAINSTORM/z36_reciprocity_obstruction_home.md` (already mapped; no new ES invariant).

### 3. Zaremba: almost-all theorem and 2026 pointwise claims
**Citation:** J. Bourgain, A. Kontorovich, “On Zaremba’s conjecture,” *Annals of Mathematics* 180 (2014), arXiv:1107.3776; source pp. 2–8 accessed 2026-10-18. **Statement:** Theorem 1.8: for δ_A > δ₀, denominators occur for almost every admissible integer, with a power-saving exceptional proportion and multiplicity ≫ N^(2δ_A−1001/1000). The paper says individual error control of size N^(2δ−1−ε) would imply the full conjecture, but establishes the requisite error bound only on average (equation (1.37)). **Status:** Published; almost-all, not all denominators. **Relation:** `BRAINSTORM/z31_zaremba_thin_orbits.md`, `BRAINSTORM/thin_group_orbits.md`.

**Citation:** I. D. Shkredov, “On some results of Korobov and Larcher and Zaremba's conjecture,” arXiv:2603.14116v2 (2026-06-24); arXiv abstract/source record accessed 2026-10-21. **Statement:** Abstract claims Zaremba for prime denominators, with broader results under stated conditions; v2 says appendix supplies a crude bound on the absolute M in the prime case. **Status:** Preprint; theorem-proof details not independently re-audited here, so treat as a claim, not an established result. **Relation:** `BRAINSTORM/z31_zaremba_thin_orbits.md` already records the claim and mechanism; no new transfer.

**Citation:** Xin Zhang, “Expansion in SL₂(Z/qZ) and Zaremba's conjecture,” arXiv:2605.02518v2 (2026-05-08); arXiv abstract/source record accessed 2026-10-21. **Statement:** Abstract says the paper develops expansion for SL₂(Z/qZ), incorporates it into Shkredov's framework, and “confirm[s] Zaremba's conjecture” (bounded partial quotients for every denominator). **Status:** Preprint; no independent proof verification or later acceptance/refutation established here. **Relation:** `BRAINSTORM/z31_zaremba_thin_orbits.md` already records the claim and its conditional transfer screen.

### 4. Markoff strong approximation: pointwise orbit-size mechanism
**Citation:** W. Y. Chen, “A proof of the Markoff conjecture,” arXiv:2011.12940 (2020), *Annals of Mathematics* 199 (2024); source pp. 6–7 excerpt accessed 2026-10-18. **Statement:** Theorem 1.2.3 (Bourgain–Gamburd–Sarnak): for every ε>0, the exceptional primes up to x are O(x^ε), and the complement of a largest Γ-orbit in X*(p) has size ≤ p^ε. Theorem 1.2.5: every Γ-orbit on X*(p) has cardinality divisible by p. Theorem 1.2.6: the exceptional set is finite and explicitly bounded; outside it there is strong approximation. **Status:** Published. Pointwise conclusion follows because a nonempty orbit has at least p elements while the complement to the giant orbit is at most p^ε; this proves transitivity for all sufficiently large p, with explicit finite treatment. It is an orbit-connectivity statement on a fibre with ≍p² points, not existence of an integral point on every varying surface. **Relation:** `BRAINSTORM/z31_markoff_chen.md`, `MARKOFF_ES.md`, `BRAINSTORM/markoff_hasse_failures_vs_es.md` (mechanism and axis distinction already documented).

### 5. Markoff integral Hasse principle is not pointwise
**Citation:** A. Ghosh, P. Sarnak, “Integral points on Markoff type cubic surfaces,” arXiv:1706.06712; *Inventiones Mathematicae* 229 (2022); arXiv abstract excerpt accessed 2026-10-18. **Statement:** Abstract says the Hasse principle holds for almost all parameters k, and fails for infinitely many k. **Status:** Published; the precise quantitative failure bound and theorem numbering were not verified from the primary source in this lane. **Relation:** `LITERATURE_AUTOMORPHIC`, `BRAINSTORM/z31_markoff_chen.md`, `BRAINSTORM/markoff_hasse_failures_vs_es.md`; supports the existing warning that varying-family integral existence is distinct from Chen's fixed-fibre connectivity.
