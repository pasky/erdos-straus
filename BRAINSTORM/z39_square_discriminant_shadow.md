# z39_square_discriminant_shadow — ES level forms are square-discriminant (split) forms: the "shadow sector" of Zagier's ℋ (O269)

**ES is not solved here.** Labels as in DISCOVERIES.md.

## Idea
In Zagier's weight-3/2 Eisenstein series ℋ = Σ H(n)q^n the obstruction to modularity (the shadow, θ) is supported
on squares, and in the theta-lift interpretation (Bruinier–Funke; Funke) the non-holomorphic terms q^{−m²} come from
lattice vectors of square discriminant — split binary forms, infinite geodesics. ES words are, level by level,
representations by the split form t·uv (discriminant t², a square). Is ES "living in the shadow sector", and could
the T1 squares be a shadow phenomenon?

## Findings
1. (Thm K PROVED; the reading is Assessment) For the particular split form tuv − u − v, the difference of the two
   cone-restricted quadrant series is a Kronecker theta quotient and the coefficients are Dirichlet-character divisor
   sums (Eisenstein data, no mock/Appell–Lerch part appears in *this* identity). This is not a classification:
   split quadratic exponents also occur inside Appell–Lerch sums (e.g. n²/2 + nr), which do have mock completions, so
   "split ⇒ shadow-free" is NOT claimed, and other mock reformulations of the ES series are not excluded.
2. (Assessment) The squares of T1 are squares of the *target* n (genus theory of tn + 1 = N(1 + m√−t)), not squares
   of discriminants. The coincidence with ℋ's shadow support is formal: in ℋ the square terms are where class
   numbers of degenerate (split) discriminants should sit; in ES every level is split and the squares are values of
   n. No dictionary maps one to the other (I found none).
3. (Assessment) The obvious source of a mock ES-side object would be a quadratic exponent with u² or v² terms
   (Hickerson–Mortenson f_{a,b,c}, ac ≠ 0); ES's exponent has none, and adding such terms changes the Diophantine
   problem. Not a proof that no mock packaging exists.
