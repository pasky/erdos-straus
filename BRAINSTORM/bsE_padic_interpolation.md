# bsE_padic_interpolation — Skolem–Mahler–Lech / p-adic analytic families in s (O178)

## Idea
s ↦ q^s is ℓ-adically analytic on residue classes of s (ℓ ≠ q), and solution sets of exponential-polynomial equations are
governed by Skolem–Mahler–Lech (periodic plus finite). Choose witnesses a_s, b_s, d_s as exponential polynomials in s with
4a_s b_s d_s = q^s p + 1 and a_s ≡ −b_s (mod q^s), to get infinitely many depths "analytically".
## Literature
Skolem (1934), Mahler (1935), Lech (1953); Everest–van der Poorten–Shparlinski–Ward, *Recurrence Sequences* (AMS 2003).
## Barrier screen
DEPTH_ASPECT Lemma 1.3 (PROVED): degree 1 in q^s forces two of the three witnesses to be constant, and then the congruence holds
only for s ≤ v_q(1 + 4b²d) < ∞. This is B1 (polynomial identities) in exponential clothing. q-adically, q^s → 0, so q-adic
analyticity brings no information about the size of factors.
