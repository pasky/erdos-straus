# z12_window_cap — the self-generated mute set: an unconditional effective pointwise cap (O236)

**Idea.** Take the Type II window q = 4x − n (small prime, q ≡ 4 − n mod 8, (q/n) = −1) and let the mute primes be
the prime factors of x itself. Since 4x ≡ n (mod q), the firing condition "−n/4 ∈ ⟨S⟩² mod q" becomes "−1 ∈ ⟨S⟩",
and (x/q) = −1 (forced by reciprocity) puts a non-residue in S, hence −1 ∈ ⟨S⟩ (cyclic group of order 2·odd).
**Result (PROVED, CAP_POINTWISE Thm 1.1/1.3).** ES^{(y,e)}(n) with e < q; e*(n) ≪ rad(n)^5 (Linnik), explicit for
squarefree n via Ahn–Kwon, ≪ (log n)² under GRH. First unconditional bound on e*(n) in terms of n.

(n+4Q²)/q huge, solution-dependent), T1 respected ((q/a²) = 1: no window at odd squares). The cap is paid in the
exponent budget, which is exactly the ES obstruction ("F3"): e = 0 ⟺ Q | x (Lemma 3.1).
