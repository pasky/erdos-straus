1. **ES₅ as a sandpile-group pairing condition on a 4-vertex fan multigraph** — The chain matrix M = [[v,−1,0],[−1,t,−1],[0,−1,u]] is the reduced Laplacian of the multigraph: path a–b–c plus a sink s joined to a, b, c by v−1, t−2, u−1 parallel edges. It has det M = tuv−u−v = p spanning trees, a cyclic sandpile group ℤ/p, and its discriminant (monodromy) pairing has self-pairing (M⁻¹)₁₁ = A/p. So ES₅(p) (∃ t,u,v ≥ 2, tuv−u−v = p, (A/p) = −1 with A = tu−1; sufficient for ES(p)) ⇔ some fan multigraph of this shape with p spanning trees has a sandpile pairing in the non-square class. — *First step:* Script: for p ≡ 1 (4) < 10⁵, enumerate all graphs on ≤ 5 vertices with p spanning trees and record which pairing classes occur; is the non-square class reached by 4-vertex fans exactly when ES₅(p) holds, and always by 5-vertex graphs? [high/cheap; form:spanning-tree, field:graph-theory, field:sandpile, new-form, computation, seen:HJ_DEEP.md]
