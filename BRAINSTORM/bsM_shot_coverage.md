# bsM_shot_coverage — sterile points as the signature of finitely many shots (O188)

**Comparison (STERILE_STEER Prop 5.1).** On the q-generic line the level-1 ET boxes cover ≪ q^{−2/5+o(1)} of ℤ_q^×
(truth ≈ log³q/q). In O183's V_φ over ℤ[φ] the unit shots x ≡ −4φ^{2k+1} (mod 𝔮) cover 1/i_𝔮 of the non-squares
(i_𝔮 = index of ⟨φ²⟩ in the squares), which is 1 for a positive proportion of 𝔮 under GRH.
**Reading.** O178-type sterile points come from per-prime coverage being summable on sparse sets (one direction,
|T| = 1; R188 repair of "exist exactly because").
A ℤ-mechanism "with infinitely many shots" would need families of q-level-1 classes covering ≫ 1 of the non-residues
at a positive proportion of q. ET classes cannot do this, since the shot modulus 4ad grows because a²d is not a unit;
At a given q only the τ₃((q+1)/4) shots (a, d) with 4ad | q + 1 are usable. Mordell's p + 4 is the unique shot
usable at every q ≡ 3 (mod 4) (R188 repair: previously "the unique bounded-modulus shot"; every fixed shot has
bounded modulus 4ad). This makes O183 spark S4 precise.
