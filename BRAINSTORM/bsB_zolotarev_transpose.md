# bsB_zolotarev_transpose — the ES sign as the sign of a permutation (O175, spark brainstorm B)

**ES is not solved here.** Labels as in DISCOVERIES.md.

## Idea
Zolotarev: for odd N and (a, N) = 1 the Jacobi symbol (a/N) is the sign of x ↦ ax on ℤ/N (Frobenius–Zolotarev).
Is the ES sign of GROWING_IDENTITIES (GI) Prop 4.1 the sign of an *explicit* permutation, so that W(n) > 0 becomes
"some permutation in an explicit family is odd" — and could a permutation-theoretic (cycle/involution) argument
force oddness at non-squares?

## Dictionary (PROVED, elementary)
Type I slice a = 1: `cfg − c − f = n` ⟺ `gn + 1 = m·m''`, m = fg − 1, m'' = cg − 1 (multiply by g), 4 | m,
m ≡ m'' ≡ −1 (mod g). Put M := m·m'' − 1 = gn.
1. **Certificate = an m × m'' rectangle whose transpose is multiplication by m mod gn.** Row-major index
   k = i·m'' + j ↦ j·m + i is k ↦ k·m mod (mm'' − 1). (Standard in-place-transposition fact.)
2. Its sign is `(−1)^{C(m,2)C(m'',2)}` (counting inversions of a transpose) and also `(m/gn)` (Zolotarev), so
   `(m/gn) = +1` because 4 | m. (This is one of the classical permutation proofs of reciprocity.)
3. CRT: ℤ/gn ≅ ℤ/g × ℤ/n (g ⊥ n), and sgn(σ × τ) = sgn(σ)^{n} sgn(τ)^{g} = sgn σ · sgn τ (g, n odd). Hence
   `sgn(×m on ℤ/n) = sgn(×m on ℤ/g) = (m/g) = (−1/g) = −1` (g ≡ 3 mod 4). Since mm'' ≡ 1 (n),
   (m''/n) = (m/n) = −1: GI Lemma 1.3 / Prop 4.1 re-derived as "the transpose is even, its ℤ/g-half is odd
   because of the class condition, so its ℤ/n-half is odd".
4. Parity: (−1)^{W(n)} = (∏_{class −1} m''/n), and more usefully O(n) := Σ_{D*(n)} (m''/n) is a sum of signs of
   the ℤ/n-components of all sign-blind rectangles (GI §4).
5. T1: at n = k² every multiplication map on ℤ/k² is even ((a/k²) = 1), so no rectangle can have an odd
   ℤ/n-half — the permutation form of ET Prop 1.6.

Checked: `scripts/bsB_sign_dict.py 400` — 352 certificates for n ≡ 1 (4) ≤ 400; transposes built explicitly for
the 52 with mm'' ≤ 400; 0 violations (signs, transpose = multiplication, ℤ/n-half sign = class sign).

## Barrier screen
* The sign of the ℤ/n-half is *forced by the class* (item 3): the permutation picture contains exactly the
  sign layer and nothing of the class layer. B2 untouched: one still needs a rectangle with m ≡ −1 (g).
* Construction attempt (iterate on the hypothesis "only individual permutations"): use the **group generated**
  by all sign-blind rectangles' ℤ/n-halves, H(n) = ⟨×m''⟩ ⊂ (ℤ/n)^*. W(n) > 0 needs an odd element *coming from
  class −1*; H(n) containing an odd element is weaker (a product of class +1 elements is never odd: they are all
  even, by item 3 with ε = +1). So the permutation group generated by the class-+1 set is inside the even
  subgroup, and **oddness can only enter through a class −1 element**: no closure/generation argument can
  manufacture it. Collapses to B2/H-A.
* Second iteration (Zolotarev's own proof of reciprocity compares *two* orderings of ℤ/pq): the analogue
  compares row-major and column-major orders of the rectangle; both are determined by (m, m''), so again
  nothing beyond the class.
* T1: respected (item 5), which is why it is consistent, and why it cannot be a route by itself.

## Assessment
Dictionary PROVED (elementary, essentially classical); promise 0/5 as a route. Value: the cleanest
"non-reciprocity" proof of GI Prop 4.1 — the ES sign is the ℤ/n-half of the (even) transpose of the m × m''
rectangle, whose ℤ/g-half is odd exactly when m ≡ −1 (mod g). It makes vivid that **the sign layer is the class
layer projected by a homomorphism that kills everything but ±1** (the item-3 Remark: class +1 elements
generate only even permutations).
