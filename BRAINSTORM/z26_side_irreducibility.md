# z26_side_irreducibility — with p free, no ES side splits (O254; JOINT_BUDGET §1)

**Idea.** Make the thin-family trick (algebraic factorisation of a side number) work for every p by choosing the slice
parameters as polynomials θ(u) in a free auxiliary vector u, hoping N_θ(p) factors in ℚ[u, p].
**Result (PROVED, Theorem A).** Every RT2 side (linear in p, or p² + 4c²d) factors as g(u)·N′(u, p) with g p-free and N′
irreducible (Gauss; for p² + 4c²d irreducibility uses d > 0). Cor A.1: algebraically forced certificates at free p are
u-indexed ET congruence classes; firing = moving-modulus event. Remark 1.2: with d = −w² (signed) the quadratic side
splits identically — positivity is the obstruction (cf. (J)52).
**Barrier screen.** B2 (the residue moves with u), B1 (fixed u = ET class). T1 untouched (nothing fires).
**Numerics.** z26_checks (a): 60 random θ × 7 sides, 0 violations.
**Label.** PROVED (negative). Steers every JF attempt to a relation p ~ u (z26_polynomial_relations) or to non-polynomial data.
