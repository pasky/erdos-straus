
## Z1973 — Denumerant reciprocity partner is an exact multiple

**What.** Ehrhart reciprocity for the one-dimensional denumerant gives extended count N(−p−u−B)=−N(p). Substituting p=4CuB−u−B makes the reciprocity partner −p−u−B=−4CuB, an exact packaging rather than an extra ES criterion. **Check:** (u,B,C)=(2,3,1) gives p=19 and N(19)=3=4C−1. **Reproduce:** enumerate x=0,…,⌊19/2⌋ and retain 2x+3y=19; for general values apply the coprime two-generator denumerant reciprocity formula.
