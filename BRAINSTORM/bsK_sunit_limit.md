# bsK_sunit_limit — B1 as the S → all-primes limit of an S-unit obstruction (O185)
For S finite and F_S(k) = {k·u + 1 : u ∈ ⟨S⟩₊}, the exponent space Ẑ^{|S|} is coupled across target primes; integer obstruction points
are the solutions of the S-unit equation k·u + 1 = v² (finitely many). As S → all primes the parameter space becomes Ẑ = Π Z_q
(product), every k gets obstruction points, and one recovers B1/Mordell (EXPONENT_COVERING §5.1, Prop 2.1).
**Question (open).** Is there an intermediate regime — S growing with the size of n, e.g. S = primes ≤ (log n)^A — where the coupling
survives but the family is dense enough to contain every prime? (Answer is almost surely no: a prime p is in F_S(k) only if p − 1 is
S-smooth up to k; Proth/Pocklington-type sets. ES for all p with p − 1 being S-smooth is still a sparse statement.)
Screen: B1 not applicable for finite S; for growing S the closure tends to Π_q and B1 returns. **Promise: 1/5.**
