# z33_kummer_shadow — residue symbols, Rédei symbols, governing fields see only the quadratic shadow (O263; §2, §5)

**Idea.** Higher reciprocity (cubic/quartic/Eisenstein), Rédei symbols, Smith/Koymans–Pagano governing fields give
non-congruence data about p. Can they force the class (mod t) of N or of an individual prime factor ℓ | N?
**Literature.** Chebotarev in composita; Kummer theory; Schinzel, "Abelian binomials…" (Acta Arith. 32, 1977); Rédei fields
(D₄); Smith 2017+, Koymans–Pagano, Chan–Koymans–Milovic–Pagano (governing fields are 2-extensions over multiquadratic fields).
**Result (PROVED, Thm 2.1(b)(c), Prop 5.1).** Frobenius data of p in a Galois Ω is independent of p mod t beyond
F = Ω ∩ ℚ(ζ_t) ⊂ Ω^{ab}. For ℓ-th power symbols (ℓ odd) Ω^{ab} = ℚ(ζ_ℓ); quartic and Rédei data have biquadratic Ω^{ab}.
Individually, for ℓ | p + 4d: χ_{−4d}(ℓ) = (ℓ/p) is the ONLY link of ℓ's class mod 4d to p; higher symbols (p/ℓ)_n =
(−4d/ℓ)_n are Frob_ℓ in ℚ(ζ_n, (−4d)^{1/n}), whose abelian shadow is ℚ(ζ_n) (n odd prime) or ℚ(i, √d) (n = 4). Multiplied
over ℓ | N, higher reciprocity says only N ≡ 4d (mod p). Theorem 5.2: no class mod 4d is forbidden for a large factor in any
Chebotarev class of p. **Reciprocity forces parity, never class.**
**Exception noted.** −4b⁴ = ((1 + i)b)⁴: the quartic Kummer datum of −4d is abelian iff d is a square, reducing to (b/ℓ);
this is the Sophie-Germain identity of the thin families (J)74/(J)80 — algebraic factorisation of the INTEGER, not a symbol.
**Barrier screen.** Prop 10.3 (WINDMILL): the same conclusion for form/cube classes; here for Galois data of p. B2 untouched.
**Assessment.** Closed in scope (Frobenius data in fixed finite Galois extensions; the abelian-part claim must be checked
for any specific Ω not of Kummer/Rédei type).
