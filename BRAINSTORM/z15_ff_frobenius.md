# z15_ff_frobenius — function-field analogue of exponential-window menus (O240)

## Idea
Thin families in F_q[T] with eventually periodic residues: P_s = c·T^{q^s} + A. Root parameters exist (u = T^{q^{s−j}}), but
x^q + y^q = (x + y)^q kills the cyclotomic/Aurifeuillean supply of moving divisors that powered Theorem C; the natural
replacement would be Carlitz-cyclotomic factors of T^{q^s} − T (products of all monic irreducibles of degree | s).
For the target of CONJECTURE 0.2 (all primes P near x*_c) Prop 5.1 holds verbatim: thin menus do not help.
