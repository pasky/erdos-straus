## §1 Verified entries

Access date for all listed source records: 2026-10-30. The decisive abstract text is preserved in `sources/z52/unitfrac_cover/arxiv-abstracts.txt`; arXiv API records were directly accessed. These abstract-level checks verify the quoted theorem statements; details not in abstracts are not asserted here.

1. **Thomas F. Bloom**, “On a density conjecture about unit fractions,” arXiv:2112.03726v2 (updated 2023-10-12; published JEMS 2025 per repo). Abstract theorem: every positive-upper-density `A⊂N` contains a finite `S⊂A` with `Σ_{n∈S}1/n=1`. Status: published. Relation: `BRAINSTORM/addcomb_unit_fraction_density.md`, `BRAINSTORM/z31_unit_fractions.md`; density-of-input, not fixed three-term pointwise.
2. **Yang P. Liu, Mehtaab Sawhney**, “On further questions regarding unit fractions,” arXiv:2404.07113v1 (2024-04-10). Abstract theorem: if `A⊂[1,N]` has reciprocal sum at least `(log N)^{4/5+o(1)}`, it contains `B` with reciprocal sum 1; techniques refine Croot/Bloom. Status: preprint (v1 source record accessed; publication status not verified). Relation: same two repo memos above.
3. **David Conlon, Jacob Fox, Xiaoyu He, Dhruv Mubayi, Huy Tuan Pham, Andrew Suk, Jacques Verstraëte**, “A question of Erdős and Graham on Egyptian fractions,” arXiv:2404.16016v2 (updated 2025-12-17), *Discrete Analysis* 2025:28. Abstract theorem: for each fixed positive rational `x`, the number of distinct-denominator representations by denominators ≤`n` is `2^{(c_x+o(1))n}`, with explicit `c_x` increasing with `x`. Status: published. Relation: `BRAINSTORM/addcomb_unit_fraction_density.md`; not an every-prime three-term assertion.
4. **Thomas F. Bloom**, “Unit fractions with shifted prime denominators,” arXiv:2305.02689v1 (2023-05-04), DOI 10.1017/prm.2024.42, *Proc. R. Soc. Edinburgh A* 155 (2025). Abstract theorem: every positive rational is a finite sum of distinct `1/(p−1)`; analogous `p−h` and positive-relative-density prime-subset versions require a stated necessary congruence condition. Status: published. Relation: `BRAINSTORM/z31_unit_fractions.md`; denominator family supplies unboundedly many terms, not three divisors at one prime.
5. **Will Sawin**, “Sets of unit fractions without two members whose average is a unit fraction,” arXiv:2607.15419v1 (2026-07-16). Abstract theorem: for all sufficiently large `N`, there is `A⊂{1,…,N}` of size `>cN` avoiding pairs whose reciprocal average is a unit fraction; also improves a lower bound for unit-fraction 3-term-AP-free sets. Status: preprint. Relation: `BRAINSTORM/addcomb_unit_fraction_density.md`; this is a dense-set avoidance result, not a representation theorem for each target.
6. **Samuel Korsky**, “Approximating 1 by Sums of Unit Fractions,” arXiv:2607.04157v2 (updated 2026-10-08). Abstract theorem: any finite multiset of unit fractions with sum `>K` has a sub-sum within `exp(−c√(K log K))` below 1, for sufficiently large K. Status: preprint. Relation: `BRAINSTORM/addcomb_unit_fraction_density.md`; approximation from a large available total is not exact three-term representation.
7. **Vjekoslav Kovač, Quanyu Tang**, “Eventually greedy best Egyptian underapproximations of rational numbers via optimal control,” arXiv:2607.28387v2 (updated 2026-08-04). Abstract theorem: every positive rational has eventually greedy best Egyptian underapproximations, both allowing repeated denominators and requiring distinct denominators. Status: preprint. Relation: `BRAINSTORM/z31_unit_fractions.md`; greedy approximation concerns an infinite sequence, not a bounded-length exact identity.
8. **Paul Balister, Béla Bollobás, Robert Morris, Julian Sahasrabudhe, Marius Tiba**, “On the Erdős Covering Problem: the density of the uncovered set,” arXiv:1811.03547v1 (2018-11-08), *Invent. Math.* 228 (2022), 377–414. Abstract verifies a sufficient near-best-possible condition for positive uncovered density for large distinct moduli/reciprocal weights, Schinzel’s conjecture, and progress on Erdős–Selfridge. Status: published. Relation: `BRAINSTORM/covering_distortion_dual.md`; the latter already analyzes distortion methods and the ES congruence-class analogue.
9. **Michael Filaseta, Alexandros Kalogirou, Jonah Klein**, “Results for distinct covering systems with a prime minimum modulus `q≥11`,” arXiv:2610.34541v1 (2026-10-25). Abstract: reciprocal-modulus sum is at least `1+10^{-28}`; no covering system can use distinct multiples of `q` as moduli. Status: preprint. Relation: `BRAINSTORM/covering_distortion_dual.md`; a finite-system bound, not an infinite/growing-modulus pointwise theorem.

**Subset-product / zero-sum scope.** The campaign's relevant deterministic multiplicative-set claims are already screened in `BRAINSTORM/addcomb_unit_fraction_density.md` (Kneser consequence: prime factors linear in the relevant index) and `z31_zero_sum.md`; no new sharp theorem with hypotheses matching the divisor-ratio set was source-verified in this lane. No theorem is added from memory.
