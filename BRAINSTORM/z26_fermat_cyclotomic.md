# z26_fermat_cyclotomic — ord_p(g), Lucas ranks, cyclotomic menus (O254; JOINT_BUDGET §4.3)

**Idea (candidates (i), (iii), (iv)).** Auxiliaries that divide p^k − 1 (factors of p ± 1, ord_p(2), Lucas rank of
apparition) give *known* residues of p (a k-th root of unity) modulo the moving modulus — exactly the "(b) residue of p
mod the companion modulus" the brief asks for.
**Results (PROVED).** Prop 4.3: no Type I/II class modulus 4ab divides p − 1 (nor p + 1 for p ≡ 1 (4)) — modulo divisors of
p − 1, p mimics n = 1 and 4/1 has no solution. Remark 4.4: p is a non-square mod every Type II modulus 4ab (Lemma CT), so
ord_{4ab}(p) is even. Bounded menus C_{≤k₀} = {4ab | p^k − 1, k ≤ k₀} are bounded programs: Thm C (H) kills them.
**Numerics (EVIDENCE).** z26_cyclo_depth: 765 Mordell-hard primes ≤ 3·10⁵, 28 153 Type II points: k* = min ord_{4ab}(p) is
always even (non-squareness checked at every point) and reaches 72 (p = 210 961); max 22 at N = 2·10⁴. Bounded cyclotomic menus fail numerically too.
**Label.** PROVED (negative) + EVIDENCE.
