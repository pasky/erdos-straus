# z24_level_dichotomy — cap-0 certificates are least-residue events in the window variable; cap ≥ 1 decouples (O252; FORCED_WINDOW §2)

**Idea.** Locate B2 in window language: what congruence does a certificate impose, on which variable, with which modulus?
**Result (PROVED, elementary; ET Type II in window coordinates).** A cap-0 certificate δ at window m is a coprime pair
(u, v) = (∏_{δ>0} s^δ, ∏_{δ<0} s^{−δ}) with uv | K_m and m | u + v; then m is the least positive residue of −p mod 4uv and
4uv ≥ 2m. Conversely every coprime (u, v) with (−p mod 4uv) | u + v (prime to p) gives one (FORCED_WINDOW Prop 2.1). Read in m
(p fixed): a least-residue event, modulus ≥ 2 × the variable (R-O252 repair: this is exponent 1 + o(1), NOT "level 2"; no claim
that other reorganisations of the count are excluded). Read in p (window fixed): modulus ≤ p — the regime of the almost-all
theorems (B6 in coordinates). Cap e ≥ 1 decouples: the one-prime witness at q | s^j + 1 needs only p ≡ −q (mod 4s),
p ≢ −q (mod s²), modulus 4s² independent of j, while q can exceed 4s² for j ≥ 5 (not for j = 3: q | s² − s + 1) (Prop 2.2).
**Barrier screen.** This is B2/B6 itself, not a way around them; it is a structural reason why provable pointwise/almost-all
results are at cap ≥ 1.
