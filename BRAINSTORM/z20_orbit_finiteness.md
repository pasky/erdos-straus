# z20_orbit_finiteness — cusp families die on the finiteness of 4/n (O248)
## Idea
Cubic fields have units, so each p = N(x) has infinitely many representations; a family valid on a unit-invariant cusp
neighbourhood would cover every prime of a class. POINTWISE_BINARYFORM §4.3 left this open (indefinite quadratics).
## Result (NORMFORM_CUBIC Lemma 2.1, Cor 2.2; PROVED)
4/n has finitely many solutions, so a family takes finitely many values on any orbit; Skolem–Mahler–Lech turns infinitely
many valid points on ⟨ε⟩x₀ into "F_i constant on T_ε·y"; for cubic K, T = R¹𝔾_m has no proper ℚ-subtori, so F_i is constant
on {N = n}. For infinitely many p this forces F_i ∈ ℚ[N] (N non-composite). Works for (x, y, z)-families too.
## Barrier screen
Leaves "finitely many valid points per orbit" (shells); closed for ET families by z20_unit_criterion.
## Assessment
PROVED. Generalisable: any family valid at infinitely many points of one level set of any parametrisation must be constant
on the Zariski closure of those points.
