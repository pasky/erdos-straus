# anal_moments_structured — moments, anti-concentration, structured exceptional sets (O151, angle (b))

Labels as in DISCOVERIES.md. Row II{a,d} (redteam-automorphic-2 §2.1): `p + 4a²d = e·(4acd − 1)` with
`p = 4abd − e`, `c = (a+b)/e` (checked by hand: `4ad(a+b) − e = e(4acd−1)`). So **ES(p) ⇐ some `p + 4a²d` has a
divisor `f ≡ −1 (mod 4ad)`**. ES is not solved here.

## 1. Idea

f(p) = f_I(p) + f_II(p) is a sum over ≍ log³p dyadic blocks of O(1)-events (redteam-2 §3.1). Ask:
(i) can moments over p (variance, higher moments) plus structure give pointwise ES for all p outside an
**explicit, structured** set (thin, described by conditions other than "is an exception")?
(ii) is there anti-concentration at fixed p, i.e. is f(p)/E f(p) concentrated (CLT-like), so that only a
large deviation could make f(p) = 0?

## 2. Literature anchor

* Chebyshev/Turán–Kubilius second-moment method; factorial moments = Bonferroni (campaign (B)7, (B)11).
* Erdős–Kac for ω(n) and its shifted-prime version (Halberstam 1956) — classical, from memory: ω(p+h) is
  asymptotically normal with mean and variance log log p; hence `log τ_k(p+h)` has variance ≍ (log k)² log log p.
* Multidimensional upper-bound sieve (Selberg; Halberstam–Richert, *Sieve Methods*, Thm 2.2 / 5.1 type) for
  "p and p + h_i free of primes in prescribed class sets": dimension `1 + Σ_i κ_i`, upper bound
  `≪ N/(log N)^{1+Σκ_i}` — standard, from memory.
* Campaign: POINTWISE_TYPEI Lemma 4.3 ("subgroup-type sieve criteria have dimension ≥ 1/2 per unforced slice,
  additive"); EXCEPTIONAL_LARGESIEVE Thm 3.1 (3/4 cap for forced-class mixtures); CEILINGS_UNIFIED (level λ ≍ 𝓛⁴).

## 3. Results and barrier screen

**3.1 Second moment (Assessment + arithmetic).** Chebyshev: `#{p ≤ N : f(p) = 0} ≤ Σ_p (f − μ)²/μ²`. The
observed relative variance is `Var/μ² ≈ 0.95/log p` (EVIDENCE §4), not the Poisson value `≍ 1/log³p`. So even an
exact second-moment theorem would give `E(N) ≪ π(N)/log N` — far weaker than (A)/(B)11. Depth-k moments are the
Bonferroni route, capped at saving `≍ (log N)^{3/4}` (CEILINGS_UNIFIED). **No new exceptional-set bound.**

**3.2 Structured complements that are cheap (PROVED, elementary; known in spirit).**
*Prop B.1.* If `4ad | 24` (i.e. (a,d) ∈ {(1,1),(1,2),(2,1),(1,3),(3,1),(1,6),(6,1),(2,3),(3,2)}), then
`p + 4a²d` has no divisor `≡ −1 (mod 4ad)` ⟺ the residues of its prime factors generate a subgroup H of
`(ℤ/4ad)^×` with `−1 ∉ H`. *Proof.* `(ℤ/4ad)^×` has exponent 2, so every element of the subgroup generated by the
residues r₁,…,r_k of the distinct prime factors is a product of a **subset** of them, which is the residue of a
squarefree divisor; conversely every divisor's residue lies in H. ∎
Hence every sterile p has, e.g., `p + 4` composed only of primes ≡ 1 (mod 4), `p + 8` composed of primes in
{1,3} or in {1,5} mod 8, and seven more such conditions. These are explicit **multiplicative** (non-congruence)
thin sets; a 10-dimensional upper-bound sieve gives `E(N) ≪ N/(log N)^{1+κ'}` with κ′ ≥ 9/2 — far weaker than
(A). These slices are EXF §3.2's exact theta slices; their multiplicative description is the content of
POINTWISE_TYPEI Lemma 4.3 (dimension ≥ 1/2 per slice).

*Remark B.2 (a tempting false lemma, recorded to save the next agent time).* If Prop B.1 held for **all**
d ≤ D, sterile p would satisfy D independent half-dimensional conditions, and a sieve with z = N^{1/(CD)},
D = (log N)^{1−ε}, would give `E(N) ≪ N exp(−c(log N)^{1−ε} log log N)` — beating the 3/4 cap. It is **false**
once `(ℤ/4ad)^×` has an element of order > 2: e.g. 4ad = 28, a prime ℓ ≡ 3 (mod 28) with `p + 28 = ℓ` has
divisor residues {1, 3} although ⟨3⟩ ∋ 27 ≡ −1. The correct condition ("no sub-multiset product ≡ −1") is a
union of forced classes ℛ(M) with composite M (divisors built from several primes), i.e. exactly the
forced-class mixtures for which EXCEPTIONAL_LARGESIEVE proves the 3/4 cap. In Haar terms the subgroup
obstruction is only relevant while `φ(4ad) ≲ 2^{ω}` ≈ (log p)^{0.69}, so its total mass is ≪ (log p)^{0.7+o(1)},
dominated by the forced-class mass ≍ log³p. **Collapses to B1/B4.**

**3.3 Anti-concentration at fixed p (EVIDENCE §4 + Assessment).** f(p) is **not** a CLT-type sum of weakly
dependent O(1) events at accessible p: its log has variance ≈ 0.95/log p (not ≍ log^{−3}p), overdispersion
against Poisson is 6–23× and growing. About half of the fluctuation is explained by (i) local data
(p mod 8, (p/q) for q ≤ 13 — saturating, nothing from 13 < q ≤ 200) and (ii) the divisor functions
`τ₃((p+e)/4)` of the first few Type II e-slices, with regression coefficients ≈ c/e (c ≈ 0.34 at p ≈ 3·10⁶,
slowly decreasing, consistent with c ∝ 1/log p). Mechanism: the e-slice `(p+e)/4 = abd, e | a+b` contributes
≈ τ₃((p+e)/4)/e, and `log τ₃` of shifted primes has Erdős–Kac variance ≍ log log p. So at fixed p the
"lottery" is driven by the **joint factorisation of the shifts (p+e)/4, e small** (and analogous Type I shifts),
which is H-A/H-B; and under Hypothesis H every finite pattern of bad factorisations occurs infinitely often
(Thm 2.2(iii)). Anti-concentration therefore gives nothing pointwise. **T7 + B2.**

**Screen summary.** B1: 3.2 is congruence/forced-class data (Remark B.2). B2: 3.3 reduces to joint factorisation
of shifts. B3: second moment gives main term ≍ log³p with relative error (log p)^{−1/2}; Chebyshev-type bounds
cannot reach pointwise. B4: Prop B.1-type multiplicative conditions are finite in number → finite slice set.
B5: Prop B.1 at n = m² (odd): `m² + 4` has every prime factor ≡ 1 (mod 4) (since −4 is a square mod each), so the
"structured" condition **holds** at odd squares — consistent: it is a necessary condition for sterility, and
odd squares are Type I/II-sterile. B6/T7: every moment statement is over p.

## 5. Viability Assessment

**COLLAPSES**: moments → B3/(B)7 ceiling and B6; structured complements → B1/B4 (Remark B.2 explains exactly
why the multiplicative structure does not extend beyond the nine exponent-2 slices); anti-concentration → B2
(EVIDENCE that the fluctuation is carried by factorisations of a few shifts).
By-products: Prop B.1 (PROVED, elementary, essentially known); EVIDENCE/Assessment: **relative variance law
`Var(log f(p)) ≈ 0.95/log p`** for p ≡ 1 (4) on 2·10⁴ – 4·10⁶, with the e-slice τ₃ mechanism; and the
**saturation** of the dependence on quadratic-residue depth beyond q = 13 — so the ES-hardness of a prime is not
governed by its least non-residue (the minimal witness is, per POINTWISE_OMEGA/TYPEI; the total count is not).
CONJECTURE (cheap, not pursued): `Var_{p≤N}(log f(p)) ~ C/log N`.
