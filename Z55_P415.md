# Z55_P415 — gap 3 kill side: towards unconditional HC > 4 infinitely often

**ES is OPEN.** Nothing here proves or disproves it. All results are internal (internally reviewed only).
Lane P415 (wave z55). Scripts `scripts/z55_p415_*`, data `data/z55/p415/`.

Notation (as in `Z54_HCKILL.md`): 𝒬 = {primes ≡ 3 (mod 4)}; S = {n ≥ 1 : every prime factor ≡ 1 (mod 4)};
N₁ = p + 4, N₂ = 4p + 1. For p ≡ 1 (mod 8) both are ≡ 5 (mod 8), so each has an even number of 𝒬-prime factors
(with multiplicity). Ω_𝒬(n) = number of 𝒬-prime factors of n with multiplicity.

## (a) Statements attacked (checkpoint 1)

* **P(4,15)** (target; OPEN): infinitely many primes p ≡ 1 (mod 8) with N₁ ∈ S and N₂ ∈ S. By HCKILL Lemma 1.1
  (PROVED) this is exactly K(4) = "HC(p) > 4 infinitely often".
* **U(δ)** (weaker unconditional target): ≫ x/(log x)² primes p ≤ x, p ≡ 1 (8), with N₁ ∈ S and N₂ free of 𝒬-primes
  below x^δ (so Ω_𝒬(N₂) ≤ 1/δ + O(1), even). Aim: PROVED for an explicit δ, and the best explicit δ.
* **E(θ)** (conditional target): EH(θ) (primes in progressions to level x^θ, max over residues) ⟹ P(4,15) with the
  true order x/(log x)². HCKILL Thm E proves this for an *unspecified* θ₀ = 1 − η₀ close to 1. Aim: an explicit θ*.
* **Algebraic route** (narrowing target): can a known two-variable prime-value theorem (Friedlander–Iwaniec a² + b⁴,
  Heath-Brown–Li a² + p⁴, Heath-Brown x³ + 2y³, …) be composed with a polynomial confinement of both N₁ and N₂?

## (b) Why not already closed

HCKILL proved only (i) EH(θ₀), θ₀ unspecified ⟹ P(4,15) and (ii) the BV shadow "no 𝒬-prime below x^ζ in N₁N₂,
ζ < 1/4" (both components sifted only partially). Neither gives one *exact* S condition unconditionally, nor an
explicit level. F6 (quadratic F only) leaves non-quadratic prime-value families open. None of this is in FRONTIER §6.
